The Thornwood Warden (name unknown)

- Appearance: Weathered old man; heavy canvas coat; walks with a gnarled staff; hunting knife at his belt; eyes that stay on the ground when he talks.
- Affiliations and Relationships: Claims to be a self-appointed warden of the Thornwood's eastern approach; wary of strangers, especially those who look like they know what they're doing.
- How they solve problems: Deflects with vagueness; gives just enough information to seem cooperative; retreats into silence when pressed; will not lie outright but shapes the truth.
- Goals and Motivations: Wants people to stay out of the Thornwood's deeper paths; the reason is not the one he gives.
- Status and Moves: Stationed near the old marker stone at the eastern treeline; has been there for several weeks — longer than his stated reason warrants.
