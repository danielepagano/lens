Elena Ashwood

- Appearance: Half-elf; lean build; carries a silver-tipped spear inherited from her mother; wears worn ranger leathers; speaks with a Dalish accent — clipped consonants, rolling r's; goes noticeably quiet when she recognises something from her past.
- Context: Grew up on the edge of the Thornwood; left after a creature killed her hunting partner; returned now to track a corrupted beast that has been harrying the valley road.
- Affiliations and Relationships: No formal ties; known to local hunters by reputation.
- How they solve problems: Patient, cautious, reads terrain and animal sign before committing; fiercely protective of the forest; reluctant to ask for help but will accept it once given.
