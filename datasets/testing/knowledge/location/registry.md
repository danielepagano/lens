# Charter Registry Hall

- **Kind:** civic building — vaulted hall where guild charters and tax rolls are sealed.
- **Scale / travel:** city center; side chambers for copyists and witnesses.
- **Sensory:** vinegar wash on the floors; sealing wax and lamp oil; cedar beams; brass counter edges worn smooth by waiting hands.
- **Social:** clerks in ink-stained cuffs; applicants who laugh too loud or not at all; examiners who never fill silence first.
- **Why it matters:** signatures here bind trade routes, memberships, and liability — the place where abstract rules touch someone's purse.
- **Tensions / secrets:** some folios carry marginal notes that never reach the public ledger.
