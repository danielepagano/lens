# The Thornwood

- **Kind:** wilderness — northern border forest, fog-laden and old-growth.
- **Scale / travel:** days across on foot; standing stones at the heart mark a ritual site overgrown with black moss.
- **Sensory:** canopy so thick noon reads like twilight; wet earth and something faintly metallic on the wind; animal paths braided with old wheel ruts.
- **Social:** few permanent settlers; hunters and rangers know the deer trails; travelers stick to the verge when they can.
- **Why it matters:** corrupted beasts have harried the road for weeks — something at the stones pulls wrong life toward the paths.
- **Tensions / secrets:** locals argue whether the corruption started with the moss or with what sleeps under the stones.
