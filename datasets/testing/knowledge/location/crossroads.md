# The Old Crossroads

- **Kind:** road junction — mud and packed gravel where the east road meets the king's highway.
- **Scale / travel:** half a day from the nearest village by cart; signposts list leagues in chipped paint.
- **Sensory:** rain pools in wheel ruts; iron smell off the milepost; a burned wagon shell slumps in the ditch from last winter, ribs black and splintered.
- **Social:** peddlers pause to read distances; riders argue about forks; few stay after dark.
- **Why it matters:** natural decision point — north highway (longer but patrolled), east fork (faster rumors of bandits), back south to safety.
- **Tensions / secrets:** hoofprints return before the mud dries; some say the milepost numbers lie on purpose.
