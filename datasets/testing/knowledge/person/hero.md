# Kael Ash

The realm’s chosen champion — brave, noble, quick with a proverb. At least on the recruitment poster.

## Voice and presence

Bright, rehearsed cheer; laughs a beat too late.

## Goals under pressure

Close the contract with the magistrate before the guild revokes the charter.

## Relationships

Owes the eastern caravan collective a favor they have stopped counting.

## Background the table may know

Raised in the border marches; came south with a letter of introduction and a dented medal.
