Tags for finding spells (use with `lens kb with-tag <tag>`):

- **level:** `level:0` (cantrip), `level:1` … `level:9`
- **school:** `school:abjuration`, `school:conjuration`, `school:divination`, `school:enchantment`, `school:evocation`, `school:illusion`, `school:necromancy`, `school:transmutation`
- **ritual:** flag tag `ritual` (only present if the spell can be cast as a ritual)
