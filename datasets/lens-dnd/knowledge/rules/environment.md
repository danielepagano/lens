D&D ENVIRONMENT, HAZARDS, AND TRAVEL

Rules for environemntal hazards that cause damage and conditions and for long-distance travel. Include if you are on a long journey or in unfriendly environments.

HAZARDS

- Burning: 1d4 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. As an action, a creature can put itself out by dropping Prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if doused, submerged, or smothered.
- Suffocation: a creature can hold its breath for 1 + its Constitution modifier minutes (minimum 30 seconds). When it runs out of breath or is choking, it gains 1 Exhaustion level at the end of each of its turns. All Exhaustion gained this way is removed when it can breathe again.
- Dehydration: a Medium creature needs 1 gallon of water per day (Tiny 1/4, Large 4, Huge 16, Gargantuan 64). Drinking less than half that gains 1 Exhaustion level at the day's end, and that Exhaustion cannot be removed until it drinks a full day's water.
- Malnutrition: a Medium creature needs 1 pound of food per day (Tiny 1/4, Large 4, Huge 16, Gargantuan 64). Eating less than half that requires a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or 1 Exhaustion level at the day's end. Eating nothing for 5 days gains 1 Exhaustion level at the end of the fifth day and another at the end of each day after. That Exhaustion cannot be removed until it eats a full day's food.
- Extreme Cold (0°F or below): DC 10 Con save per hour or 1 Exhaustion level. Immune: cold resistance/immunity, appropriate gear.
- Extreme Heat: DC 5 Con save per hour (DC +1 per hour) or 1 Exhaustion level. Immune: fire resistance/immunity, appropriate gear.
- High Altitude (above 10,000 ft, unacclimatized): Exhaustion after 1 hour; Disadvantage on Str/Con checks and saves.
- Deep Water (below 100 ft, no Swim Speed): DC 10 Con save per hour or 1 Exhaustion level.

ENVIRONMENTAL EFFECTS

- Slippery Ice: Difficult Terrain. Walking requires DC 10 Acrobatics or fall Prone.
- Strong Wind: Disadvantage on ranged attack rolls. Concentration checks for spells with Verbal components may be warranted.
- Thin Ice: Crack threshold 3d10 × 10 lbs. Heavier creatures break through into icy water.
- Dead Magic Zone: As Antimagic Field spell — all magic ceases within (typically ≤300 ft diameter).
- Wild Magic Zone: Spellcasting triggers a Wild Magic surge.


TRAVEL

A group travelling outside combat chooses a Fast, Normal, or Slow pace.

- Fast — 400 feet per minute, 4 miles per hour, 30 miles per day. Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception or Survival) and Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
- Normal — 300 feet per minute, 3 miles per hour, 24 miles per day. Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
- Slow — 200 feet per minute, 2 miles per hour, 18 miles per day. Advantage on Wisdom (Perception or Survival) checks.

Mounts can cover twice the pace distance for 1 hour, after which they need a Short or Long Rest before doing it again. Waterborne travellers move at the vessel's speed and do not choose a pace.

Break a longer trip into 2–3 stages, each carrying one challenge type: an encounter, foraging, navigation, a hazard, or something worth finding. Travel that does not carry one of those can be summarised in a sentence.
