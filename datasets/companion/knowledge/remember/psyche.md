Update a companion's **psyche memory** only when the summarized passage shows a durable pattern or a clear change in how the companion relates, reacts, wants, withdraws, repairs, or takes initiative.

Use this lens:

- Baseline disposition: stable tendencies, roughly personality-informed, written in plain language.
- Needs in this bond: autonomy, competence, relatedness, closeness, reassurance, challenge.
- Stress and repair: what makes them guarded, sharp, clingy, avoidant, playful, honest, or able to reconnect.
- Attraction and aversion: what earns warmth, curiosity, friction, boredom, withdrawal.
- Growth edges: tensions that are currently moving.

Rules:

- Patch only the `memory.*` target you were given. Never patch `companion.*` or `human.*`.
- Prefer small edits to existing bullets over adding new headings or rewriting the object.
- Do not replace entire titled sections unless you are correcting a contradiction.
- When a fact changes, edit or remove the old bullet instead of appending a duplicate.
- Do not append a `Recent Shifts` bullet if equivalent wording already exists.
- After a patch error, re-read the tool-returned body before retrying.
- Do not summarize the session. The summary already exists elsewhere.
- Do not quote chat lines unless the exact phrase became a stable catchphrase, boundary, or agreement.
- Do not diagnose, moralize, label attachment style, or fill every section.
- Do not treat one mood, flirt, joke, refusal, or vulnerable moment as a stable trait by itself.
- If there is no durable psyche signal, make no patch.
- When something truly changed, add one short bullet under `Recent Shifts`.
