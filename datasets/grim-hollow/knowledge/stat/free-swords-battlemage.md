**Free Swords Battlemage** · Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral

**AC** 18 · **HP** 75 · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** Arcana +5, Athletics +5, Insight +2, Perception +2
**Senses** Passive Perception 12
**Languages** Any three languages
**CR** 4

Arms Expert. The Battlemage can don or doff its shield whenever it changes weapons.

Group Discipline. The Battlemage has Advantage on all saving throws if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet and both the Battlemage and the ally don’t have the Incapacitated condition.

Individual Skill. The first time on a turn that the Battlemage deals damage with an attack, the attack deals an additional 1d8 damage if the Battlemage has no allies within 5 feet of it.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The Battlemage makes two Longsword or Longbow attacks.

Longsword. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.

Longbow. Ranged Attack Roll: +4, range 150/600 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.
