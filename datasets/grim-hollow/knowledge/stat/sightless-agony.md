**Sightless Agony** · Medium Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral

**AC** 16 · **HP** 187 · **Speed** 30 ft., fly 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 20 (+5) | 15 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) |

**Senses** Blindsight 60 ft.,  Passive Perception 10
**Languages** All languages known before the curse
**CR** 10

Shared Agony. When a sightless agony takes damage, it emits a wave of energy, causing each creature within 5 feet of it to take an amount of Psychic damage equal to half the triggering damage (rounding down).

Stunted Flight. When a sightless agony chooses to fly, it must land by the end of its turn or have the Prone condition.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The sightless agony makes one Bite attack, two Claw attacks, and one Tail attack.

Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) Piercing damage plus 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage.

Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft., Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 14 (2d8 + 5) Psychic damage. If both Claw attacks hit the same creature, the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17). Checks made to escape the grapple have Disadvantage.

Tail. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 10 ft., Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 18 (3d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target is subjected to the following effect. Strength Saving Throw: DC 17. Failure: The target has the Prone condition.

---
**Reactions**

Wave of Agony (1/Day). Trigger: The sightless agony becomes Bloodied. Response: Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17, each creature within a 20-foot Emanation. Failure: 45 (10d8) Psychic damage. Success: Half damage.

---
**Description**

Transforming into a Sightless Agony is the result of being afflicted with a Curse of Crushing Sensation. Victims find themselves fleeing the company of others in search of peaceful solitude, as even the gentle sounds of nature become jarring and obtrusive to these poor souls. Such unfortunates have often left those behind who care for them, or perhaps even those who have the need for some rare skill the individual possesses.

A Sightless Agony seeks a dark, quiet place to hide itself. They often tear out their own eyes and claw away their skin to escape the agony of sensation, and the company of others becomes entirely unbearable. In such a state, they know only a boundless, white-hot rage.

When discovered, a Sightless Agony appears as a mutated gargoyle with skin flayed from its malformed figure. They attack with razor-sharp claws and a mouthful of fangs, and though a Sightless Agony possesses wings, they are loath to fly far, since the feeling of the wind is nearly unbearable. Even standing in one place, the Sightless Agony quivers and twitches as sensations from their immediate environment cause them to flinch at the unseen.
