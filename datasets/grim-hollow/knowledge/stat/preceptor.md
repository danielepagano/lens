|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|

**Skills** Animal Handling +5, Arcana +9, History +9, Investigation +9, Medicine +5, Nature +9
**Senses** Passive Perception 11
**Languages** Any five languages
**CR** 9

Academic Specialty. The preceptor has Advantage on all ability checks using their chosen specialty and gains Resistance to a type of damage.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The preceptor makes three Scalpel attacks. It can replace one Scalpel attack with an Acid Flask, Incendiary Bomb, or Neurotoxic Cloud.

Scalpel. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft., Hit: 7 (1d4 + 5) Slashing damage and 7 (2d6) Poison damage.

Acid Flask (Decosopher). Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) Acid damage and 7 (2d6) Acid damage at the end of the target’s next turn.

Incendiary Bomb (Blastwright). Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., Hit: 18 (4d8) Fire damage and the target has the Prone condition.

Neurotoxic Cloud (Biothurge). Targets in a 20-ft. Cone take 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage and have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. If the target succeeds on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, it takes half damage and is not Poisoned.

Spellcasting. The preceptor casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks):

At Will: Light, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation

1/Day Each: Acid Arrow, Animate Dead, Banishment, Detect Magic, Dispel Magic, Enhance Ability, Fireball, Levitate, Magic Circle, Misty Step, Summon Dragon, Unseen Servant
