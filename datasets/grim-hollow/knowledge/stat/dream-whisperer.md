**Description**

Those learned individuals with aspirations of greatness, but who have fallen to fate’s twisted ironies and been afflicted by a Curse of Lost Sentiment, may emerge from such trials as a Dream Whisperer. Now a deformed, hunched creature covered in contorted arms, the Dream Whisperer uses its many hands to grasp and shred the hopes, dreams, thoughts, and memories of its victims.

Though animalistic in behavior, Dream Whisperers often reside within larger settlements, cities, and the like, where they can spread their influence and remain undetected for long periods of time. Preferring to attack at night, Dream Whisperers often sneak into unsuspecting targets’ homes through windows and otherwise inaccessible openings, since their many arms allow them to scale otherwise impossible surfaces. Once inside, their arms serve a second function— to physically tear treasured memories from their victims and replace them with feelings of paranoia and doubt.

Fighting a Dream Whisperer is a dangerous task. Brave heroes have been driven mad by the Dream Whisperer’s aberrant attacks. Comrades-in-arms have been compelled to turn on each other, and the weakest minds snap, leaving a drooling mess in the creature’s wake. With each stolen memory, the Dream Whisperer seeks to slake its unquenchable thirst for its lost cognition.

Lost Memories

| 1d6 | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The target becomes deeply suspicious of a character or NPC of the GM’s choice. They gain the following flaw: “I know they are seeking to betray me. I have to act first—it’s me or them!” |
| 2 | The target forgets all memories of a loved one of the GM’s choice. The next time the target sees them, it’s as if they are meeting them for the first time. |
| 3 | The target becomes stricken with doubt and gains the following flaw: “The world is out to get me, and I can’t win. I may as well give up now.” |
| 4 | The target forgets how to speak in one language known, decided by the GM. |
| 5 | The target forgets the location of something important to them, of the GM’s choice. It may be the location of a goal, their home settlement, or a hidden treasure. |
| 6 | The target loses an inconsequential memory such as what they ate a few nights ago or the name of an unimportant NPC. |

A Remove Curse spell reverses this effect and restores the victim's memory.
