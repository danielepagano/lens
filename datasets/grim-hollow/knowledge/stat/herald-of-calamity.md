**Languages** All languages known before the curse

Aura of Calamity. An aura of misfortune surrounds the herald in a 30-foot Emanation. Creatures other than the herald within the area have Disadvantage on D20 Tests. Whenever a creature in the area other than the herald takes damage from any source other than another creature (such as a trap, a hazard, or a fall), it takes the maximum possible damage.

Incorporeal Movement. The herald of calamity can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Invisibility. The herald of calamity has the Invisible condition unless it is in an area illuminated by moonlight.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The herald of calamity makes two Life Drain attacks.

Life Drain. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 21 (4d8 + 3) Necrotic damage, and the herald of calamity regains Hit Points equal to half the amount of Necrotic damage dealt.

---
**Reactions**

Mishap. Trigger: An attack roll against the herald of calamity hits it. Response: The herald gains a +6 bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If this causes the attack to miss, it is redirected to a creature of the Herald’s choice within 5 feet of it. Use the original attack roll to determine the success of the redirected attack.

---
**Description**

The tragic individuals who become Heralds of Calamity were afflicted by the Fates themselves, or so the story goes. As recipients of a Curse of Ill-Fated Fortune, Heralds of Calamity bring tragedy upon all who linger in their presence.

Legends say these foul beings hunt unseen, lurking near vital locations such as a village well, bridge, or other communal site where they can inflict many casualties before survivors think to abandon the place.

When seen, Heralds of Calamity appear as incorporeal skeletal figures, though they are invisible except under the light of the moon. Unlike other curse monsters, Heralds of Calamity are not physically formidable, nor do they wield tremendous arcane power.

Many monster hunters, however, have fallen believing that a Herald of Calamity would be an easy challenge. Expert archers have misfired, fatally wounding companions. Seasoned adventurers have slipped on trivial obstacles and received debilitating injuries.

Regardless of the circumstance, all who have engaged a Herald of Calamity have the same advice: Rely on the maxim that if something can possibly go wrong, it will.
