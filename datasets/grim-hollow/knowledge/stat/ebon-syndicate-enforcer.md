**Ebon Syndicate Enforcer** · Medium Humanoid, Neutral

**AC** 15 · **HP** 52 · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) |

**Skills** Athletics +4, Stealth +5
**Senses** Passive Perception 11
**Languages** Thieves' Cant plus three other languages
**CR** 2

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The enforcer makes two attacks using their Dagger and Fighting Chain in any combination.

Dagger. Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.

Fighting Chain. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition(escape DC 12). The enforcer can only have one creature grappled by its fighting chain.

---
**Description**

Street-level agents deliver overt official messages. Thugs and brutes with some manners and basic business sense can beat both into a target when the need arises. In addition to common weapons, Syndicate enforcers favor fighting chains or “chain-knuckles” that give them the choice to strangle or bind their opponents. These have the benefit of being less conspicuous than a blade.
