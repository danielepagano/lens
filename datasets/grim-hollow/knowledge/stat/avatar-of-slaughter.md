**Languages** All languages known before the curse
**CR** 14

Blood Frenzy. The avatar of slaughter has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that doesn’t have all its Hit Points.

Imprison in Weapon. When the avatar of slaughter is reduced to 0 Hit Points by a melee weapon attack by a creature within 5 feet, its spirit infests the weapon that struck the blow. The weapon gains the properties and Curse associated with a Berserker Axe, though it retains its original weapon type. Additionally, while the curse remains the weapon is sentient and can telepathically communicate with any creature that wields it, encouraging violence and malice.

Magic Resistance. The avatar of slaughter has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Rejuvenation. If the avatar of slaughter is destroyed, and not imprisoned in a weapon, it reforms and regains all its Hit Points in 1d6 days unless a Wish spell is cast on its remains.

Silver Weakness. Damage dealt by Silver weapons ignores the avatar's Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

Standing Leap. The avatar of slaughter’s Long Jump distance is up to 60 feet and its High Jump distance is up to 30 feet, with or without a running start.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The avatar of slaughter makes four Fist attacks. It can replace one attack with a Gore attack.

Fist. Melee Weapon Attack: +13, reach 5 ft. Hit: 17 (2d8 + 8) Bludgeoning damage.

Gore. Melee Weapon Attack: +13, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d8 + 8) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the avatar of slaughter moved at least 20 feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) Piercing damage and has the Stunned condition until the end of the target’s next turn.

---
**Description**

In any war, the most vicious combatants revel in carnage, destroying homes and lives without a second thought. Within the confines of such conflicts, a Curse of Uncontrollable Wrath can be the most powerful instrument of vengeance.

Brave knights and bloodthirsty tyrants alike are reduced to a state of primal rage upon becoming an Avatar of Slaughter. Under the right circumstances, even a pacifist can fall prey to this curse if the perpetrator responsible is truly vile.

An Avatar of Slaughter appears as a daemonic minotaur with smoke fuming from its nostrils, existing only to kill any one unfortunate enough to cross its path. They often seek out friends and family first, as they unconsciously attempt to demolish their former lives.

Engaging an Avatar of Slaughter in combat is a task best left to the extremely brave or equally reckless. Born in the fires of conflict, they cannot be completely killed by it, though their affinity for violence means a defeated Avatar of Slaughter’s essence seeks refuge within a weapon.
