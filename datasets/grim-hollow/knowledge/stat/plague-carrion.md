Cloud of Blight. A miasma surrounds the plague carrion in a 20-foot Emanation. Each time another creature starts its turn or enters the area, it gains a -1 penalty to damage rolls. Multiple instances of this penalty stack, but a creature can't gain it more than once each round. The penalty is removed when the creature finishes a Quick, Short, or Long Rest.

Death Burst. When the plague carrion dies, it explodes in a cloud of sickening gas. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation centered on the plague carrion. Failure: 27 (6d8) Poison damage, and the creature’s Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to half the Poison damage taken (rounding down). Success: Half damage only.

The Hit Point maximum reduction lasts until the creature finishes a Short or Long Rest.

**Actions**

Multiattack. The plague carrion makes one Bite attack and one Plague Touch attack, or two Plague Beam attacks.

Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 20 (4d8 + 2) Piercing damage.

Plague Touch. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 49 (10d8 + 4) Poison damage. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17. Failure: The target’s Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to half the Poison damage taken (rounding down).

Plague Beam. Ranged Attack Roll: +8, range 60 ft. Hit: 16 (3d8 + 3) Poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the plague carrion’s next turn.

Spellcasting. The plague carrion casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material component and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks):

1/Day Each: Contagion, Insect Plague

**Description**

A Plague Carrion appears as a vile, decaying creature spewing pestilence across the landscape, destroying vegetation and poisoning living creatures unlucky enough to cross its path. A Plague Carrion is a sad creature that loathes its own existence and eschews combat, though such a creature defends itself when forced or cornered.

Despite being the least aggressive of all the curse monsters, individual Plague Carrions are believed to represent the greatest threat to the people of Etharis. Some even say Weeping Pox is a product of a Plague Carrion.

A Curse of Foul Blight is most frequently used by hags and dark sorcerers to spite beautiful rivals out of jealousy. Those who fall victim suffer a miserable fate. If not cured quickly, the victim becomes a Plague Carrion, a deformed creature covered in gaping cysts that expel clouds of profane corruption. Venomous insects belch forth from these calloused apertures.

A Plague Carrion is compelled by its curse to constantly wander, tormented by its own corporeal decay if it lingers in the same place for too long. This cruel punishment ensures its foul blight spreads far and wide throughout the land.
