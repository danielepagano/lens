**Weeping Willow** · Large Plant, Neutral Evil

**AC** 18 · **HP** 228 · **Speed** 10 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 17 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 19 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 21 (+5) |

**Skills** Deception +15, Persuasion +15
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft.,  Passive Perception 12
**Languages** Telepathy 120 ft.
**CR** 14

Alluring Cry. The cry of the weeping willow attracts creatures in a 120-foot Emanation from the willow. At the start of each of its turns, a creature within the area must succeed on a DC 18 Charisma save or have the Charmed condition for 1 hour. On a success, the target is immune to this weeping willow's Alluring Cry for 24 hours.

While Charmed, the target also has the Incapacitated condition. On each of its turns, a Charmed creature must use its movement to approach the weeping willow by the most direct route, stopping when it is within 5 feet of the weeping willow. It doesn't avoid Opportunity Attacks, but if this movement would obviously cause damage (such as by walking through fire), the creature repeats the save before entering that space.

Deep Rooted. The weeping willow is immune to effects that would move it.

Magic Resistance. The weeping willow has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The weeping willow makes two Slam attacks.

Slam. Melee Attack Roll: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 25 (4d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.

Lignify. Constitution Saving Throw, one creature within 5 feet: DC 17. First Failure: The target has the Restrained condition and repeats the save at the end of its next turn if it is still Restrained, ending the effect on itself on a success. Second Failure: The target has the Petrified condition instead of the Restrained condition, becoming a tree permanently. Greater Restoration or similar magic returns an affected creature to their original form.

Spellcasting. The weeping willow casts one of the following spells without the need for components, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):

At Will: Detect Thoughts, Major Image

2/Day: Dominate Monster

**Description**

The wise have been known to provide this advice: if you hear crying in a woodland or forest that has become strangely quiet, run as quickly as you can—though it may already be too late. The cry of a Weeping Willow is difficult to ignore and has often led the unwary to an untimely demise.

A Weeping Willow is the unfortunate victim of a Curse of Conceited Obsession. Once a vain or narcissistic individual, they become a twisted half-tree, half-humanoid creature warped by self-love and malicious intent.

A Weeping Willow is obsessed with its own appearance, calling for brave heroes to aid it in its distress. Upon reaching the Weeping Willow, however, heroes find only a vicious predator, a grove of unusual shimmering trees, and a very likely fate worse than death.

Combating a Weeping Willow unprepared often ends in disaster. The creature’s enthralling cries and illusory beauty are difficult to resist, drawing its prey closer. The touch of a Weeping Willow can transform any creature's into a tree, bearing mirrored leaves for the vain creature to gaze into. Survivors of these encounters say that if you can resist the creature’s malignant lure, it’s best to leave them alone with their gardens of reflecting trees and self-admiration.
