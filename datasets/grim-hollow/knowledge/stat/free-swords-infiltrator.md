**Free Swords Infiltrator** · Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral

**AC** 18 · **HP** 75 · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +5, Insight +2, Perception +2, Stealth +7
**Senses** Passive Perception 12
**Languages** Any three languages
**CR** 4

Arms Expert. The Infiltrator can don or doff its shield whenever it changes weapons.

Group Discipline. The Infiltrator has Advantage on all saving throws if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet and both the Infiltrator and the ally don’t have the Incapacitated condition.

Individual Skill. The first time on a turn that the Infiltrator deals damage with an attack, the attack deals an additional 1d8 damage if the Infiltrator has no allies within 5 feet of it.

Poisonous Blade. The first time the infiltrator deals damage with a Melee Weapon on its turn, it deals an additional 10 (3d6) poison damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the infiltrator’s next turn.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The Infiltrator makes two Longsword or Longbow attacks.

Longsword. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.

Longbow. Ranged Attack Roll: +4, range 150/600 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.
