Electric Invigoration. Whenever a body snatcher would take Lightning damage, it absorbs the energy and instead gains Temporary Hit Points equal to half the damage, rounded down. The body snatcher also immediately recharges its Electric Torrent ability.

Skin Sack. The body snatcher is physically indistinguishable from its host and cannot be detected by nonmagical means. If the body snatcher is reduced to 0 Hit Points, and it has been inside its current host for less than 24 hours, the host regurgitates it then has the Unconscious condition and 1 Hit Point.

**Actions**

Multiattack. The body snatcher makes two Slam attacks.

Slam. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed at a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). A surprised creature automatically fails this saving throw.The body snatcher can only Grapple one creature at a time.

Invade. Strength Saving Throw: DC 15; one Humanoid creature Grappled by the body snatcher. Failure: The body snatcher departs its current host and moves into the Grappled creature. It then regains all Hit Points and recharges Electric Torrent. If it had been inside its previous host for less than 24 hours, that creature has the Unconscious condition and 1 Hit Point. Otherwise, the previous host body becomes a lifeless skin sack.

Electric Torrent (Recharge 5-6). The body snatcher unleashes a large electric pulse. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 15, each other creature within 20 feet of the body snatcher. Failure: 28 (8d6) Lightning damage. Success: Half damage.

**Description**

Body Snatchers are humanoids who have been transformed by a Curse of Damned Aging. Notoriously difficult to locate, Body Snatchers frequently hide in plain sight, since they essentially resemble their host creature. It’s common that when on the trail of a Body Snatcher, monster hunters and investigators find themselves trailing a step behind the creature. The only evidence that typically remains is the withered and discarded skin sack of their latest victim.

Body Snatchers possess an amorphous form, which they use to inhabit the bodies of their victims, living inside them as a parasite that consumes their being from the inside.

The Body Snatcher takes complete control of its host body, enhancing it with supernatural capabilities. The only sign that an individual has been invaded by a Body Snatcher is their erratic behavior and rapid aging—a host body can appear to age a lifetime in a matter of a few weeks.

While far from helpless, Body Snatchers prefer to avoid detection, engaging enemies only in dire circumstances or when changing hosts. They typically make every effort to complete the latter in seclusion to maintain their gruesome charade.
