Regeneration. The bloated gastromorph regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the Bloated Gastromorph takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn’t function on the bloated gastromorph’s next turn. The bloated gastromorph dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn’t regenerate.

Spider Climb. The bloated gastromorph can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The bloated gastromorph makes three Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with a Tongue attack.

Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 10 ft. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and is moved up to 5 feet toward the bloated gastromorph.

Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. Hit: 27 (4d10 + 5) Piercing damage.

If the bloated gastromorph takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, it regurgitates all swallowed creatures at the end of that turn, each of which falls in a space within 10 feet of the gastromorph and has the Prone condition. If the bloated gastromorph dies, any swallowed creature no longer has the Restrained condition and can escape from the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting with the Prone condition.

Tongue (Recharge 5–6). Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 25 ft. Hit: 31 (4d12 + 5) Acid damage. A creature that is hit by this attack is subjected to the following effect. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17. Failure: The target has the Paralyzed condition and is moved up to 20 feet toward the gastromorph. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Description**

Often whispered of in folk tales and legends, most frequently as a warning to children regarding the gruesome consequences awaiting those who recklessly indulge, the Bloated Gastromorph is an incarnation of gluttony and selfishness. This horrific creature has been transformed and twisted by the final stages of a Curse of Ravenous Hunger.

Those afflicted become an enormous, slug-like monster possessing multiple fist-like claws, a wicked tentacular tongue, and a giant, voracious maw of gnashing teeth. Often dwelling in dank ruins of once-great castles, a Bloated Gastromorph cares about nothing but consuming every morsel of flesh it encounters, living or not. Bereft of memories of their prior lives, these creatures dwell fully in this new existence of unfettered consumption.
