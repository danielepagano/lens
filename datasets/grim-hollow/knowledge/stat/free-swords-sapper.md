**Free Swords Sapper** · Medium Humanoid, Lawful Neutral

**AC** 18 · **HP** 75 · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +5, Insight +2, Perception +2
**Senses** Passive Perception 12
**Languages** Any three languages
**CR** 4

Arms Expert. The Sapper can don or doff its shield whenever it changes weapons.

Group Discipline. The Sapper has Advantage on all saving throws if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet and both the Sapper the ally don’t have the Incapacitated condition.

Individual Skill. The first time on a turn that the Sapper deals damage with an attack, the attack deals an additional 1d8 damage if the Sapper has no allies within 5 feet of it.

---
**Actions**

Multiattack. The Sapper makes two Longsword or Longbow attacks.

Longsword. Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage.

Longbow. Ranged Attack Roll: +4, range 150/600 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.
