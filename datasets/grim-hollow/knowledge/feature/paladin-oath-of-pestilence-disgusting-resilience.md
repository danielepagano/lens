# Disgusting Resilience (Oath of Pestilence)

When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points and not killed outright, you can spend any number of Hit Dice, roll them, and reduce the damage taken by the total rolled on those dice.

In addition, if you are killed, your corpse explodes in a shower of pus and gore. Each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from you makes Constitution saving throw, taking 8d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
