# Dark Doom Revisited (Path of the Wrathful Dead)

Once per active Rage, you can take a Magic action to channel a traumatic death. When you do, choose one of the following options.

***Contamination.*** Each creature of your choice in a 10- foot Emanation originating from you must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or take Poison damage and gain the Poisoned condition for 1 minute.

To determine the Poison damage, roll a number of d6s equal to your Rage Damage bonus, and add them together. An affected creature can use an action to end the Poisoned condition on itself.

***Hypothermia.*** Each creature of your choice in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or take Cold damage and have its Speed halved for 1 minute.

To determine the Cold damage, roll a number of d6s equal to your Rage Damage bonus, and add them together. The affected creature takes the Cold damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can use an action to end this effect on itself.

***Immolation.*** Each creature of your choice in a 15-foot Emanation originating from you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or take Fire damage.

To determine the Fire damage, roll a number of d6s equal to your Rage Damage bonus, and add them together. The affected creature takes the Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. As an action, a creature can extinguish the fire on itself by giving itself the Prone condition and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated.
