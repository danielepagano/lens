**Intaglio** · 1st-level Transmutation

**Casting Time**: 1 Action Ritual
**Range**: 5 ft.
**Components**: V, S, M ((a quill or a vial of ink))
**Duration**: Concentration 1 Hour

You transform a piece of parchment into a duplicate of a text or symbol. During the spell’s duration, you can take a Magic action to trace your hand over any non-magical text or imagery on a surface within range. You imprint the text or imagery onto the parchment you provide, choosing how to resize or crop it. This spell copies a single page with each Magic action. When copying a spellbook, you can do so using the same method as you would replace a spellbook, except the time and GP requirements are both halved.
