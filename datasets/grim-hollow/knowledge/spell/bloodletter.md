**Bloodletter** · 2nd-level Necromancy

**Casting Time**: 1 Bonus Action
**Range**: Touch
**Components**: V, S
**Duration**: Concentration 1 Minute

You touch a nonmagical weapon. Until the spell ends, the first creature hit by the weapon must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or receive a festering wound. A creature with a festering wound takes 2d6 Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns for the spell’s duration. A creature can only have one festering wound at a time. If the creature receives healing, the spell and recurring damage end.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot above 2.
