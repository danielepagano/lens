**Supernal Smite** · 4th-level Evocation

**Casting Time**: 1 Bonus Action *
**Range**: Self
**Components**: V
**Duration**: Concentration 1 Minute

Your strikes cause ambient magic surrounding a creature to collapse and detonate. The target hit by the strike takes an extra 4d6 Force damage from the attack. If the creature is concentrating on a spell, that Concentration is broken.

In addition, each ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC 10 plus that spell’s level). On a successful check, the spell ends.
