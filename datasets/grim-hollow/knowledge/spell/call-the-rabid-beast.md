**Call the Rabid Beast** · 3rd-level Transmutation

**Casting Time**: 1 Action
**Range**: Touch
**Components**: V, S, M ((a fang from a rabid animal))
**Duration**: Concentration 1 Minute

A creature you touch transforms into a rabid monster, filled with primal fury. The target sprouts thick fur and its mouth elongates into a fanged maw. While transformed into a seething monster, the creature gains the following benefits:

- Gains 20 Temporary Hit Points and has an AC of 17 if its AC is lower than that.
- Does an additional +5 damage on Strength-based attacks.
- Can make a Bite attack as a Bonus Action.
- The Bite does 1d6 + the creature’s Strength modifier Slashing damage.
- Is Immune to the Frightened condition.
- Can’t maintain Concentration, and can’t cast spells.
- At the start of each of the creature’s turns, it must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or lose control. If the creature fails this saving throw, it must take an Attack action on each of its turns to attack the closest creature within reach, whether it is an ally or enemy. If none are within reach, the creature must use its movement, and if necessary, a Dash action to move within reach of the nearest creature.
