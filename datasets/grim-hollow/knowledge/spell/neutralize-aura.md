**Neutralize Aura** · 1st-level Abjuration

**Casting Time**: 1 Minute
**Range**: Touch
**Components**: V, S, M ((a sprig of sage))
**Duration**: Concentration 1 Hour

You touch a willing creature and neutralize its aura. Until the spell ends, the target can’t be perceived by Fey, Fiends, Celestials, and Undead.

The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, casts a spell, or moves within 5 feet of one of the creature types.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
