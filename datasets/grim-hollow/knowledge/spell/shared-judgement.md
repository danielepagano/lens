**Shared Judgement** · 4th-level Evocation

**Casting Time**: 1 Bonus Action
**Range**: Self
**Components**: V
**Duration**: Concentration 1 Minute

Once on each of your turns when you reduce an Undead to 0 Hit Points, you can deal 5d6 Radiant damage to a creature you can see within 60 feet of you.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 4.
