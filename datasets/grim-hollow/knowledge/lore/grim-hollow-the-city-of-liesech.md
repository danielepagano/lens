# The City of Liesech

Liesech is a plague-ridden city-state on Bite Bay, north of the Charneault Kingdom, near the Rock-Teeth Mountains. A perpetual green haze hangs over the city. It is the origin of the Weeping Pox — a disfiguring, mentally corrosive plague with supernatural roots.

Visitors notice the haze, the unsettling calm of the populace, and the High Belfry bells that momentarily captivate citizens before they resume their routines. Behind every friendly smile is abject terror.

## Geography

A green haze perpetually covers Liesech's skies, blamed on bay vapors. At night the glow is visible for miles. The city is reached primarily by boat. Surrounding waters harbor turbulent currents and sea stacks near the "Eye of the Sea," a dark, historically significant area. Channels and ditches manage tidal swells. Climate is mild; rainfall sparse. In winter, regional predators wander into the city seeking food.

## History

Otto Bouwengracht, from a family of ambitious fisherfolk, sailed to the Eye of the Sea seeking larger catches. There he encountered the **Filth Grazer** — a powerful otherworldly creature that spoke in a soothing feminine voice. It offered knowledge and counsel to make Liesech prosperous. In exchange, it would feed on the city's waste and dead, maintained through ceremonial sea offerings. Bouwengracht agreed.

Under Bouwengracht's leadership, Liesech grew wealthy. He built canals, irrigation, and a merchant fleet. Liesech declared itself a free city. Both the Burach Empire and Charneault Kingdom used its ports, but Liesech's rising taxes provoked imperial anger.

### Annexation by the Burach Empire

The Empire unleashed a weapon: **Analita von Raiza**, an ancient vampire discovered chained in a coffin beneath Rauland, starved for centuries. She was sent to Liesech as an emissary to compel the Bouwengrachts into annexation through vampiric domination. The Empire believed they could control her through her need for blood. They underestimated her ambitions.

### The Red Mourning

Von Raiza charmed her way to the Bouwengrachts, dominated them, and learned everything — including the Filth Grazer bargain. Growing tired of being the Empire's puppet, she acted: she drained and killed the Bouwengracht family and other nobles in a single night.

This severed the contract with the Filth Grazer. The creature retaliated with destructive tidal swells carrying tentacled, many-mouthed minions that consumed everything in their path. Von Raiza flew to the Eye of the Sea to fight the creature.

### Battle with the Filth Grazer

Neither von Raiza nor the Filth Grazer could defeat the other. When von Raiza tried to dominate its mind, she found the same soothing voice that had tempted Bouwengracht. It offered her the same bargain: knowledge, luxury, power — if she maintained the waste offerings and paid a future tithe. As a show of force, it devoured traumatic memories from von Raiza and the populace alike. The vampire sealed the deal in blood — a vampire's most binding act.

### The Weeping Pox

The Filth Grazer's vengeance was hidden in the bargain. It placed a virus in each corpse of the honored dead. The disease spread at the funerals following the Red Mourning, then traveled outward from there.

**Symptoms**: Hyper-vigilance and excitability, then delirium and fever. In final stages, flesh turns bone-white with two red pustules on the neck. The victim weeps all blood from their body before death.

Von Raiza discovered that infected blood, while sustaining her vampiric nature, made her grotesque — covered in boils, pox, and sores that never heal. The Pox was the Filth Grazer's leash: she dare not break the blood bargain.

The **Morbus Doctore** — masked healers from across Etharis — arrived to treat the afflicted. They built Zielenhaus, an iron-walled compound, and began producing the green smoke that feeds the city's haze. They offer hope of a cure, but few are actually cured.

### The Spread of the Pox

Trade ships carried the Pox abroad. The Burach Empire blockaded Liesech, but merchants, smugglers, and pirates continued using the port. Von Raiza and the Morbus Doctore claim the Pox is under control — they have claimed this for years. Liesech prospers despite the plague, much as it did under Bouwengracht.

### The Liesecher Way

Citizens greet visitors pleasantly, but something dark lurks behind every expression. Mention of the Pox brings silent reverence and a glance toward the bay. Life follows the High Belfry bells: five morning tolls start the day, six evening tolls drive activity indoors. Those out after six bells work for the Gold Barge Company — or risk joining the accepted nightly disappearances.

Locals call the Pox a necessity: it prevents overpopulation and scares visitors from staying. They note it "spares" children — though children still bear disfiguring marks and spread the disease.

### Culture

**Ceremony of the Nurturing Sea**: Annual white-robed procession from the High Belfry to the docks. Families place offerings on boats pushed into the bay for "the old sea gods." In truth, the boats feed the Filth Grazer and maintain von Raiza's contract. Something else may watch from the depths — the old sea gods may not be as gone as assumed.

**Week of the Grand Citywide Market**: Formerly quarterly, now twice yearly. Ships arrive for open-street trading. Visitors undergo Gold Barge Company and Morbus Doctore inspections. Affluent guests visit the High Belfry only after dark — "The Countess prefers company at night." Notable recent event: scholar Professor Terrence Myron von Kufen was escorted to the High Belfry by the Morbus Doctore and hasn't been seen since.

## Districts

**Hauthoff-Liesech (Main Town)**: Dark stone buildings, cobblestone streets. Bell posts at each corner alert Morbus Doctore to Pox cases or deaths. The Gold Barge Company provides transport. Two structures are walled off: the High Belfry and Zielenhaus.

**Traughafen (Dock Town)**: Stretches across Bite Bay's shoreline. The Gold Barge Company operates shipyards and boathouses. Sanitized Handelzimmer rooms calm merchants fearing Pox exposure. Inns and taverns provide modest lodging.

## Important Individuals

### The Bouwengracht Family
The founding family is immortalized in history, though records of Otto's bargain with the Filth Grazer have vanished. After their murder, Countess von Raiza assumed their holdings as "temporary executor." The family has ties to Rauland but no heir has come forward. Rumors persist that a child, **Wilhelm Bouwengracht**, was smuggled to safety during the murders.

### Countess Analita von Raiza
One of the oldest known vampires. Captured and imprisoned in an enchanted sarcophagus by ancient Burach tribes, nearly losing her mind. Released by the Empire as an agent, she completed her mission then betrayed them. Disfigured by the Weeping Pox from feeding on infected blood, she rules from the High Belfry, nursed by the **Cult of the Black Lady** — her vampiric spawn who serve as advisors, bodyguards, and agents.

### The Filth Grazer
An otherworldly being dwelling in the Eye of the Sea. Appears as a miasmic mass of eyes, mouths, and tentacles speaking with soothing, alien voices. Feeds on all waste — physical and metaphysical — incorporating it into its intellect. Its excretions carry slivers of its sentience through seawater and air, creating a far-reaching telepathic bond it uses to torment victims. The Weeping Pox is its creation: every carrier is a thrall expanding its knowledge and influence across Etharis.

## Important Factions

### Cult of the Black Lady
Von Raiza's vampiric spawn. They perform overt and covert acts to bolster her power and eliminate opposition. They hunt for Pox-free individuals whose untainted blood can help the countess recover. Mixed vampiric and non-vampiric membership allows day-and-night operations. They distrust the Morbus Doctore. Day-shift cultists report hearing the Countess cry out in her sleep about some powerful force in the sea — they don't know what it is.

### Golden Barge Company (G.B.C.)
Traces lineage to Bouwengracht-era devotees of Aphaleon, Arch Seraph of Temperance, who rejected Otto's sea offerings as blasphemy. They protect and transport Liesechers and commerce without discrimination. They employ thieves and assassins when profitable, justifying it as the greater good. They know every street, sewer, and waterway. A few leaders suspect the Red Mourning and Weeping Pox are connected, trusting Aphaleon will reveal the truth.

### Morbus Doctore
Masked healers in dark coats and bird-like masks, recruited from across Etharis after the Pox emerged. Operate from **Zielenhaus**, their iron-walled compound. Most patients die, but suffering is lessened. They treat the sick respectfully, play with children, and offer hope. However, whispers grow of unrelated experiments inside Zielenhaus, and blood-covered plague-masked figures have been spotted skulking through alleys at night.

## Notable Locations

### Eye of the Sea
Dark, turbulent waters at Bite Bay's center where ships are lost. Legends speak of sea creature settlements and sunken treasure. Otto Bouwengracht encountered the Filth Grazer here. Only von Raiza knows the creature's corporeal form resides there.

### The High Belfry
Liesech's central structure, evolved from the original town hall into a multi-story manse with council hall and bell-tower chapel. Named humbly by Otto Bouwengracht. Now renovated for von Raiza and the Cult of the Black Lady. Public access limited to special occasions.

### Zielenhaus
The Morbus Doctore's four-block iron-walled compound. Contains cells, examination rooms, laboratories, libraries, and vaults. Children gather at its gates to play with the healers. The tallest structure, the **Morbus Plinth**, is a factory-like complex housing secret laboratories and the central dispersal chamber producing the green haze.

## Quest Hooks

### Golden Knights
The G.B.C. hires mercenaries for night deliveries in Traughafen that keep failing — employees vanish. The target: an iron barrel of foreign Weeping Pox samples bound for the Morbus Doctore. Both plague carrion and Cult of the Black Lady thieves want it — the cultists believe it may hold a cure. Leads to confrontation with senior cult leaders and ultimately to Zielenhaus gates.

### The Relic
The Froth Church — Bouwengracht's house of worship that veiled the Filth Grazer cult — was destroyed during the Red Mourning. Its ruins jut from a sandbar near shore. No one ventures there; the Filth Grazer consumed all memory of it. The church's tithe coffer, ceremonial instruments, and gilded altar remain in the sinking ruins. Something touched by the Filth Grazer's essence may still linger.

### Captured Envoy
Ivik Garpok, a wulven diplomat from Ostoya's Crimson Court, was sent to investigate weaponizing the Weeping Pox against rebels and the Order of Dawn. His bodyguards were found dead; he is missing. Characters are hired to investigate quietly. Trail leads to Order of Dawn agents who captured Ivik — they seek to make the Pox affect undead.
