# Chapter 12: Magic in Etharis

## Arcane Magic and Numena

Arcane magic draws on a force called **numen** — energy from a parallel dimension called **Numena**, first named by archmage Raakan el-Adakar. Casting a spell means "speaking" to Numena using verbal, somatic, and material components, weakening the border between Numena and the Material Plane to shape numen into effect.

Wizards learn spells through study; sorcerers touch Numena instinctively; warlocks receive arcane knowledge whispered from unknown entities beyond the Material Plane. Numena's true nature is disputed — whether it is a traversable realm, an invisible force, or a living entity attempting to reshape reality. Raakan himself went mad after visiting it and died incoherent, locked in Cold Iron Keep.

## Societal Mistrust

Magic is feared and hated by those who cannot wield it. To rulers, it represents a threat to authority. To commoners, mages are a convenient scapegoat for every unexplained misfortune. There is genuine basis for the fear — Ulmyr's Gate was obliterated when its mages tried to open a permanent portal to the Ethereal Plane.

A growing theory holds that spellcasters are responsible for the Great Beast — that every spell cast thins the veil between Numena and the Material Plane, allowing horrors to seep through. This is unproven, but it has made mages even more despised. Wise mages hide what they can do. Even allies may turn hostile when confronted with obvious spellcasting.

## Regional Attitudes

- **Bürach**: Magic valued as a tool of imperial power, but mages are strictly licensed. The Magician's Codex governs what spells they may cast and under what conditions. Sorcerers and warlocks operating outside these sanctions are outlaws.
- **Ostoya (Crimson Court territory)**: Magic is the exclusive right of the vampire nobility. Mages must serve the Crimson Court or be destroyed.
- **Ostoya (Raevo)**: Magic is accepted and valued — necessary to maintain independence against the Crimson Court and Bürach.
- **Charneault**: Elves revere magic as integral to life. Human-controlled areas vary: respected in learned circles, viewed with suspicion by Knight Chapters, actively opposed by the Severe Templars.
- **Castinella**: All arcane and primal magic is heresy. The Arcanist Inquisition arrests, imprisons, and executes mages. Common folk believe magic is witchcraft and demonism.
- **Valika**: Magic is treated like any skilled craft — masters take apprentices, and trained mages travel freely after completing their learning.

## Colleges of Magic

The major arcane colleges are the primary institutions regulating magical education. Each reflects its nation's attitude toward magic.

**Erlefurt Colleges (Bürach)** — The largest concentration of magical colleges on the continent, centered in the capital Erlefurt. The oldest and most prestigious is the Magister College. All graduates swear to the Magician's Codex, which restricts their activities. Elite abjurers called Enforcers hunt mages who break the code. Erlefurt treats unsanctioned mages as enemy combatants.

**Ravencourt Sanctuary (Raevo/Ostoya)** — Hidden on an enchanted isle, the Sanctuary believes magic grows only through freedom and imposes almost no restrictions. The one rule: keep the Sanctuary's secrets. Graduates sign their name in blood to a cursed book — if they betray the Sanctuary, the page tears itself out and they die. Technically at war with the Erlefurt Colleges.

**Sarmar Academy (Charneault)** — An entirely elven institution in Tol Leyemil, focused on patient observation of the natural world as a path to spellcraft. Magical education takes decades. Non-elves are barred from the main academy; a small external branch (Aemanyir House) teaches non-elves on an experimental basis.

**Renegade Colleges** — Unsanctioned schools viewed with scorn by established institutions. In Castinella, secret underground centers help arcanically-sensitive individuals control their powers before the Inquisition finds them.

## The Planes

Planes of existence other than the Material Plane are perilous and should be rare destinations — not a frontier for adventure, but an ocean of cosmic horror threatening to drown the mortal world.

- **Realm of Faerie (Feywild)** — Stitched into the fabric of the Material Plane; entered through hidden portals. A wilderness of contradictions ruled by seasonal Faerie Courts (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter). Beautiful and nightmarish, its roads don't always lead to the same place.
- **Ethereal Plane** — Exists behind the veil of reality; encircles the Material Plane and connects all other planes. No natural portals. Used by powerful mages for teleportation. Eldritch horrors and Aether Kindred have been witnessed within it.
- **Primordial Realms** — Four elemental planes (Air, Fire, Water, Earth) that the Valikans believe cradle the mortal world. Named Flamebourne (Fire), the Endless Sea (Water), Stonereach (Earth), and the Whispering Void (Air). Accessible only where an element is strongest — volcanos, deep ocean — and deeply hostile to mortals.
- **Netherworld** — Created when the Aetheric War ruptured the Heavens. Ruled by Arch Daemons, each in their own hellish fiefdom. Only for the dead, or those who use dark magic to breach it. Daemonic incursions occur when cracks open into the mortal realm.
- **The Heavens** — Rebuilt as a fortress after the Aetheric War by the Arch Seraphs. Heavily guarded; no portals open to the mortal realm. No verified account of its interior exists.

## Numena

Numena is the name Raakan el-Adakar gave to the source of arcane power — possibly a traversable plane, possibly an invisible force, possibly a living entity. It cannot be visited or confirmed despite centuries of effort. Some fear it is the realm of the Aether Kindred: a void of chaos and distant dark stars. Others believe secret "astral travelers" have visited it and return as near-demigods, possibly connected to a shadowy cabal of archmages called the Thaumaturge. Most scholars dismiss this. Each spell cast may thin the veil between Numena and the Material Plane, potentially explaining the proliferation of aberrant horrors in Etharis.
