# Chapter 14: Magic Items

Unleashing the Enchantment

No dark fantasy game or setting would be complete without magic items. The heroes, villains, and threats inhabiting Etharis find any advantage they can, and magic is a powerful tool in their arsenals.

## Magic Items in Etharis

Like the world of Etharis itself, the magic items in a Grim Hollow campaign tend to be a twisted and dark reflection of the powerful relics in a fantasy world. After all, the same magic that casts a malicious pall over the people and creatures of Etharis is harnessed to create magic items.

Like any fantasy world, most magic items in Etharis can be picked up and used by anyone, or used after the wielder takes the time to attune to the item. Some rarer items are particularly useful for creatures who have started to undergo a Transformation.

The Arcanist Inquisition’s philosophical leaders have agreed that using magic items that tap into arcane powers is no less dangerous and corrupting than channeling arcane energies through spellcasting. Worst of all, anyone willing to attune to a corrupting magic item is as culpable as a wizard or a sorcerer.

However, the Arcanist Inquisition agrees that attuning to or using divinely inspired items is permissible, especially if it aids in the hunting, capture, and ultimate redemption of arcanists. Not surprisingly, what is a safe magic item and what is not is often up to interpretation by the leaders of the Inquisition, who are likely attuned to a powerful item or two.

Using remnants of magical creatures, even the most horrible monster, is a common practice among the experienced adventurers of Etharis. The dreaded, feared, but ultimately necessary members of the various monster hunter guilds are most known for their manipulation of monster parts into tools of the trade.

### Magic Item Descriptions

The following magic items may be found in Etharis.

#### Adder Stone

Wondrous Item, Rare

These glassy stones are often found near the place where a marra was slain. Each Adder Stone is the size of a small fruit and has a natural hole in it. While you carry an Adder Stone, the first time you would gain the Paralyzed condition, the stone shatters and that condition is removed.

#### Aether Blood

Potion, Rare

The most extravagant rumors claim the creation of this glowing purple liquid requires the blood of a unicorn -- a creature generally regarded as mythical. Regardless of the truth, the potent magic in a vial of Aether Blood leads many spellcasters to carry it for use in dire circumstances.

Consuming this potion enhances your spellcasting prowess for 1 minute. While the effect lasts, add 1 to the saving throw DCs for the spells you cast. In addition, whenever your spell could be cast using a higher-level spell slot, increase the spell's effective level by 1.

Prolonged use of Aether Blood is grueling. You can consume it safely a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). Any further use results in painful addiction. While addicted, you have Disadvantage on Strength and Constitution ability checks and saving throws, and attack rolls against you have Advantage. Consuming another vial of Aether Blood negates this effect until you finish a Long Rest, but you must also make a DC 10 Charisma save. On a failure, you become lethargic, and your Speed is reduced by 20 feet for 1 minute.

The addiction lasts until you receive a Greater Restoration spell, or until you have not consumed Aether Blood for 30 days.

#### Amulet of Curse Suppression

Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This glittering amulet of ivory and silver can be used to temporarily fend off the effects of curses. While attuned to the amulet, the effects of any curses on you are nullified, as though they had been removed by the Remove Curse spell.

However, this relief is only temporary. When the amulet is removed or you end your attunement to it, any curses it was suppressing return with full force.

#### Angelskin Armor

Armor (Leather), Legendary (Requires Attunement)

This leather armor is made from the flayed flesh of angels. While attuned to the armor, you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage. Any Celestial that sees this armor immediately becomes Hostile toward the wearer, and any Fiend wants to possess it at any cost.

#### Arcane Oil

Potion, Rare

This substance is created by distilling the jelly that encases the brain of a xakalonus. You can take a Bonus Action to coat one weapon or up to 20 pieces of ammunition. If the coated objects are not magical, for the next minute, your attacks with the coated weapon or ammunition gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. If the coated objects are magical, they instead gain the ability to ignore the hraptnon's Immunity to magical weapons.

#### Arc Rifle

Wondrous Item, Very Rare

In the unforgiving lands of Etharis, having reliable and efficient weapons is as essential as carrying water and food. In that regard, skilled inventors are highly sought, and among them gnomes are the most renowned. However, innovation can sometimes go too far, and the Arc Rifle is the perfect example of how.

This part-science, part-arcane contraption is a rifle-like weapon housing glass tubes of sparking electricity and powered by a small perpetual motion device. Invented by a team of famous gnome engineers, it literally shoots lightning, hitting the target faster than other missile weapons and reducing flesh to ashes in mere seconds. Due in part to this tremendous efficiency, this weapon was quickly declared illegal in most regions of Etharis. It is also believed to destroy the target’s soul when it kills them.

The rifle has 4 charges. You can expend 1 charge as a Magic action to launch a torrent of lightning in a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. Each creature in the Line must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d6 Lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.

Regaining Charges. The rifle regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the rifle becomes a useless hunk of metal and glass.


#### Ardor

Weapon (Battleaxe), Artifact (Requires Attunement)

The blades of this axe smolder with an inner light, and the dark wooden handle is accented with silver. After the celestial realm turned back yet another assault from the forces of evil, a fiendish forge master received a vision, allowing them to craft a weapon like no other. Ardor now lies in a hidden vault, waiting to be wielded by a scion of darkness who strikes on behalf of the forces of the Arch Daemons. The fiends don’t realize that an equally powerful weapon rests in the hands of the celestials, who wage eternal war against them. In the interest of maintaining order, the fates who inspired the forgemaster to craft Ardor also sent a similar vision to a smith of heaven. Axe of Hell. This magic battleaxe grants a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. When you hit with it, the target takes an extra 2d10 Necrotic damage. Champion of Evil. While holding the battleaxe, you have Immunity to the Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, and Stunned conditions. Siraj Fakhri Ardor

Ardor’s Pursuit. Ardor's Pursuit: You can take a Bonus Action to toss the axe into the air, causing it to hover magically in your space. While the weapon hovers, you can take a Bonus Action to cause it to fly up to 50 feet to another spot within 50 feet of you. As part of the same Bonus Action, you can either grasp the weapon if it is within reach, or cause it to attack one creature within 5 feet of the weapon. The weapon uses your attack roll and adds your ability modifier to the damage rolls. The weapon ceases to hover if you grasp it, if you are more than 50 feet away from it, or if you die.

Spells. While the axe is on your person, you can use a Magic action to cast one of the following spells (save DC 18): Bane, Bestow Curse, or Incendiary Cloud. Once you use the axe to cast a spell, that spell can’t be cast again from it until the next dawn.

Destroying the Axe. The only thing capable of damaging the battleaxe is its celestial twin, Zeal. A creature wielding Zeal can choose to attack Ardor, which has an Armor Class of 21 and 300 HP. No other creature or object can damage Ardor.

#### Armor of Zombie Flesh

Armor (Hide), Rare (Requires Attunement)

This armor is formed from the tanned flesh of a zombie. While wearing this armor, your Armor Class becomes 14 + your Dexterity modifier. While attuned to this armor, when you take Necrotic damage, you can use a Reaction to halve that Necrotic damage (round down).

#### Banshee Box

Wondrous Item, Common

This ivory music box is inlaid with silver decorations depicting tiny scenes of gruesome violence. If a creature holds the box for at least one minute, they can use a Magic action to mentally command the box to make a sound or phrase of their choosing, lasting up to one minute. Once the lid is open, that sound or phrase continues to play on a loop until the box is closed. If the person who created the repeating sound travels more than 300 feet away from the box, the lid closes and the music box ceases to play.

The box can create any soundscape, like haunting piano music, the sounds of a lively party, a spoken phrase, or a continuous series of screams. When the Magic action is used to create the sounds, the user can also choose whether the sound is audible in an Emanation with a 5-foot radius, a 30-foot radius, or a 120-foot radius.

#### Blessed Reliquary

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

This small wooden or ivory box holds a piece of a saint. While it is on your person, you gain the following benefits:

- You have Advantage on saving throws caused by Undead creatures.
- You can use each of your Channel Divinity class features one additional time.
- When you use Turn Undead, you may choose for the targets to have Disadvantage on their saving throws. This can be used once. You regain its use after a Long Rest.

#### Bliss

Wondrous Item, Uncommon

The magical drug comes in the form of a small, round pellet of white powder. If ingested by a Humanoid, that creature is filled with feelings of calm and happiness.

Upon ingestion, you have the following effects for 1d4 hours:

- You have Immunity to the Frightened condition.
- You gain 3d10 Temporary Hit Points.
- Your Speed is reduced by 15 feet.
- You have Disadvantage on D20 Tests.
- If you cause another creature to make a saving throw, it has Advantage on that save.

After the effects wear off, you must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, or you gain 1 Exhaustion level.

#### Blood Capsule

Wondrous Item, Common

This cylindrical flask is etched with arcane runes. Humanoid blood stored in the blood capsule remains fresh for up to one year. The flask stores enough blood to sustain a vampire for one day.


#### Bow of Four

Weapon (Longbow), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

David Alunan Bow of Four This white wooden bow is set with four gemstones: a ruby, an emerald, a sapphire, and a diamond. When you hold this bow, as a Bonus Action you can summon a magical arrow composed of one of the four elements. You must still make an attack roll to hit a creature with the magical arrow, and it does only the damage listed. When you take the Attack action, you can attack with this summoned arrow. If you have a feature that lets you fire more than one arrow on a turn, you can fire other arrows as part of the Attack action. Each time you summon a magical arrow, you must choose one of the four options below. If you do not fire the arrow within 1 minute of summoning it, it dissolves. Arrow of Air. On a hit, the arrow of air deals 4d8 Lightning damage. Arrow of Earth. On a hit, the arrow of earth deals 4d8 Bludgeoning damage.

Arrow of Fire. On a hit, the arrow of fire deals 4d8 Fire damage.

Arrow of Water. On a hit, the arrow of water deals 4d8 Cold damage.

#### Brazen Armor

Armor (Plate Armor), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This armor, fashioned from the body of an Empyrean brazen bull, gleams brightly in direct sunlight. While wearing this armor, you have a +1 bonus to Armor Class. In addition, you have Resistance to Fire and Radiant damage.

#### Cap of Bravery

Wondrous Item, Uncommon

Woven from the remains of an old snarite, this cap provides the wearer with a sense of magical peace and calm that emanates from them. While wearing this cap, you radiate an aura in a 30-foot Emanation. You and allies in this aura have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end effects that include the Frightened condition.

#### Carvaloth Scale Armor

Armor (Scale Mail), Uncommon

This red-tinged armor made from carvaloth scales automatically shifts its color to camouflage you. When you wear this armor, Wisdom (Perception) checks made to perceive you have Disadvantage, and you have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.


#### Caustic Rapier

Weapon (Rapier), Rare (Requires Attunement)

This strange weapon is fashioned from the mandible of a hivewolf. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. When you roll a 19 or 20 on your attack roll with this weapon, that attack does an additional 7 (2d6) Acid damage. In addition, you have Immunity to Acid damage while wielding the weapon.

#### Cinder Coat

Armor (Leather), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This floor-length leather duster is the color of dying embers. You have a +2 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this armor.

The coat has 3 charges. When you hit a creature with a melee attack, and that creature is not at its Hit Point maximum, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 1d10 Fire damage to the target. The coat regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

#### Cloak of the Listener

Wondrous Item, Rare

This is a cloak made of ears that were once part of a swarm of ears. It pulses gently and oozes yellow fluid from its many holes. While wearing this cloak, you can take a Magic action to create one of two effects:

Ooze. You tug the cloak to create a puddle of waxy goo that covers the ground in a 10-foot square centered on a point within 30 feet of you. A creature that enters or starts its turn in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or have the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). This wax remains for 1 minute.

Hide. You completely conceal your head with the cloak. You have the Blinded condition, you have Blindsight with a range of 120 feet, and you can hear all speech within 120 feet regardless of obstructions. These effects last for 1 minute.

Once each effect is used, that effect can't be used again until the next dawn.

#### Cloak of the Mausgeist

Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This fine garment is made of mausgeist fur with a silk lining. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits:

Heightened Senses. You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

Magic Protection. You have Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Charm. You can take a Magic action to cast the Charm Person spell (save DC 13). Once used, this property of the cloak can’t be used again until the next dawn.

#### Cloak of the Stygian Bat

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

This cloak is made from stygian bat hides and bristles with coarse black hair, While wearing the cloak, you have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

In addition, you can take a Magic action to gain a Fly speed of 30 feet for one minute. While this effect is active, you also have Blindsight with a range of 120 feet, and you have Advantage on Dexterity saving throws while in the air. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

Cloak of the Stygian Bat

#### Cloak of Therianthropy

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

This spotted grey cloak is made of styrkvisnar hide. While wearing it, you have Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks.

Additionally, you can take a Magic action to transform into a wolf, eagle, or seal for up to 1 hour. The transformation otherwise functions as the Polymorph spell, but you can take a Bonus Action to revert to your normal form. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

#### Coat of Lies

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)

This coat is made from the tongues of a harvester of lies’ victims. While you wear it, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class and you have Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks.

The cloak has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. You can use a Bonus Action and expend 1 charge while wearing the cloak to enhance the next spell you cast before the start of your next turn, allowing that spell to be cast without any Verbal or Somatic components.

#### Coin of War and Fear

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

This dark metal coin is a token of heretical dogma that claims Empyreus and Beleth are one and the same. One side bears the Empyreus's icon, while the other shows Beleth's leering face.

While holding this coin, you can take a Magic action to flip it. Roll a d6. The icon of Empyreus shows on an even number, granting you the Boon of Empyreus. On an odd number, Beleth shows and confers the Boon of Beleth.

Boon of Empyreus. For one minute, you gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class, and you add 1d4 to the first attack roll you make each of your turns. If you would receive this boon again before the next dawn, you instead take 3d6 Radiant damage and the boon is not applied.

Boon of Beleth. For one minute, an aura radiates from you in a 10-foot Emanation. When an enemy ends its turn in the area, it must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or have the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. While the aura lasts, you deal an extra 1d6 Psychic damage whenever you hit a Frightened creature with a weapon attack. If you would receive this boon again before the next dawn, you instead take 3d6 Necrotic damage and the boon is not applied.

#### Coldrazor

Weapon (Handaxe), Legendary (Requires Attunement)

This infamous axe is wielded by the memori lich known as Runa Banasár. It appears to be made of ice, carved with runes and studded with various tiny, glittering gems.

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. On a hit, the weapon deals an extra 14 (4d6) Cold damage. If a single attack with Coldrazor deals more than 20 points of Cold damage, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn.

Immediately after hitting or missing with a ranged attack made with this weapon, it flies back to your hand.

#### Collar of the Undead

Wondrous Item, Very Rare

Some people have no qualms about exploiting any opportunity for profit, including the sorrow caused by the loss of a loved one. Such people would appreciate the Collar of the Undead, whether as con artists looking for bereaved individuals to fleece, or as dark sorcerers in need of souls.

As a Magic action, you can place this collar around the neck of a Humanoid creature of Large size or smaller that has died within the last 10 days. The creature returns to life with 1 Hit Point. The creature uses its normal game statistics, except that its creature type becomes Undead and it doesn't need to eat, sleep, or breathe. If its Hit Points are reduced to 0, the creature is utterly destroyed, turning into dust.

Each dawn, the creature’s Hit Point Maximum decreases by 1d4 as its soul slowly slips away. When the creature’s Hit Point Maximum reaches 0, or its Hit Points are reduced to 0, it falls apart into a heap of dust and is destroyed.

On each of your turns, you can take a Bonus Action to mentally command the creature if it is within 60 feet of you, and it follows your orders to the best of your ability. If you issue no commands, the creature takes the Dodge action and moves only to avoid harm. Once given an order, the creature continues to follow it until its task is complete.

Over time, the creature's soul slips away. Each dawn, reduce the creature's Hit Point maximum by 1d4. When its Hit Point Maximum reaches 0, it is destroyed as described above.

If the collar is removed, the creature is immediately reduced to 0 Hit Points.

#### Crimson Mist

Wondrous Item, Uncommon

This tiny bottle contains a cocktail of pheromones from creatures of prey. As a Magic action, you spray the pheromones on one target within 5 feet, and its effect lasts for 1 minute.

Beasts or other creatures that must attack the nearest creature will target the affected individual instead, as long as that individual is within 60 feet of the attacker.

#### Crown of the Keppmir King

Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This platinum crown is adorned with black gems and shells. While you wear the crown, you have Resistance to Cold damage and can breathe underwater. Also, your Constitution is 20 while you wear the crown, unless it is 20 or higher without it.

Curse. This crown is cursed, a fact revealed only when an Identify spell is cast on the crown or you attune to it. Attuning to the crown curses you until you are targeted by the Remove Curse spell or similar magic; it is impossible to remove the crown. While cursed, you turn into a keppmir after 2d10 days.


#### Dreams Leaves

Potion, Uncommon

Made from a mysterious, magically grown plant, this popular new drug induces vivid hallucinations in which the user's dreams appear to be fulfilled. Some users even see visions of branching possibilities that their life could take.

Upon consuming Dreams Leaves, you can choose to fall into a blissful, trance-like state for 1 hour, during which time you have the Unconscious condition. Taking damage ends the trance early.

If you remain in the trance for the full hour, you awaken with a faint insight into your future. Once before you finish your next Long Rest, you can take a Reaction to reroll a D20 Test and use the new result.

Using this Reaction creates an addiction to Dreams Leaves. While addicted, you have Disadvantage on saving throws. Consuming another dose of Dreams Leaves negates this effect until you finish a Long Rest.

The addiction lasts until you receive a Greater Restoration spell, or until you have not consumed Dreams Leaves for 30 days.

#### Ectoplasmic Whip

Weapon (Whip), Rare (Requires Attunement)

This weapon is made of silver spirit energy that phases in and out of reality, making it extremely difficult for living creatures to perceive. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Attack rolls made with the whip against non-Undead creatures have Advantage.

#### Elixir of Elemental Spittle

Potion, Rare

This foul-smelling liquid is made from the saliva of an elemental troll. For one minute after consuming the potion, you can take a Bonus Action to spit energy at a creature within 5 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 damage of a type corresponding to the troll whose spittle was used to craft the potion. No damage is inflicted on a successful save. If the target rolls a natural 20 on its saving throw, the potion explodes inside you. You take 6d6 damage of the type listed for it, and the potion's duration ends immediately.

#### Elixir of Life Protection

#### Enchanted Feather

#### Fin Symbiote

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

This tendril of flesh, harvested from an Aberration, melds with the body of its host. If you are a Humanoid, you can attune to this item by stitching it into your skin during the Attunement process. At the end of this time, the fin burrows into your body and bonds with your muscular system. Non-Humanoids cannot attune to the Fin Symbiote.

While attuned to the symbiote, you gain the following benefits:

Symbiotic Health. You have Advantage on Constitution saving throws.

Immovable. When you would be moved against your will, you can take a Reaction to subtract 10 feet from that movement.

Aberrant Power. If you have achieved at least Stage 1 of the Aberrant Horror Transformation, add +2 to the DC of save DCs for your Aberrant Horror Boons.

Symbiote Link. You cannot voluntarily end your Attunement to this magic item. If it is physically removed from your body, it regrows in 1d4 hours unless you receive a Remove Curse spell before it grows back (which also ends your Attunement).


#### Faerie Crown

Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This crown is woven of aromatic flowers. When you first don the crown after attuning to it, 1d6 + 3 butterflies begin fluttering around it.

When you are hit by a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to release one of the butterflies. The attack misses you and destroys the butterfly instead.

Once all butterflies are destroyed, the crown withers and crumbles to dust.

#### Flammable Tincture

Wondrous Item, Uncommon

This swirling mixture of red and white liquid is formed from the remains of a coldfire ooze. As a Magic action, the vial of Flammable Tincture can be hurled up to 30 feet at a target, which must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or be covered with the liquid. For 1 minute, the creature loses its Immunity or Resistance to Fire damage and has Vulnerability to Fire damage instead.

#### Full Moon Extract

Potion, Uncommon

This potent powder is supposedly created by grinding up the dried organs of a werewolf. Despite the risks associated with its use, it is a favored drug of many wrestlers, boxers, and soldiers because of the raw strength it provides.

Consuming this potion enhances your physical prowess for 1 hour. While the effect lasts, your Strength score becomes 19 (unless it was already 19 or higher), and you have Advantage on Strength ability checks and saving throws.

Prolonged use of Full Moon Extract is risky. You can consume it safely a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). Any further use results in painful addiction. While addicted, you have Disadvantage on Dexterity ability checks and saving throws, and attack rolls against you have Advantage.

Consuming another dose of Full Moon Extract negates this effect until you finish a Long Rest, but you must also make a DC 10 Charisma save. On a failure, you become overcome by violent urges for 1 minute. During that time, you cannot take Bonus Actions or Reactions. At the start of each of your turns, you regard the creature nearest to you that you can see or hear as your enemy. During that turn, you must move as close to the creature as possible and take the Attack action, targeting the creature. If your movement doesn't bring you within reach, you must use your Dash action to continue moving toward it.

The addiction lasts until you receive a Greater Restoration spell, or until you have not consumed Full Moon Extract for 30 days.

#### Funeral Mask

Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

This delicate mask is made of thinly beaten copper that molds to the skin when worn. While wearing this mask, you can use a Magic action to alter your appearance, including your height, weight, facial features, the sound of your voice, hair length, coloration, and distinguishing characteristics for 8 hours. You can make yourself appear as a member of another species, though none of your statistics change. You also can’t appear as a creature of a different size, and your basic shape stays the same; if you’re bipedal, you can’t use this item to become quadrupedal, for instance.

Creatures within 5 feet of you can use a Study action to make a DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check to discern your true appearance.

If you have reached at least Stage 1 of the Lich Transformation, you also gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class while your appearance is disguised by the mask.

Once you use the mask, you cannot do so again until the following dawn.

#### Gristaline

Potion, Uncommon

This gruesome mixture is made from ground mouruge bones, along with rotten meat and mud. When consumed, your Strength score becomes 19 for 1 hour (unless it is already 19 or higher). You also have Disadvantage on Intelligence ability checks and saving throws for the duration.

Wondrous Item, Very Rare

This golden trumpet is never dulled by rust or dirt. As a Magic action, you can blow the trumpet to create a rousing battle call that can be heard up to 600 feet away.

#### Iridescent Armor

Armor (Breastplate), Rare (Requires Attunement)

## The Four Sacred Artifacts

Four Sacred Artifacts appeared at the dawn of the Bürach Empire, each a divine gift from the four gods: Aurelia, Galt, Maligant, and Ulmyr. These relics were given to Bürach’s first emperor, Indorius I, and inherited by each of his successors.

Maligant provided the mighty sword, Dämglesvir, to slay Bürach’s enemies. Galt placed a crown on Indorius I’s head to signify his divine right. Ulmyr gifted the emperor an orb of foresight to make him a wise ruler. Aurelia clad him in a breastplate, wishing only to protect him and his heirs.

Many centuries later, after Emperor Leopold I was assassinated, custody of the artifacts sparked a civil war. Each of the holy treasures disappeared amid the fighting. One religiously fervent tale claims they blazed with radiant light before banishing themselves beyond mortal reach. Other rumors suggest they were each stolen or lost in various separate conspiracies. It’s certain that whoever holds the Sacred Artifacts controls the fate of the Bürach Empire.

### Wielding An Artifact

Before disappearing, the artifacts were tarnished and corrupted by Emperor Leopold I’s delusions, cruelty, and malice. Darkness had seeped into them, transforming their divine power and purpose. Though it’s heretical to suggest, some believe the artifacts’ befoulment is somehow linked to the death of the gods and the growing influence of the Great Beast.

Wielding even one Sacred Artifact is difficult. Their true power can only be unlocked by practicing their use. Yet this necessitates more time exposed to the darkness that now infuses them, which can infest the wielder’s body and mind.

### Attunement and Proficiency

Each Sacred Artifact has three tiers of Attunement available to its user. When a character attunes to the item, they have access only to the benefits (and the curse) listed under the Aspirant tier. The Adept and Master tiers of attunement are unlocked over time (see Attunement Milestones, below).

Attunement to a Sacred Artifact can't be voluntarily ended. If the Attunement does end, the character can attune to the item again at the Aspirant tier.

Each artifact also curses its wielder. In addition to the curse effects listed for each tier, a character attuned to a Sacred Artifact is unwilling to part with the item, keeping it within reach at all times. If the artifact's curse is ended or suppressed, the character's Attunement with that item is broken.

### Attunement Milestones

To ascend to a higher tier of Attunement and unlock additional powers of a Sacred Artifact, the attuned character must achieve a specific milestone. There is no minimum level required to achieve a milestone. However, Adept-tier artifacts are appropriate for characters of level 5 to 10, while Master-tier artifacts are appropriate for characters of level 11 and above.

The GM and players should work together to create appropriate milestones for the campaign, using these examples (themed to the four artifacts) as guidance:

- Wield Dämglesvir to slay a mighty foe.
- Take no damage during a difficult combat.
- Earn the fealty of an important ruler.
- You alter a future witnessed in Ulmyr’s Eye.

### Shared Delusions

The darkness that seeped into the Sacred Artifacts causes their wielders to experience the same paranoid delusions that plagued Emperor Leopold I.

All characters attuned to a Sacred Artifact suffer the same delusions, which could include the sense of being watched, believing a friendly NPC to be hostile, or mistaking an innocent person or household pet for a monster. Characters can even experience multiple simultaneous delusions.

A character suffering a delusion can use the Search action to discern the truth, typically by succeeding on a DC 20 (or higher) Wisdom (Insight or Perception) check.

If the GM is using the Influence Dice rules from Chapter 16, the frequency and intensity of delusions increases while the number of dice in the Beast Pool exceeds the number of dice in the Resolve Pool.
