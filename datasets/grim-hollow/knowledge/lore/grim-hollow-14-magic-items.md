# Chapter 14: Magic Items

## Magic Items in Etharis

Like the world itself, magic items in a Grim Hollow campaign tend to be twisted and dark reflections of the powerful relics found in a typical fantasy setting. The same malicious magic that shadows the people and creatures of Etharis is harnessed to create these items. Most magic items can be used by anyone willing to attune to them — but the Arcanist Inquisition considers attuning to arcane items just as dangerous and corrupting as spellcasting itself.

Magic items in Etharis frequently carry curses, sinister properties, or signs of their creator's dark purpose. Players accustomed to reliable, consequence-free magic items may be surprised. A wand made by servants of an Arch Daemon may refuse to function — or harm its user — when turned toward good ends. Attunement is a commitment to the unknown.

## The Four Sacred Artifacts

At the dawn of the Bürach Empire, four gods each gifted Emperor Indorius I a divine relic: Maligant gave a sword, Galt a crown, Ulmyr an orb of foresight, and Aurelia a breastplate of protection. These items passed to each successive emperor for centuries.

After Emperor Leopold I was assassinated, custody of the artifacts sparked a civil war. All four disappeared amid the chaos. Whoever holds the Sacred Artifacts controls the fate of the Bürach Empire — and they are out there, waiting to be found.

Before disappearing, the artifacts were tarnished and corrupted by Leopold's cruelty and delusions. Darkness seeped into them, warping their divine purpose. Wielding one grows easier with practice — but each tier of mastery deepens the curse embedded in the item. All four artifacts share one affliction: **Shared Delusions** — every wielder suffers the same paranoid visions that consumed Leopold I, including a sense of being watched, seeing hostile faces on friends, and mistaking innocents for monsters.

### Dämglesvir — "Might of Heaven"

Originally a weapon wielded by the god Maligant himself during the Aetheric War. Its siblings are still carried by Empyreus (called Judgement) and Tormach (called Wrath). Once a gleaming blade; now black as shadow, as if dipped in darkness. The sword was meant to slay Bürach's enemies — it still does, but the wielder pays in flesh and vitality. Extended use causes the body to atrophy and resist healing.

### Hearthfire Breastplate — "Goddess's Protection"

Aurelia's gift to protect the emperor and his heirs. Once emblazoned with a golden sun that radiated light; now its surface is black and distorted, the metal seeming to writhe as if alive. The breastplate still protects its wearer and those nearby — but the corruption transforms that protective impulse into anguish, making the wearer feel every wound their companions suffer as their own psychic pain.

### Indorius's Crown — "Divine Authority"

Galt's gift signifying divine right to rule. Named for the first emperor who wore it, inherited by each successor. The crown amplifies the wearer's authority and power of compulsion — their reasoning seems stronger, their commands harder to resist. The corruption, however, erodes the wearer's capacity to listen or perceive the needs of others, making them increasingly dependent on the very followers they intimidate.

### Ulmyr's Eye — "Blessed Wisdom"

Ulmyr is said to have plucked out one of their own eyes as a gift for the emperor, placing it inside a golden reliquary orb — the Sovereign's Orb — inlaid with gems from every Bürach province. The orb reveals hazy, disturbing visions to those who peer within. It grants foresight and the ability to shape fate — but fills the wielder's dreams and meditations with nightmarish visions, and at its deepest level of corruption, causes the user to experience waking phantasmal terrors triggered by moments of extreme distress.
