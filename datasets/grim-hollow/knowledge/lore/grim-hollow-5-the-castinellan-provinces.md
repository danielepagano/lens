**Chapter 5: The Castinellan Provinces**

## Setting Tone

Castinella is a land where order has become the face of horror. The Arcanist Inquisition enforces a theocratic police state across the provinces of Toletum, Faro, and Therpena — and at its spiritual heart, the holy city of Ember Cairn. Arcane magic is not merely illegal; it is hunted with religious ecstasy. Torture is performed in churches to hymn-singing crowds. The impossible horror of the setting is that many citizens genuinely feel safer for it. Shadows are deepest where the light burns brightest.

The psychological core: those who need magic to survive — to fight monsters, cure curses, or protect their families — must choose between death by Inquisition and death by the creatures they cannot fight without it.

## Geography

A dry Mediterranean peninsula split by the Corova Mountains. The north is industrial (Toletum's forges and ore mines), the center fertile (Faro's cliffs and harbor), the south warm and vine-covered (Therpena). The Shattered Peaks rise in the south around Ember Cairn — jagged rock formed by some ancient cataclysm, which the faithful credit to the dangers of uncontrolled magic. Ancient dragonborn ruins buried under the Inquisition's new construction are everywhere, reminders of a heritage being deliberately erased.

## Key History

- **Unification**: The prophet-conqueror Sagrado Valieda claimed Empyreus gave him a mandate to purge magic. His movement grew from genuine monster-hunting heroism into systematic extermination. Dark whispers say it was Beleth, Arch Daemon of Fear, who spoke to him in the Shattered Peaks — not Empyreus at all.
- **Ember Cairn established**: Valieda chose the ruined dragonborn city as his stronghold, burying the ancient heritage under the Cathedral of Embers. The catacombs beneath it predate the Inquisition.
- **Purge declared**: The Inquisition shifted from containing dangerous mages to executing all arcanists — even those fully in control of their power.

## Tone Details: Life Under the Inquisition

Families hide magical children. Parents teach concealment before they teach reading. Torture is public, performed in amphitheaters while workers go about their day. The Penitent Mage — a nail-lined hollow statue hung over fire — channels screams down city streets. Citizens bear this with relief: each scream means the world is a little safer.

Curses are treated the same as magic: death is the only Inquisition-sanctioned cure. Black markets exist for actual cures, but honest healers are caught more often than fraudsters. Monster hunters are a tolerated necessity — possibly sorcerers in disguise, the clergy suspects. The Order of Templars (Inquisition-backed monster hunters who study but never cast magic) is regarded with suspicion by the very church that created them.

## Ember Cairn

The holy city of the Arcanist Inquisition, built high in the Shattered Peaks in the ancestral ruins of the dragonborn. Corpses rot in gibbets over market squares; skulls are piled into ashen cairns. Pilgrims crowd the steps of the Cathedral of Embers begging for protection or leniency as if addressing Empyreus himself. The city glows red at night from brazier-lit white granite — visible from Therpena's hills like a burning eye.

### Notable Locations

- **Cathedral of Embers**: Fortress-temple atop the western peak. White domes above, vast dungeon-labyrinth below. Inquisitors are blessed and dispatched on crusades from here. The public has no idea how many mages rot in the cells beneath.
- **Cathedral Catacombs / Hidden Labyrinth**: The catacombs spread further than the Inquisition claims to have built. Ancient dragonborn tunnels run beneath. Monsters, mad arcanists, and artifacts of unknown origin lurk in the deep levels. The black market sells access; it is also sold as a path to rescue condemned mages.
- **Empyrean Pit**: Fighting arena where exhausted, tortured arcanists are herded in to be publicly executed by Inquisitor knights while crowds chant hymns. Gambling is illegal and thriving.
- **Marketplace**: Loud, crowded, and thick with smugglers. The most lucrative illicit trade is in people and information about Inquisition movements.

## Key Individuals

### Lord Inquisitor Crevax
Golden dragonborn warrior-saint who serves as Warden of the Cathedral of Embers. Her flaming longsword Heretic's End is famous for its body count. Aged but still formidable, she no longer crusades — she guards the city and passes judgment on the most dangerous apostates brought before her. She dreams of expanding the Eternal Dogma across all of Etharis. Crowds gather in hundreds to witness her executions.

### Hedric, the High Redeemer
Crevax's second, a grizzled dragonborn whose curved horns make him look more daemon than seraph — which helps with confessions. Commands crusading legions outside Ember Cairn. Relentless and ruthless: he would rather raze a settlement than let one mage escape. The provinces breathe easier when his crusades take him far from home. His legend is beginning to eclipse Crevax's.

### The Bleeding Man
An urban myth with real bodies: an arcanist (or an enclave of them) who murders Inquisitors in Ember Cairn, draining them of blood and scrawling crimson manifestos on the walls. The Inquisition has publicly executed him twice; the messages keep reappearing. His sangromantic spell-scrolls now circulate in the black market, distributed by a cult called the Whisperers. No one knows his true identity, or whether he is mortal or something worse.

### Arieda Starwin (exiled)
An elf-blood soldier who hid her arcane gifts for years before accidentally using magic to save a nobleman's son from a werewolf. Hedric spared her — hearing a voice he took for the Seraphs — and exiled her. Now traveling Etharis, growing in power, and becoming a Seraph-like figure herself. She is preparing to return to Castinella and destroy the Inquisition. Potential major ally or disruptive force in any campaign set here.

### Sebastián, the Goat Shepherd
A humble shepherd who leads his flock through wildfire-prone areas of the Shattered Peaks, miraculously never caught in a blaze. Shows up at burned villages with quiet comfort. Speaks against a church that gilds itself while commoners burn. Rumored to be the Arch Seraph Morael in disguise. The Inquisition has taken an interest in meeting him.

## Key Factions

- **Arcanist Inquisition**: Theocratic military that controls all three provinces. Fanatics at the top, true believers and terrified pragmatists filling the ranks.
- **Castibona Family (Toletum)**: The most ideologically anti-Inquisition crime syndicate. More of their members are arcanists than any other. They spirit mages out of Castinella for a steep price — and are the Inquisition's most doggedly pursued enemies.
- **The Whisperers**: Underground cult distributing the Bleeding Man's sangromantic writings. Know multiple hidden entrances to the labyrinth. Deeply paranoid; responsible for the deaths of several Inquisitors who tried to infiltrate them.
