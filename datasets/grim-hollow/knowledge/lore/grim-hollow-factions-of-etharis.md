# Factions of Etharis

The major factions of Etharis permeate every stratum of society, driven by one overarching goal: power -- whether through magic, knowledge, influence, or fear. Each faction can serve as patron or adversary; all have the capability to propagate great evil in the name of doing good.

## Augustine Trading Company

**Identity**: A global corporation headquartered in Morencia, nominally a bastion of free trade and education. In truth, a ruthless enterprise with a draconic thirst for coin. Founded by the disgraced clergyman Manoch Augustus, who built a pyramid of wealth from arms dealing, predatory lending, and real estate.

**Leadership**: Administered by the Montarri, a council of five led by Treasurer Kolyhs "The Blazing Brain" Papinian -- a gnome orphan turned master manipulator who rose through the Banker's Guild and Morencian Supreme Council. Other Montarri: Senior Preceptor Melchora Rosalle (research), Master of Ledgers Enzo Reiff (trade), Lord Barrister Anlon Katzenbach (contracts), Intendant of Compliance Odriana van de Nijveldt (spymaster).

**Operations**: Controls shell companies and governments via proxy ownership, debt-leverage, and economic entrapment. Funds wars, builds cities, collapses markets, and breeds monsters for battlefield use. Maintains elite Augustine Centers (universities) to develop talent -- graduates become indentured servants.

**Secret Projects**: Project Warbreed (lycanthropic super soldiers, crossing thralls with vampires). Facility Null-9 under frozen Grarjord. Project Fumarialis (magical and black powder weapons), tested at Ashhold Alpha in Corova's mountains. Project Mandragora (performance-enhancing potions and addictive drugs) at the Unlisted Garden in Charneault forests.

**Key Location**: The August Spire in Morencia -- a fortress-palazzo housing a portal of twisted flesh and enchanted iron for dispatching couriers across Etharis. Ruinously expensive to activate, requiring vampire blood, fiend flesh, and rare magical essences.

**Tensions**: The Arcanist Inquisition and Watchers despise the Company but need its goods. The Ebon Syndicate sees it as both competitor and kindred spirit. The Thaumaturge finds it useful for reagents. The Free Swords maintain coldly transactional relationships.

**Market Adaptation**: Tailors operations to local customs. Bribes governments, emphasizes different goods depending on regional laws. Always finds someone desperate enough to buy.


## Company of Free Swords

**Identity**: The pre-eminent mercenary organization on the continent, with branches in most major cities. Takes all manner of contracts -- monster hunting, village defense, full-scale war. Maintains a code of honor: stay loyal, be honest, take vengeance on betrayers. Motto: "Blood and Honor, Strength in Steel."

**History**: Founded nearly two centuries ago when Commander Darion Harkin refused orders to slaughter a village during a rebellion in Falaris province. After mass desertion and assassinating the tyrant King Vaclav II, Harkin negotiated to purchase the tyrant's keep and founded the Company. Famous early victory at the Battle of Kovich Crag established their reputation. The legend of the Silent Seven -- who killed a daemon lord at Kravburg but never spoke of it beyond "We killed the daemon. We saved the people" -- remains their greatest story.

**Leadership**: Led by Yrsa Bitterwind, a towering Valikan woman who won command by decapitating the previous leader in single combat. Her right-hand is Chandra Veln, an elf sorcerer and spymaster. Yrsa's temper and personal ambition (eventual return to Valika to claim leadership of Clan Volgr) concern some members.

**Structure**: Organized into independent local chapters with minimal hierarchy. Ranks: Recruit, Free Sword, Veteran/Specialist, Captain. All same-rank members share equal coin. Equipment is personal responsibility; the company provides only basics and the distinctive red and gold "Double Lion" tabard.

**Key Locations**: Harkin's Hold (founding keep, now a pilgrimage site). Lowengard Fortress in Burach (main fortress, houses 1000+). Lions Dens in major cities (urban guild halls), the most impressive in Voros, Castinella.

**Tensions**: Rumored splinter group "The Red Swords" who take contracts regardless of morality. In Castinella, the Inquisition restricts mages in chapters. Individual branches sometimes stray from the code, causing philosophical debates about duty versus contract honor.


## Ebon Syndicate

**Identity**: The continent's dominant criminal organization, operating from the shadows in Etharis's biggest cities. Controls petty crime, drug smuggling, extortion, assassination, and spy networks. Symbol: the hawk moth, adopted from founder Hiram Greyman. Current leader takes the title "Greyman."

**History**: Founded by Hiram Greyman, a bandit who escaped capture in Altenheim's hedge maze and saw a hawk moth as divine favor. Expanded aggressively; the bloody conflict with the Augustine Trading Company in Morencia ended after a mysterious incident during Castinella's failed invasion -- the Red Gondola, a blood-soaked ship left near the Augustine leader's home with black roses shaped as a hawk moth. Since then, the syndicate gained acceptance in Morencia.

**Leadership**: Current Greyman is Natalia Koscheii, of Ostoyan minor nobility -- famous for surviving assassination attempts. Ancestry traces to Soman founders of the Order of Dawn; mother was a smuggler, father a thief. She values loyalty above all and has an uncanny ability to infiltrate local arms to uncover disloyalty. Other notable leaders: Darion Aufir (debonair ogresh Greyman of Grabenstein), Kallista de Locra (brutal young Grayman of Morencia, suspected of assassination attempts against Natalia).

**Structure**: Local gangs report to bosses, who report to regional Lieutenants ("Grayman"), who report to Natalia. The Shadow Academy trains recruits through hands-on mentorship: "Every lesson is an action; every action is a test." Recruits who fail repeatedly disappear.

**Key Location**: The Grey City -- an underground fortress beneath Altenheim's Ebon Halls, a maze of tunnels and deadly traps. Includes extensive tunnel networks connecting to all areas of the city, sewers, and the river.

**Goals**: Money as a tool (not an end), shadow control of political processes, and ensuring no authority grows large enough to threaten operations. The Syndicate's lack of allegiance makes them paradoxically accessible -- they right wrongs for a price, but a hand that rights a wrong can also wrong a right.

**Regional Presence**: Prominent in Altenheim and across Burach. In Ostoya, stronger in Raevo (Crimson Court has own spies). In Castinella, runs mage smuggling both ways -- helping mages escape and hunting them for the Inquisition. Finds Charneault most difficult, but the chaos of the dark mist creates opportunities.


## Thaumaturge

**Identity**: The Grand Thaumatic Order of Archmages -- a secret society of archmages whose existence has never been conclusively proven. Every great magical event in Etharis, whether calamitous or benevolent, has been attributed to or blamed on them.

**Operations**: Believed to pursue knowledge of the cosmos, collect artifacts and tomes, recruit talented mages (knowingly or not), and conduct grand-scale experiments. Their manipulation is subtle and invisible, spanning decades or centuries. They believe non-action can be as powerful as interference. Every life is a flickering candle -- methods may appear heartlessly cruel but often serve to prevent greater calamity.

**Suspected Members**: Raakan el-Adakar (body never recovered from Cold Iron Keep). The Ravencourt Keeper (unknown identity, may recruit best students). Martorius (mysterious mage whose castle appeared magically atop a Burach mountain). Mina Taliesin, "Lady of Masks" (archmage ruler of northern Ostoya). Professor Franciszka Welgirson (disembodied tutor to the Burach Emperor). Odis (elf from Sarmar Academy traveling Valika studying Gormadraug). Volarius of Aloran (knight-sorcerer on Charneault's Royal Council). Some suspect Pharazorthok, an Aether Kindred, controls senior members.

**Recent Activity**: Keen interest in the Sacred Artifacts. Strange arcane traces reported continent-wide; increased encounters with aetheric aberrations suggest members may use dangerous aether planes for travel.

**Key Locations**: No known central stronghold. Suspected meeting places include the Ravencourt Sanctuary, Erlefurt's Magister College, Sarmar Academy, Castle Martorius, and Cold Iron Keep.

**Agents**: True members are immensely powerful mages. Initiated mages are skilled in deception, illusions, and arcane trickery. Full archmages can manipulate magic, teleport, summon devastating rifts, and combine energies for grand feats.


## Watchers of the Faithful

**Identity**: A young religious faction utterly devoted to the Eternal Dogma of Sagrado Valieda: revere the Arch Seraphs, follow sacred laws, destroy all that is unholy. Born in the fires of the Gods' End. Unlike the militant Arcanist Inquisition, the Watchers fight with fiery words -- preaching and persuasion to reshape the world.

**History**: After Sagrado Valieda unified Castinella, he asked the High Priests of each Arch Seraph to form the Watchers. The Gods' End legitimized their control and spurred outreach to other nations. Their evangelists preach directly to the people, denounce corrupt clergy, and condemn exploitation of the poor. Thousands flock to them on pilgrimages.

**Leadership**: Six sects, each following an Arch Seraph, each with a High Priest who collectively elect a Divine Speaker. Current Speaker: Nola Cirdanal (Empyreus) -- strong, uncompromising Castinellan who deploys masses of evangelists regardless of rulers' wishes. Other High Priests: Audres D'Xeo (Aphaeleon, favors tranquility), Carma Aruejo (Morael, unpredictable vote), Lawirsen Kheshev (Zabriel, controls spy/librarian network), Srokkush (Solyma, dragonborn, wants even more militancy), Velten Giessers (Miklas, heals the sick, natural rival to the Speaker).

**Goals**: Convert as many as possible. Enforce sacred laws. Hunt aberrations, daemons, fey, undead, and heretics. Secret fourth goal: theocratic supremacy -- subjugation of world governments through organized faith.

**Key Location**: Grand Temple of Toletum -- starburst cathedral with chapels to each Arch Seraph. Secret underground levels house a scriptorium, library, holy relics, and passages for covert movement. Zabriel's sect alone has a true relic: the Tome of Saint Lysimachus. Wandering evangelists travel with capillarante -- wagons that unfold into shrines, carrying banners, food, coin, and cudgels.

**Relations**: Tentative alliances with Hearthkeepers (Burach) and Chapter Knights (Charneault). Send missionaries to convert Valikans. Detest the Crimson Court. Complicated relationship with the Arcanist Inquisition -- each tries to use the other. The Watchers regularly induce the Inquisition to eliminate obstacles by passing along reports of "heretics."


## Arcanist Inquisition

**Identity**: The militant religious order at the heart of the Castinellan Provinces, founded by Montego "Sagrado" Valieda after communing with Empyreus, who declared arcane magic a curse. Inquisitors identify, arrest, and increasingly imprison those with magical gifts.

**History**: Initially non-violent -- the arrested were confined in humble conditions and dedicated to seraphic service. Over time, treatment shifted to cruel imprisonment, especially under current Lord Inquisitor Crevax, an imposing seven-foot dragonborn. The Inquisition sealed dragonborn support by offering them dominion over Ember Cairn.

**Headquarters**: The Ember Cathedral in Ember Cairn -- a massive hollowed-out rock formation with an impressive facade and vast underground complex of quarters, prison cells, and labyrinthine paths only Inquisitors can navigate.

**Tensions**: Some within believe the Inquisition is losing its way, but fear speaking up. Agents proudly display their emblem; the mere mention of the Inquisition strikes fear. They work on propaganda as much as deeds.


## Crimson Court

**Identity**: The ruling body of Ostoya, consisting of seven powerful vampire nobles. Governs through fear and command of vast resources, demanding complete obedience in exchange for "protection." Formed after the Darkfall -- the vampiric curse proved crucial in turning the tide of the Second Burach-Ostoyan War.

**Leadership**: Ruled by Grand Duke Drago Koshevek, the most ancient and powerful among them. Obsessed with defeating the Burach Empire. Each of the six other members rules a province with relative autonomy. Behind closed doors, all engage in subterfuge, manipulation, and assassination for power.

**Operations**: Common folk submit to the blood tax rather than face worse fates. Each member controls lieutenants including spawned minor vampire nobles. The Court employs mortals (some loyal, some fearful, some hoping to become vampires) and deploys lesser undead as terror weapons and disposable soldiers. They despise liches and other non-vampiric undead lords.

**Key Locations**: Each member maintains an estate. The Grand Duke's fortress of Blackharbour in Nov Ostoya hosts rare full Court meetings -- invitations often herald membership changes.


## Monster Hunter Guilds

**Identity**: Independent guilds of specialized monster hunters, originating from shared hunting methodologies. Four main disciplines: carvers (study weaknesses), trappers (containment), occultists (arcane counter-magic), and devourers (consume monster essence -- shunned by others). Most train through apprenticeship.

**Structure**: Smaller guilds rival minor mercenary companies but rarely have guild halls, using castles or remote locations instead. All hunters are trained to work individually and often join adventuring groups for specific hunts. Few in number, accomplished hunters are known by name.

**Notable Leader**: Weaver Faloenea, a Dreamer occultist who leads The Nightshield in Altenheim. Her divination can reach into monsters' dreams. She has refused all requests to probe the Great Beast's nature.


## Morbus Doctore

**Identity**: Plague doctors who appeared after the Weeping Pox in Liesech, blending arcane magic and medical science into treatments of dubious morality. Their arrival means disease is present, with misery not far behind. Easily identified by blood-stained boots, vials-hung satchels, and frightful masks.

**Structure**: Pyramid organization where each level knows only those directly above and below. At base: Adjuncts (local doctors interfacing with public) and Orderlies (hired muscle, apothecaries). Middle: Residents apprenticed to Fellows who pursue their own research with wide discretion. Top: five Trustees, identities unknown, never unmasked. The Attending speaks for them with cold logic: "This subject must die so others might live."

**Reputation**: Members share insights internally but never outside their cabal. Rumors say they revel in death, perform cruel experiments, and may have been responsible for the Weeping Pox's initial spread. Not every member lacks morals, but individuals overstep ethical bounds more frequently than they should.


## Order of Dawn

**Identity**: An Ostoyan militia committed to destroying the undead and liberating Ostoya from the Crimson Court. Based primarily in Raevo, formed by Ostoyans who refused vampire rule. Reveres the Arch Seraph Solyma, Lady of Vengeance.

**Leadership**: General Artemios Dulka, a Soman noble who fled the vampire curse. Driven by the dream of an Ostoya without the Darkfall. The sight of a vampire triggers vengeance-filled fervor in this typically benevolent leader.

**Methods**: Merciless and methodical. Members trained from childhood. Captured undead are studied and dissected. Graves unearthed and corpses burned to prevent rising. High tolerance for collateral damage. Three divisions: Sentinels (direct combat knights), Stalkers (spies and assassins, elite ones blessed by Solyma deal radiant damage), Cleansers (priests handling incorporeal undead and corruption). All members receive tattoos with blessed ink that prevents resurrection.

**Key Locations**: Repurposed forts and ruined churches on Ostoya's fringes, plus shifting safe houses deep in Crimson Court territory.

**Tensions**: Maintains superiority complex even toward Raevan mages who sheltered them. Once someone joins, they receive steadfast loyalty -- but outsiders are met with suspicion and information is closely guarded.


## Prismatic Circle

**Identity**: An ancient Valikan religious order driving the clans of Thrull toward violence, raiding, and sacrifice to keep the Great Prismatic Wyrm Gormadraug asleep. Founded by the elemental druid Kybard, whose visions decreed Gormadraug had not been slain but merely slumbered -- its reawakening would end the world. Core tenet: only constant bloody sacrifice forestalls the apocalypse.

**Leadership**: Grand Druid Hjadana, spiritual leader of the three clans of Thrull, rules unchallenged. Closely allied with Chief Nolgr Magnusson of Clan Volgr, whose warriors serve as the Circle's military might. Sanctum is a living fortress of dense trees on the isle of Holgar at Tyburn.

**Practices**: Druids make regular pilgrimages to Sentinel Woods -- scattered oak groves where they oversee multi-day slaughter of captured livestock and people (criminals, southern captives, the unfortunate). Primal magic enhances suffering. Frequency has increased since coldfire's return, seen as a sign of Gormadraug's awakening.

**Opposition**: The clans of Kandar in Eastern Valika oppose the Circle, escalating conflict from acceptable raiding to warfare. The Cult of the Great Wyrm, led by the druid Grenhildr (former protege of the Grand Druid), actively seeks Gormadraug's return, believing the Primordial will preserve clan culture. Guhjdal the Revenant, a druid killed by the Circle and returned to life with power to cast Resurrection, builds a growing army of those she has saved from sacrifice.
