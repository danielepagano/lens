# Chapter 11: Factions of Etharis

Eleven major factions shape life across Etharis. All pursue power — through wealth, faith, knowledge, violence, or fear. Most can serve as patrons, antagonists, or uneasy allies depending on context. The same faction that hires PCs for a job may later mark them as enemies.

---

## Augustine Trading Company

**Mission:** Profit above all else, by any means necessary.

**Leader:** Kolyhs "The Blazing Brain" Papinian — a gnome archmage who rose from the streets to control the Company's five-member ruling council (the Montarri). Disarmingly charming, ruthlessly calculating. She understands that every system has a door, and every door has a price.

**Methods:** The Company is headquartered in Morencia and operates globally through trading hubs, shell companies, and debt leverage. It funds wars, collapses markets, breeds monstrous super-soldiers (Project Warbreed — lycanthrope-vampire hybrids), creates performance-enhancing and addictive drugs (Project Mandragora), and runs elite universities to cultivate indentured talent. It maintains dossiers on everyone who has ever crossed it.

**PC encounter:** PCs are likely hired as disposable contractors for morally compromised jobs — escort missions with cursed cargo, monster salvage, abductions framed as justice. Payment is generous; the moral cost accrues later.

**Faction relations:** Coldly transactional with the Company of Free Swords. Competitive with the Ebon Syndicate but also does business with them. Despised by the Watchers of the Faithful and Arcanist Inquisition, who nonetheless need its goods.

---

## Company of Free Swords

**Mission:** Provide elite mercenary services for the highest coin, without committing atrocities.

**Leader:** Yrsa Bitterwind — a towering Valikan woman who decapitated the previous leader in a duel and claimed command. Ferocious in battle, politically blunt. Her drive for personal glory sometimes puts the Company at risk.

**Methods:** Branches in most major cities across Etharis. The Company maintains a strict code: loyalty to the Company, honesty in dealings, vengeance on betrayers. They take contracts ranging from village monster hunts to full-scale war. They "collect payment one way or another" — crossing them is inadvisable.

**PC encounter:** PCs may be hired by the Company for sensitive contracts, compete against them on the same job, join as members, or find themselves as targets if they owe a debt.

**Faction relations:** Strictly transactional relationship with the Augustine Trading Company. Sometimes engaged by the Arcanist Inquisition in Castinella with politically complicated terms.

---

## Ebon Syndicate

**Mission:** Control the criminal underworld of Etharis — assassination, theft, drugs, extortion, black market, espionage.

**Leader:** Known only as "the Greyman," a title passed down through leaders who maintain near-total anonymity. The symbol is a hawk moth — nocturnal, drawn to dangerous and shiny things.

**Methods:** Operates primarily in major cities. Lesser organizations either join, pay tribute, or bleed. Maintains an unparalleled spy network that even the Augustine Company and the Watchers quietly use. Prefers shadows and hired freelancers for dangerous work; direct confrontation is a last resort.

**PC encounter:** PCs meet the Syndicate as clients, targets, competitors, or reluctant allies. Getting something done illegally almost always involves the Syndicate eventually.

**Faction relations:** Views the Augustine Company as a competitor and occasional business partner. Despised by the Watchers of the Faithful, though the Watchers have used their spy network.

---

## Thaumaturge

**Mission:** Accumulate arcane knowledge and subtly guide the fate of the world — over decades or centuries.

**Leader:** Unknown. Suspects include Raakan el-Adakar (an archmage believed dead but possibly not), the mysterious Martorius (whose castle appeared magically atop a mountain), and possibly an Aether god named Pharazorthok. May be ruled by a council rather than any individual.

**Methods:** The Order operates through invisible manipulation. It recruits talented mages knowingly or unknowingly. It believes non-action can be as powerful as interference, and that individual lives are candles — precious but expendable against the cosmic scale. Strange arcane traces and increased aetheric aberration sightings have raised suspicions about Thaumaturge experiments with dangerous planes.

**PC encounter:** The Thaumaturge is experienced as mystery: a mage appearing to warn of danger, a dead end after defeating one of its members, a cryptic letter. Confirming that the Thaumaturge is involved is itself a campaign achievement.

**Faction relations:** The Watchers of the Faithful consider the Thaumaturge their most dangerous rival, sharing intelligence with the Arcanist Inquisition to hunt them.

---

## Watchers of the Faithful

**Mission:** Spread worship of the Arch Seraphs and guide the world toward theocratic rule under the Eternal Dogma.

**Leader:** Divine Speaker Nola Cirdanal — a strong, uncompromising Castinellan woman who deploys evangelists aggressively and tolerates no interference from secular rulers.

**Methods:** The Watchers fight with words, sermons, and manipulated mobs rather than swords. Priests, paladins, and clergy spread the Eternal Dogma across all nations. They enrage populations against heretics, issue edicts that incite the Inquisition to violence, and secretly pursue theocratic supremacy. Their traveling capillarante wagons carry food, coin, and cudgels for sparking riots when needed.

**PC encounter:** The Watchers may commission PCs to recover relics, protect missionaries, or investigate heretics. Their missions always carry a religious angle; even benevolent ones tend to produce morally uncomfortable outcomes.

**Faction relations:** Closest allies with the Arcanist Inquisition, whom they manipulate as a "blunt instrument." Hostile to the Crimson Court, Thaumaturge, Ebon Syndicate, and Augustine Trading Company (publicly at least).

---

## Arcanist Inquisition

**Mission:** Identify, capture, and suppress all arcane magic users — viewed as a curse endangering the world.

**Leader:** Lord Inquisitor Crevax — a seven-foot-tall dragonborn, zealous and uncompromising. Under his reign, the Inquisition's treatment of prisoners has shifted from monastic confinement to cruel imprisonment.

**Methods:** Based at the Ember Cathedral in Ember Cairn, the Inquisition patrols Castinella and beyond. Inquisitors wear their emblems openly — their reputation alone stirs fear. They make use of propaganda, arrest on suspicion, and brutal interrogation. They pursue arcane users but also fight monsters and provide healing for those they deem worthy.

**PC encounter:** The Inquisition is an ambient threat in Castinella and nearby regions. Any party with arcane casters will draw scrutiny. They may hire PCs for anti-magic operations or appear as antagonists when PCs protect mages.

**Faction relations:** Closely allied with the Watchers of the Faithful, though the Watchers see the Inquisition as a tool to direct. Hostile to virtually all arcane practitioners including the Thaumaturge.

---

## Crimson Court

**Mission:** Maintain and expand the vampire noble rule of Ostoya while keeping the living population compliant and productive.

**Leader:** Grand Duke Drago Koshevek — the oldest and most powerful vampire in Ostoya, obsessed with defeating the Bürach Empire. Rules through fear; other Court members dare not oppose him openly.

**Methods:** Seven vampire nobles each govern a province, with relative autonomy as long as they serve the Grand Duke's schemes. The Court demands a blood tax from the citizenry. Internally, constant subterfuge, assassination attempts, and political maneuvering characterize Court life. The Court uses lesser undead as disposable soldiers and grotesque displays of power.

**PC encounter:** PCs entering Ostoya will inevitably be drawn into Court politics, either as pawns hired for one noble's scheme against another, or as threats the Court seeks to neutralize. Being "invited" to serve the Court is a trap with no clean exit.

**Faction relations:** Enemies of the Order of Dawn, which seeks their destruction. Wary of the Thaumaturge. The Bürach Empire is their primary external military threat.

---

## Monster Hunter Guilds

**Mission:** Study and kill monsters for pay, and preserve the knowledge needed to do so effectively.

**Leader:** No unified leadership. Notable figure: Weaver Faloenea, a Dreamer who leads the Nightshield guild in Altenheim. A powerful occultist who can reach into monsters' dreams to learn their nature — she has refused all requests to study the Great Beast.

**Methods:** Guilds vary by method (carvers, trappers, occultists, devourers) but all share a commitment to knowledge as the primary weapon. They share contracts, research, and resources. Most hunters work independently or in small teams and travel extensively. Larger guilds rival small mercenary companies.

**PC encounter:** PCs encounter hunters as competitors on the same contract, as sources of specialized knowledge, as potential allies in difficult hunts, or — if the party has a Transformation or monstrous ally — as antagonists who have done their homework.

**Faction relations:** Neutral to most factions; occasionally engaged by the Watchers of the Faithful. Devourers are private and often shunned even by other hunters.

---

## Morbus Doctore

**Mission:** Understand and document disease, injury, and biological life — officially to heal, actually to experiment without limit.

**Leader:** Five anonymous Trustees whose identities are unknown even to most members. The faction communicates through a cell structure where each member knows only one or two levels above and below them.

**Methods:** Members are easy to spot — blood-stained boots, strange coats, vials, plague masks. They treat the sick of Liesech (where the Weeping Pox originated) and other major cities. Their treatments combine alchemy and arcane medicine, frequently with dangerous side effects. Accusations of deliberately spreading disease to test cures are... not baseless.

**PC encounter:** PCs may need a cure, a treatment, or something only the Doctore's knowledge can provide. The price is always something uncomfortable. Members who have doubts about the faction's ethics make compelling NPC allies and cautionary figures.

**Faction relations:** Largely independent of other factions. Their secrecy makes alliances rare and suspicion universal.

---

## Order of Dawn

**Mission:** Destroy all undead, liberate Ostoya from the Crimson Court, and serve Solyma, Lady of Vengeance.

**Leader:** General Artemios Dulka — a Soman noble consumed by the dream of an Ostoya free from vampires. Typically benevolent, but the sight of a vampire triggers uncontrollable fury. The Order's methods are merciless: no quarter for the undead, high tolerance for collateral damage.

**Methods:** Based in Raevo, the Order trains members from childhood to fight undead. Captured undead are dissected for tactical knowledge. Members are tattooed with blessed inks that prevent resurrection. They operate safehouses inside Crimson Court territory and systematically cleanse one region at a time before pushing further.

**PC encounter:** The Order is slow to trust outsiders, but when pragmatism wins, they hire mercenaries for dangerous operations they cannot staff. PCs with anti-undead capabilities and respect for Ostoyan tradition earn favor fastest.

**Faction relations:** Enemies of the Crimson Court. Uneasy with Raevan mages despite being based in Raevo. No meaningful alliances with other major factions.

---

## Prismatic Circle

**Mission:** Prevent the reawakening of Gormadraug, the Prismatic Wyrm, through continuous blood sacrifice and warfare.

**Leader:** Grand Druid Hjadana — spiritual leader of three Valikan clans in Thrull, ruling without challenge for two decades. Allied closely with Chief Nolgr Magnusson of Clan Völgr.

**Methods:** An ancient druidic order that predates Etharis's modern nations. The Circle drives the clans of Thrull to raid, fight, and sacrifice captives in the Sentinel Woods — sacred oak groves where multi-day slaughter rituals are performed. The frequency of these rites has increased since coldfire returned, which the Circle reads as a sign Gormadraug stirs. Opposition within Thrull is rare; speaking against the Circle risks being sent to the Sentinel Woods as the next sacrifice.

**PC encounter:** PCs in Valika encounter the Circle as oppressive theocrats, as the force behind raider attacks, or as the faction standing between the world and something far worse — if their theology is correct.

**Faction relations:** Opposed by the eastern Kandar clans of Valika. The Circle of the Great Wyrm (a splinter heresy) seeks Gormadraug's return rather than prevention. The Watchers of the Faithful send missionaries to convert Valikans away from Primordial worship, putting them at odds with the Circle.
