**Chapter 7: The City of Liesech**

## Setting Tone

Liesech is a city that has accepted its own death and built a pleasant smile over the wound. The Weeping Pox — a supernatural plague of body horror — has been endemic here for generations. Citizens greet visitors warmly, then drift mid-sentence, their eyes sliding toward the bay. They call the pox a necessity. They insist it prevents overpopulation. Behind every friendly smile is abject terror so old it has become indistinguishable from normalcy.

The city is ruled by a vampire who is herself a victim of the same cosmic deal that created the plague. The doctors who arrived to help are conducting experiments behind iron walls. The god-creature under the water is watching everything.

Tone note: Liesechers are not cruel — they are traumatized past the point of reaction. GMs should play them as warm, slightly vacant, unfazed by death, and quietly relieved when outsiders leave before the pox takes them.

## Geography and Atmosphere

Liesech festers inside the Bite Bay, a city-state north of the Charneault Kingdom. A permanent green haze hangs over it — ostensibly from bay vapors, actually from the Morbus Doctore's chimney stacks. On clear nights the emerald glow is visible for miles. The surrounding waters are treacherous: the **Eye of the Sea** at the bay's center has claimed ships for generations. Access is primarily by boat; even then, captains must navigate turbulent currents and sea stacks.

## History (Condensed)

**Otto Bouwengracht** founded Liesech's prosperity by making a deal with an eldritch creature — the Filth Grazer — which offered knowledge and wealth in exchange for the city's dead as offerings. Liesech flourished. When the Bürach Empire tried to annex them, they dispatched **Countess Analita von Raiza**, an ancient vampire, to compel the Bouwengracht family. Von Raiza fulfilled her mission, then killed the Bouwengracht family to seize power for herself.

This broke the original pact. The Filth Grazer attacked with tidal monsters. Von Raiza fought it to a standstill, then was tricked into a new blood contract — the most binding oath a vampire can make. As part of the pact's "penance," the Filth Grazer cursed the animated dead von Raiza had used in the battle, seeding the **Weeping Pox**: a plague that spreads through the city, turns flesh bone-white, raises red pustules, and ends with the victim weeping all their blood from their body in one final wail. Every pox carrier is a thrall extending the Filth Grazer's telepathic reach across Etharis.

Von Raiza is herself disfigured by the pox — her punishment for breaking the original deal. She cannot refuse the Filth Grazer's terms without violating her blood oath. She continues to honor the Nurturing Sea Ceremony (offering the city's dead to the creature) while publicly pretending to seek a cure.

## Districts

- **Hauthoff-Liesech (Main Town)**: Dark stone buildings, cobblestone streets. Bell posts at every corner — rung only when someone contracts the pox or dies. The Inquisition-style Golden Barge Company controls transport. Two structures are gated from the rest of the city: the High Belfry and Zielenhaus.
- **Traughafen (Dock Town)**: Liesech's mercantile heart. The Golden Barge Company runs the docks. Sanitized trade rooms (Handelzimmer buildings) let visiting merchants conduct business without directly exposing themselves to pox-carriers in the streets.

## Key Individuals

### Countess Analita Von Raiza
One of the oldest known vampires, disfigured by the Weeping Pox, ruling Liesech from the High Belfry as interim leader since the Bouwengracht deaths. Her power is real but constrained — she cannot move against the Filth Grazer's interests without violating her blood oath, so she manages the city, performs the ceremonies, and maintains the pretense of seeking a cure. Her vampiric Cult of the Black Lady serves her by day and night. She still cries out in her sleep about something in the sea.

### The Filth Grazer
An eldritch entity under the Eye of the Sea — a miasmic mass of eyes, mouths, and tentacles that speaks in soothing alien voices. It feeds on all forms of waste (physical and metaphysical), incorporating it into its intellect. Its waste carries a fragment of its sentience; this flows through sea water, rises as haze, clings to life. Every pox victim is its thrall. It has seduced two rulers of Liesech with the same offer: knowledge and prosperity for a price. It does not appear to be defeatable by conventional means.

### The Morbus Doctore
An organization of masked physicians (long dark coats, bird-beak masks) who arrived to treat the pox and have become a permanent institution. They run **Zielenhaus**, an iron-walled compound spanning four city blocks — the source of the green haze. They do reduce suffering and are genuinely beloved by most citizens, who see them as merciful. Behind the walls: painful experiments, laboratories, vaults of instruments. Children gather at the gates; some go in and are not seen again. The Cult of the Black Lady distrusts them deeply.

## Key Factions

- **Cult of the Black Lady**: Von Raiza's vampiric inner circle. Day-and-night protective detail. They hunt untainted blood for the countess and quietly remove opposition. They fear what she says in her sleep.
- **Golden Barge Company**: City transport and enforcement. Founded by Bouwengracht loyalists who refused to participate in the pox ceremonies. Pragmatic, commercially ruthless — they employ assassins "for the greater good." They control the docks and most of the city's movement infrastructure.
- **Morbus Doctore**: See above. Their true agenda remains opaque.

## Notable Locations

- **Eye of the Sea**: The dark turbulent water at the bay's center. The Filth Grazer's physical form resides here. Only Countess Von Raiza knows this with certainty.
- **The High Belfry**: The Bouwengracht family manse, now Von Raiza's seat of power. Six morning bells start the city's day; six evening bells close it. The Countess receives guests (at night only) during the Grand Market weeks. Her apartments are not open to the public.
- **Zielenhaus**: The Morbus Doctore's iron-walled compound. Source of the green haze. Children congregate outside its gates. The Morbus Plinth inside is a factory-like structure with a central dispersal chamber that pumps experimental waste into the sky.
