**Chapter 6: The Valikan Clans**

## Setting Tone

Valika is the frozen north of Etharis — a land of brutal survival, clan glory, and apocalyptic dread. Its people live in the shadow of a sleeping monster (the Great Prismatic Wyrm Gormadraug) and are manipulated by druids who claim only blood and raiding can keep it dormant. The six major clans range from cosmopolitan diplomats (Clan Mithra) to savage reavers (Clan Völgr), but all share a culture where strength earns authority, sagas shape identity, and hardship is a matter of simple fact. The supernatural winter horror — coldfire, an expanding glacial phenomenon that consumes the land — looms as an existential threat that most factions are too divided to address.

Tone note: Valikans speak plainly, value deeds over words, and treat southerners with either curiosity or contempt depending on context. Clan loyalty is near-sacred. The Prismatic Circle's manipulation is the hidden rot in an otherwise proud culture.

## Geography

Valika spans the far north of Etharis: fjord-cut coastlines, open frozen tundra, ice-covered mountain ranges, and deep pine forests near the Bürach Empire border. The Teeth — a series of long fjord-cut peninsulas — give the north its fearsome silhouette from the sea. **Coldfire** sweeps down from the volcanic island of Volgen in the northeast, a supernatural glacial fire that consumes living things and turns the land to barren wasteland. An ice bridge now connects Volgen to the mainland. The spread of coldfire is the region's slow apocalypse.

## History and Defining Events

- **The Great Prismatic Wyrm**: The founding myth. Seven heroes — Kentigern, Rune, Sýr, Völgr, Limgri, Morgöng, and Mithra — defeated the elemental serpent Gormadraug by being swallowed and destroying him from within. Many Valikans believe the wyrm merely sleeps and will rise again.
- **Thorgard's Saga**: A legendary orc hero of Clan Völgr who united the clans against southern invaders, then sailed west and vanished. His saga is the model of what a Valikan hero should be: cunning, fearless, ultimately consumed by their own ambition.
- **The Prismatic Circle's founding**: Druid Kybard claimed prophetic visions that Gormadraug sleeps — and only blood sacrifice at the Sentinel Woods can keep him dormant. This lie has driven centuries of raiding and slaughter, with the druids controlling the most violent clans through manufactured apocalyptic fear.

## The Two Valikas

**Thrull** (west): Dominated by Clans Völgr, Rune, and Sýr. Controlled in practice by the Prismatic Circle. Raiding culture is not just tradition — it is religious obligation. The Sentinel Woods sacrificial sites stand in impossible oak groves throughout the tundra, their soil black with generations of blood.

**Kandar** (east): Clans Mithra, Morgöng, and Limgri. Diplomatically ambitious, ethnically diverse, fighting to break the stereotype that all Valikans are raiders. Queen Andrea Helsing leads their alliance and maintains standing armies against constant Thrull incursions. Coldfire threatens Kandar's northeastern reaches most acutely.

## Key Individuals

### Grand Druid Hjadana (Thrull / Prismatic Circle)
Fanatical leader of the Prismatic Circle, operating from Tyburn on Holgar Island. Drives the clan of Thrull to perpetual bloodlust through the claim that Gormadraug must be fed blood and magic to stay asleep. Closely allied — possibly romantically — with Chief Nolgr Magnusson of Clan Völgr. Her control is near-absolute in western Valika and depends entirely on the clans never questioning Kybard's visions.

### Chief Nolgr Magnusson (Clan Völgr)
Warrior-chief who has led Clan Völgr and the three Thrull clans for nearly thirty years. Personally leads raids. As Keeper of the Three Tribes (a title bestowed by the Prismatic Circle), holds ceremonial authority over Clans Rune and Sýr. Magnetic, charismatic, and utterly dependent on the Prismatic Circle's ideology for his legitimacy.

### Grenhildr (Cult of the Great Wyrm)
Hjadana's former protégé who, after surviving a blizzard, returned claiming Gormadraug himself spoke to her — saying the wyrm's power is fading, not a threat, and that the sacrifices are wrong. Her Cult of the Great Wyrm is spreading across Valika, terrifying Hjadana. She represents a potential fracture in the Prismatic Circle's hold over the clans.

### Queen Andrea Helsing (Clan Mithra / Kandar)
Elected war-chief of the Kandar clans. Grayed by worry, scarred by battle, and pragmatic to the point of moral ambiguity — she uses adventurers for deniable operations, plays politics with an iron hand, and will compromise honor before she compromises survival. Her goal: keep Kandar free from the Prismatic Circle and negotiate alliances with the south before coldfire swallows them all.

### Commander Bodil Garðr (Cold Iron Keep)
Exiled skald-turned-warden, a Røkkva (doomsday prophet) who genuinely wants to hasten the end of the world. Charismatic, calculating, and surrounded by scholars, adventurers, and scoundrels who have earned her dangerous favor. Runs Cold Iron Keep as a combination of prison, court, and delver's base. Her key project: Geas tattoos that let her control select prisoners as obedient agents.

## Cold Iron Keep

A fortress raised from living rock by the wicked mage Mordu, now Kandar's maximum-security prison for enemies too dangerous for ordinary clan law. Located in the remote Jakkan Mountains, accessible only by crossing Lake Jakkan (a natural moat with something monstrous beneath it). Clan Limgri operates it under Queen Andrea's arrangement.

The Keep has three layers: **Guest Halls** (ground floor, teeming with scholars, adventurers, and merchants drawn to Mordu's surviving magic), **the Cells** (prison, with laboratories for experimenting on uncooperative inmates), and **the Deepvaults** (unmapped, dangerous, partially flooded, containing Mordu's deepest secrets and worse things). Coldfire drifts through the upper Deepvault corridors called the Creep.

**Notable prisoners**: Astar the medusa (iron-masked, kept in a mirrored cell, her whispered counsel is as dangerous as her gaze); Jormund the Dreamer (a frost giant vampire in enchanted torpor, whose nightmares can be scried); Gorm, Heir of the Wyrm (a coldfire-burning madman Garðr tattooed with Geas marks and sends into the depths).

## Key Factions

- **Prismatic Circle**: Druidic order manipulating the clans of Thrull toward perpetual raiding and sacrifice. Led by Hjadana from Tyburn. Their Sentinel Woods ritual sites are places of genuine horror.
- **Cult of the Great Wyrm**: Splinter movement led by Grenhildr, challenging the Circle's foundational claim. Growing and dangerous to the established order.
- **Order of Kentigern**: Contemplative monks-turned-soldiers at Fort Kentigern, holding back the coldfire advance. Accepts anyone who can fight. Led by the weary Abbot Sanrun, who knows the border war and the coldfire crisis need to be faced together — and is ignored.
- **The Redclaws**: Shapeshifter druids led by the werebear Thrulldr, tracing lineage to the hero Kentigern. Offer an alternative path for those who embrace Valika's harsh nature rather than fighting it.
