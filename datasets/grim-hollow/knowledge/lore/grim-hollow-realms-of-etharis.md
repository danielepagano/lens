# Realms of Etharis

## The Realms of Etharis

Etharis is an expansive continent occupied by empires and ancient kingdoms. Cultures are diverse and deep rooted. Each nation has been shaped by its people, history, power groups, relationship with magic, cataclysmic events, and neighboring sovereigns.

No realm is singular in its culture or values. Each represents a different genre of dark fantasy and horror, and therefore, different themes are best explored there. A single region could accommodate an entire campaign, or you can experience them all through your adventures by traveling across the continent.

The history, culture, and themes of each region are explored deeply in this book. Below, small snapshots of these realms are provided. There is no correct order to experience them, except that you should start wherever your inspiration takes you.

### The Bürach Empire

Once a beacon of civilization that extended its territory into every realm, the Bürach Empire crumbles and rots, now clinging to its heartland provinces like an old lion ravaged by a lifetime of wounds and illness. Though still formidable in wealth and might, those within the empire live each day sensing that the end is approaching.

Stories of cosmic horror sit perfectly in the Bürach Empire, where the hopes of a better tomorrow are overshadowed by an impending and unavoidable doom. A shadow stalks the Bürach Empire, a creature of colossal size whose mere presence twists and corrupts the land around it. This beast has been seen by many yet never truly seen, for it is as much an archetype as it is a mortal being. But the horrors that spring up in its wake cannot be denied.

The provinces that comprise the remains of the Bürach Empire see the impending collapse of its leadership and society, and their leaders position themselves to benefit from that collapse—and perhaps even look to hasten it. Whether that end comes by war, rebellion, disease, or cults of strange gods, the empire may be witnessing its final days.

The dark fantasy themes of cosmic horror, gothic horror, and existential dread work well in a campaign set in the Bürach Empire. The corruption of the political and social spheres of Bürach are mirrored through the corruptive and mutative magic. The imperialism and colonialism that made Bürach strong have led to a soul-stealing disregard for others and their struggles. The “might makes right” attitudes of its people are now being torturously reversed, as the might slips away to be replaced by doubt and a proliferation of the might of monsters.

### The Ostoyan Empire

Ostoya is a sunless realm of shadows and blood. The hearts of the folk who live in the Ostoyan Empire are as hard and cold as the land they live upon, forced to toil beneath a black sky and a callous nobility that wants to drain everything from its peasantry, including its blood.

Ostoya is ruled by a coterie of seven vampires named the Crimson Court, who both enjoy the perks of their power in gaudy displays, while also fretting moment by moment over the plots against them, both by hunters of vampires and by each other.

The gothic horror of vampires, werewolves, zombies, undead, ghosts, and ghouls pairs well with the themes of autocracy, civil unrest, resistance, rebellion, and personal safety. Stories and adventures in Ostoya can examine how much normal people are willing to endure politically and socially if their leaders promise to keep them safe from more local and realized threats. But when those same leaders also cause the threats, what are people willing to risk to fight for their freedom?

### The Charneault Kingdom

The Charneault Kingdom is a realm of beauty that far exceeds any other. Hidden behind the mystical forest of the Grove Maze is a land of rolling emerald hills, mist-veiled glens, and countless flowing rivers. Charneault is patrolled by knights of virtue. A deep connection to nature has been entwined into Charnelaise culture from the elves who first settled these idyllic lands.

Yet all this beauty is merely an illusion. Stories told in the Charneault Kingdom are similar to children’s fairytales—whimsical narratives that disguise the true brutality and danger at their core. The fairytales at the heart of Charneault are much darker and grimmer.

Capricious fey, worshipped on the one hand and feared on the other, take what they want with no fear of retribution. A corrupting mist veils their activities and decimates everything it touches with no end in sight. The tales and adventures of Charneault are those of nightmares—the supernatural horror of amoral fey creatures performing indescribable acts of cruelty and pain on mortal creatures just to see what will happen.

### The Castinellan Provinces

Faith unifies the Castinellan Provinces. Fervently devoted to the edicts of the Arch Seraphs, the people of Castinella recognize the evils that can be performed through acts of arcane magic—and they do everything in their power to stop those acts from happening. If a few innocent or good-hearted people are killed or tortured in the process, it is considered a small price to pay in the name of safety.

Castinella is a realm where religion has transformed the populace from the best of intentions into monsters in their own right. The ignorance, misinformation, and blind devotion of the Arcanist Inquisition and its followers set up campaigns of psychological horror, where it only takes one wayward accusation to turn an otherwise innocent person’s life into a nightmare. And the presence of actual monsters that need to be fought means choosing between utilizing the magic that kills monsters, or hiding the magic that will get you killed as one.

### The Valikan Clans

Far to the north, at the frozen edge of the continent, lies the homeland of the Valikan Clans. Each day, the hardy folk who dwell in Valika must endure the unforgiving environment, rampaging elementals, and the blood-fueled conflicts that erupt between the various clans.

Life is short among the Great Clans. Yet the legacies of great heroes and clan chiefs live across generations through the sagas that the Valikans tell of their exalted history. Like the diverse seasons themselves, the honored and skilled bravery of Valikans is one side of a coin. The other, like the harsh winter, is about survival at any cost, including performing terrible and selfish acts to make sure you and your clan survive. Those acts include raiding settlements and taking captives—some of which may be sacrificed.

The tales of Valika include survival and supernatural horror. The arctic climate and magical coldfire make both the natural and the supernatural horror meld into one grim reality for those hoping to live or adventure there.

### Liesech and Morencia

The two city-states of Liesech and Morencia are not full nations, but they are nonetheless thematically, geographically, politically, and socially distinct from the other areas. Each brings its own flavor and own sense of horror to a campaign.

Liesech is the putrid cauldron of disease where the Weeping Pox originated. Its people attempt to carry on their mercantile ways of life as their friends, family, peers, and even enemies die from the dreaded disease. Although the disease has a supernatural cause, its effects are nonetheless clear to see. Body horror and the corruption involved in that genre come not just through the disease itself, but from the sometimes questionable actions of those who attempt to cure it.

Morencia’s disease is social rather than medical. The greedy and conniving residents of this center of banking and commerce smile and extend one hand while the other clutches a poisoned dagger. The vibrant and effusive façade of Morencia hides dark secrets and secret cults that would do anything for an ounce of power and one more gold coin.

## History of Etharis

The complete histories, timelines, and myths of Etharis are meant to be illustrative rather than proscriptive. Each nation and people have their own sense of history and lore, much of which might be contradictory, as everyone tries to make themselves look like the hero or the victim, depending on the circumstances. Not every event is fully explored or every character detailed—such an undertaking would be impossible to contain within a single volume. There are many mysteries yet to be revealed.

While future volumes may delve even further into the recesses of Etharis, for now, they’re left open for you to fill as the master of this game world. To use this book is to make Etharis your own. The ingredients of a fully realized world are provided to you within these pages to inspire your own tales, centered on characters who will change the history of the continent the moment they set foot upon it. Embrace this power. Grim Hollow and the world of Etharis belong to you now, and those you choose to share it with, to tell your own stories of darkness and hope.

The histories of Etharis have been written in the blood of the fallen by those who remain, honoring their ancestors through recorded and oral traditions. Yet Etharis is an ancient continent of diverse cultures. Knowledge has been lost or misremembered, great heroes have been glorified beyond their actual deeds, and calamities have erased much of Etharis’s past.

This timeline charts the history of Etharis in rough order of events, though it’s impossible to pinpoint exactly when each moment occurred. The mythologies of different cultures clash. There are no records of whether the dreamers were already asleep when Ember Cairn fell. Therefore, these histories are left open for the scholars of Etharis to reconstruct and debate.

Furthermore, the order of events as we’ve imagined them may not suit the sorts of stories you wish to tell at your table. For example, the lore written in this book suggests the Darkfall is a recent tragedy. However, you may desire to set your campaign in Ostoya during a time after centuries of vampiric rule. Move the Darkfall forward or back on the timeline, or add new entries to the recent past of your Etharis, moving the timeline forward.
As with everything in this campaign guide, the intent is to give you the tools to craft stories in Etharis of your own dark fantasies!

### Era of Antiquity
An era of myth and prehistory, filled with conflicting creation stories. The distance of time is unknown. Many events in this era have no recorded accounts, only the evidence of their occurrence, which has survived to the modern day.

- **The Gods Awaken**: According to Büracher scriptures, the gods are the first beings to awaken and sculpt the mortal world.
- **The Primordic Cycle**: The kin of the Valikan Clans tell of Gormadraug, the Great Prismatic Wyrm, as the first being to exist.
- **Hymn of Viduami**: In Ostoya, a myth is told about a death god, entombed in the depths of the City Below by its kindred deities.
- **Kentigern’s Saga**: After Gormadraug is slain by the hero Kentigern, Valikan sagas say the Great Wyrm’s corpse is used to craft the mortal realm.
- **The Aetheric War**: The Aether Kindred emerge from the void and destroy the gods. Only Aurelia, Galt, Ulmyr, and Maligant survive.
- **The Netherworld**: The heavens are ruptured by the Aetheric War, forming the Netherworld. Arch Seraphs of dead gods fall from grace.
- **Birth of Caer Neiada**: The archfey cultivate Caer Neiada. Some believe them to be fallen seraphs, while the elves of Charneault worship them as deities.
- **Twilight of the Dreamers**: The dreamers fall into a slumber that will last an epoch, escaping an unknown cataclysm.
- **Descent to the City Below**: A civilization rises and falls, swallowed by the earth beneath a realm that would one day be named Ostoya.
- **The Ulufey**: The ancestors of the elves, believing themselves children of archfey, settle Caer Neiada.
- **Fall of Ember Cairn**: Ember Cairn, the ancient home of the dragonborn, is destroyed. Their people scatter across Etharis.
- **Kingdoms of Stone**: The dwarves build their fortress cities in the heartland of Etharis, raising kingdoms of their own.
- **Human Tribes**: Humans wander the Büracher forests, forming into small tribes that grow into kingdoms.
- **Exodus of the Orcs**: The people known as orcs sail from northern Etharis, heading west to distant lands.

### Era of Expansion
Humans spread from west-central Etharis to settle in regions to the north, south, and east. This is an era of bloodshed and mourning. As history unfolds through centuries of human expansion, smaller kingdoms rise and fall until the modern civilizations of Etharis take shape.

- **Four Kingdoms of the Divines**: The Norden, Raulfolk, Unters, and Abendlanders become the dominant human kingdoms in central Etharis.
- **Cold Iron Keep**: The wicked mage, Mordu, raises Cold Iron Keep as his fortress. He’s soon after slain by the Valikans.
- **Thorgard’s Saga**: Valikan hero, Thorgard av Holgar, unites the clans and conquers southwards. He then sails his fleet west in search of new lands and his orcish kin.
- **Writings of Numena**: Raakan el-Adakar’s research on the nature of arcane magic forms the basis for magical theory.
- **Rise of the Prismatic Circle**: The archdruid Kybard has a vision that Gormadraug merely slumbers, forming the Prismatic Circle.
- **Arrival of the First Men**: The earliest human settlers reach Caer Neiada, journeying through the Grove Maze.
- **Wars of Stehlenwald & Grabenstein**: The dwarven city of Grabenstein falls to the Unters, whilst Stehlenwald endures.
- **The Lord Edda**: The Lord Edda is written, compiling Valika’s myths and sagas from earlier centuries.
- **The Bürach Empire**: Emperor Indorius I is ordained by the Hearthkeepers, founding the Bürach Empire.
- **Crossing the Grey Spine**: Büracher migrants cross the Grey Spine, discovering Kaer Vena and new lands in the east.
- **Flickers of Coldfire**: The first sightings of coldfire occur on Volgen, and the beginning of the ice bridge forms.
- **Castinellan Peninsula**: The Bürach Empire extends its reach to the Castinellan peninsula, discovering the shattered kingdom of the dragonborn.
- **Founding of Morencia**: The city of Morencia is founded as a settlement, and it becomes a haven for folk fleeing the Bürach Empire.
- **Pact of the Sacred Land**: Thuridan Sarador crowns Aymeric Noblecoeur, signing the Pact of the Sacred Land and founding the Charneault Kingdom.

### Era of Descent
At the height of its dominance, the Bürach Empire endured two hundred years of cascading tragedies and defeats, which eroded its might and culminated in the Gods’ End. Historians call this period the Era of Descent.

- **First Ostoyan War**: Ostoya secedes from the Bürach Empire, declaring its independence and winning victory at Fallowheart.
- **Unification**: The Unifier, Sagrado Valieda, unites the Castinellan Provinces of Faro, Toletum, and Therpena, founding the Arcanist Inquisition.
- **Company of Free Swords**: Darion Harkin founds the Company of Free Swords in the wake of the Falaris resistance.
- **Augustine Trading Company**: Manoch Augustus leaves the clergy to found the Augustine Trading Company in Morencia
- **Ebon Syndicate**: Hiram Greyman narrowly escapes death by the fortune of a hawk moth and founds the Ebon Syndicate.
- **Ulmyr’s Gate**: The mages of Ulmyr’s Gate attempt to open a permanent rift to the Ethereal Plane, destroying their entire city.
- **The Red Mourning**: The Bürach Empire attempts to annex Liesech through Analita von Raiza, culminating in unleashing the Weeping Pox.
- **Crowning of Emperor Leopold I**: The coronation of Emperor Leopold I leads the Bürach Empire into civil war, and the emperor’s assassination leads to the Gods’ End.

### Era of Beast
In the current dark age of Etharis, humanity clings to survival. Every realm is haunted by the shadow of annihilation through this godless era, marked by the arrival of the Great Beast.

- **The Gods’ End**: The last gods of Etharis--Aurelia, Galt, Ulmyr, and Maligant—destroy each other, leading to a new dark age.
- **Watchers of the Faithful**: The Castinellan theocracy ordain themselves the Watchers of the Faithful in the aftermath of the Gods’ End.
- **Second Ostoyan War**: The Bürach Empire invades Ostoya, trying to stabilize its strength by reclaiming territories.
- **Shadow of the Great Beast**: The first stories of the Great Beast begin circulating in the Bürach Empire.
- **Order of Kentigern**: The Order of Kentigern becomes the last shield against the coldfire crisis, now erupting from Volgen.
- **The Darkfall**: The sun vanishes from the Ostoyan Empire, giving rise to the Crimson Court, who seize power.
- **The Raevo Rebellion**: The province of Raevo secedes from the Ostoyan Empire, and the Order of Dawn is founded.
- **The Dark Mist**: Princess Tiphaine de Sauveterre disappears, and the Pact of the Sacred Land is broken.
