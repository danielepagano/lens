**Chapter 8: The City of Morencia**

## Setting Tone

Morencia is a mercantile city-state that presents a facade of determined prosperity and civic virtue while rotting from the inside. By day: trade deals, guild negotiations, ancestor-honoring festivals, warm smiles, and the quiet pride of a refugee people who built something real. By night: decadence, assassination, unexplained murders, and secret cults operating within the same institutions that govern the city by day.

The gap between the cheerful surface and the corrupt interior is the city's defining horror. Morencians are not hypocrites by accident — they are a traumatized people whose founding mythology demands they endure suffering quietly and present strength. That cultural armor is now being exploited by everyone in power.

Tone note: NPCs here smile genuinely and stab efficiently. Morencians speak of their ancestors with reverence and their current leaders with careful neutrality. They know something is wrong and have learned not to say so aloud.

## Geography

A cluster of canal-connected islands in the Gulf of Lions, between Castinella and Charneault, protected by **the Misty Reefs** — a supernatural fog wall with hidden reefs that destroy ships without a magically-imbued navigation device (the Nautantlicus, issued by the Navigator's Guild). Ships approaching Morencia are escorted to quarantine bays by Arsenal cutters. The city proper is built on once-swampy islands; narrow canals wind between stone buildings. Even midday brings a light haze. At night, bioluminescent orbs rise from the bog beneath the Water Strider Bridge.

## History (Condensed)

Morencia was founded by refugees fleeing Bürach subjugation — led by the sacrificed spiritual leader **Mother Frida Morenc**, who stayed behind to lure their pursuers away while her people escaped by sea. The crossing killed many. Those who survived built a new home on swamp islands, protected by mist they eventually learned to navigate with magic. This founding trauma — sacrifice, survival, and the moral weight of who lives and who dies — is baked into every Morencian institution, including the darker ones.

The **Castinellan Provinces** have repeatedly tried to conquer Morencia. A recent invasion was foiled when Charneault fishers spotted the fleet and Morencia's navy, with allied support, drove it off. The aftermath created an opening for Kolyhs Papinian — who then brokered trade deals with Castinella, infuriating citizens but enriching the Banker's Guild enormously.

## Districts

- **Arbrumel (Harbor)**: Docks, quarantine bay, Arsenal shipyards behind dark steel walls. Crow-watched battlements. Flotsam scourers on skiffs picking through wreckage from the Misty Reefs.
- **Vueami (Guest District)**: Inns, taverns, business offices. Visitors are monitored; business intelligence flows to city authorities. Identified fraudsters are arrested and never seen again.
- **Zitoean (Citizen District)**: Residential blocks, neighborhood shops, Arsenal patrols. Quiet at night to the point of eeriness. Foreigners need escorts or paperwork.
- **Ke 'Centre (Business District)**: Headquarters of the Augustine Trading Company, Banker's Guild, Navigator's Guild, Carpenter's Guild. Perpetually crowded. Guild skiffs dart through the canals.
- **Enducour (Government District)**: Supreme Council Hall, the Maegilate (arcane academy), and the Doge's alabaster manse in a thick bog. Swamp gases give the canals a sickly green glow at night. Lightning occasionally materializes above the Maegilate.

## Key Individuals

### Doge Lorenzo Flabenici
Morencia's lifetime-elected governor, managing the city through relentless crisis. He correctly suspects Kolyhs Papinian has corrupted the council but cannot prove it. His own credibility is shredded by scandal: accusations that he manipulated White Carnival mask allocations to exile political enemies. Still in power through resilience, political skill, and genuine negotiating talent. The dockworkers say when Flabenici smiles, someone ends up dead or broke.

### Kolyhs Papinian ("The Blazing Brain")
The real power in Morencia. Simultaneously leads the Banker's Guild and — through a proxy she later revealed — controls the Augustine Trading Company. She wrote loans for every faction during the Castinellan invasion crisis, financed the Supreme Council seat auction (buying seats herself and through proxies), and now controls Morencian commerce and governance without holding any single obvious title. She avoids the monster hunter Kaltstahl's questioning. The Banker's Guild accepts disturbing payments from some clients — blood, exotic animals — and only Kolyhs knows what they're for.

### Witch Hunter Vendemir Kaltstahl
Legendary monster hunter hired by the Supreme Council to investigate the **Moonless Night Murders** — ritualistic killings on moonless nights that leave bloodless bodies with rapidly decomposing flesh and ghostly victim-apparitions roaming the streets afterward. Kaltstahl has been erratic: moving between evidence unpredictably, demanding private council interviews with unsettling interrogation methods, privately meeting the Doge. He recently missed appointments. He is reportedly close to solving the murders but his current whereabouts are uncertain.

## Key Factions

- **The Supreme Council**: Twenty-seat governing body (expanded from ten after the seat auction). Theoretically representative; practically infiltrated by Kolyhs's proxies. Some members genuinely try to serve the city.
- **Augustine Trading Company**: Morencia's dominant trade entity, with reach across all of Etharis's major ports. Now merged with the Banker's Guild under Kolyhs's control.
- **The Banker's Guild**: Most prominent lending institution in Morencia. Issues loans to citizens, guilds, and government. Guild vaults protected by Maegilate magic. Accepts unusual payments that Kolyhs does not discuss publicly.
- **The Navigator's Guild**: Controls the Nautantlicus enchantments that allow safe passage through the Misty Reefs. Raises "pathway taxes" on merchants, creating friction with the Maegilate. Led by Admiral Endoria Onaglie, hero of the Castinellan armada battle (who lost an eye in that fight).
- **The Maegilate**: Morencia's arcane academy. City-service duties include Supreme Council escorts, Arsenal support, and consulting. Running secret joint projects with the Augustine Trading Company and Arsenal whose progress halted after the last Black Carnival. The Magi Profesorie is visibly hostile to Kaltstahl's presence.

## Notable Locations

- **The Misty Reefs**: Morencia's supernatural defense. The ancestral magic that lets ships navigate it must constantly evolve because the Misty Reefs keep evolving too. Some scholars fear the phenomenon wants to trap people inside it, not merely keep them out.
- **The Arsenal of Morencia**: Naval shipyards behind dark steel walls. The most formidable fleet in Etharis. Arsenal commanders are disciplinarians; dereliction is punished with a swim along the Misty Reef wall.
- **The Water Strider Bridge**: A spectacular bridge connecting the two halves of Enducour, sculpted to resemble a bog spider. At night, bioluminescent orbs rise from the bog and drift above it. Believed by some to be remnants of whatever lived on the islands before the Morencians arrived. They have not attacked anyone yet.
