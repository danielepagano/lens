# Chapter 1: Realms of Etharis

## Dark Themes of the Realms

### The World is Broken

Etharis is a world on the brink of destruction. The gods are dead, fear and superstition are rife, and every realm is beset by cataclysms threatening to collapse civilization. Monsters stalk the land freely while common folk cower in fragile settlements. In times of such difficulty, the sins of hatred, prejudice, and cruelty often rise above kinder virtues — raising an army requires taxes, and survival demands moral compromise.

### The Gods are Dead

The disappearance of the gods is the event most blame for Etharis's current state. Praying to them brings no aid; divine magic has dwindled to near nothing. Characters who wield divine power are anomalies — treated with awe, suspicion, or outright hostility. Healing and resurrection are near impossibilities; apothecaries and surgeons have replaced temples.

### Magic is Mistrusted

Spellcasters are targets of hatred across Etharis. A magical inquisition hunts them in the Castinellan Provinces; bureaucracies regulate them elsewhere; mobs blame them for every misfortune. Arcane casters who visibly use magic to save a village may be shunned or attacked by the very people they saved. Mages must manage their visibility carefully.

### Humanity is Flawed

Etharis is populated by the desperate and fearful — people who look out for themselves, often to the detriment of others. Help is not given freely by strangers. Bribery, intimidation, and coercion are more effective than appeals to mercy. Even those who would otherwise choose decency may be forced into cruelty by circumstance.

### Hope Within the Darkness

Despite everything, hope lingers — scattered, dimmed, but not extinguished. Small acts of kindness and bravery stand out against the dark backdrop precisely because they are rare. A dark fantasy campaign depends on these brief moments: hard-fought victories at steep price, rare acts of selflessness that illuminate what could be.

## The Realms of Etharis

### The Bürach Empire

Once a continent-spanning beacon of civilization, the Bürach Empire now crumbles — still formidable in wealth and military, but sensing inevitable collapse. A creature of colossal size known as the Great Beast stalks its provinces, corrupting the land and spawning horrors. Provincial leaders maneuver to benefit from the empire's fall. Themes: cosmic horror, gothic horror, existential dread, political corruption.

### The Ostoyan Empire

A sunless realm of shadows and blood, Ostoya is ruled by the Crimson Court — seven vampires who flaunt their power while scheming against each other and vampire hunters alike. The peasantry toils under a callous nobility that wants to drain everything from them, including their blood. Themes: gothic horror, undead, autocracy, rebellion, the cost of safety under monstrous rulers.

### The Charneault Kingdom

Hidden behind the magical Grove Maze, Charneault appears to be a land of rolling emerald hills, mist-veiled glens, and knightly virtue — but this beauty is an illusion. Capricious fey take what they want from mortals without fear of retribution. A corrupting dark mist spreads without end. The fairytales here are grimmer than they appear. Themes: supernatural fey horror, amoral cruelty, beauty masking nightmare.

### The Castinellan Provinces

Faith unifies Castinella — and that faith has transformed the populace into monsters in their own right. The Arcanist Inquisition hunts magic users with righteous zeal, torturing and killing the innocent alongside the guilty. One false accusation can destroy a life. Mages face a brutal choice: use the magic that kills monsters, or hide it and survive. Themes: religious extremism, psychological horror, witch-hunt paranoia.

### The Valikan Clans

At the frozen northern edge of the continent, the Valikan Clans endure an unforgiving arctic environment, rampaging elementals, and constant inter-clan bloodshed. Life is short; legacies live on through sagas. The honored bravery of Valikan culture is one side of the coin — brutal survival at any cost, including raiding and sacrifice, is the other. Themes: survival horror, supernatural cold, brutal honor culture.

### Liesech

The putrid cauldron where the supernatural Weeping Pox originated. Merchants attempt to carry on their lives as friends and family die around them. Body horror pervades — not just through the disease itself, but through the sometimes-questionable acts of those who try to cure it.

### Morencia

A center of banking and commerce where every smile conceals a poisoned dagger. Vibrant and effusive on the surface, Morencia hides dark secrets and secret cults willing to do anything for power and coin. Its disease is social rather than medical.
