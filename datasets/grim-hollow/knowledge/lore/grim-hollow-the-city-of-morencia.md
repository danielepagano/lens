# The City of Morencia

Morencia is an island city-state in the Gulf of Lions, between Castinella and Charneault. Protected by supernatural mists and treacherous reefs, it thrives on trade, secrecy, and ruthless power politics. Behind every daily dalliance and trade deal lie nights of decadence, deception, and death.

## Geography

An unnatural fog wall surrounds Morencia. Ships must use a **Nautantlicus** — an enchanted sextant from the Navigator's Guild — to navigate the Misty Reefs. Outdated or missing Nautantlici mean shipwreck; spectral voices of the drowned welcome newcomers from the mists.

Morencia sits on former swamp isles. Narrow canals wind between buildings. Even bright mornings bring haze; at night, canal lamps and dim moonlight barely suffice. Two short periods in summer and winter bring deadly extreme temperatures — celebrated by citizens.

## History

Morencia's founders fled Burach persecution. Their spiritual leader, **Mother Frida Morenc**, sacrificed herself to lure pursuers away while her people escaped by boat. The journey was brutal — dehydration, starvation, and desperate sacrifices thinned their numbers before refugee mages pierced the supernatural fog and found the dark, swampy islands.

The survivors named their settlement Morencia. Protected by mists and reefs, they flourished — building ships, charting waters, and perfecting navigation magic. Every citizen carries an innate determination born from ancestral sacrifice.

### Politics

Morencia is a recognized trade center with fluctuating population. Strict policies govern visitors:
- All ships undergo harbor quarantine; crews go to the **Vueami** (Guest District)
- Authorities monitor visitors' activities through business proprietors
- Permanent residency requires vetting and approval
- Foreign merchants' presence is limited, while Morencian entities — especially the **Augustine Trading Company** — dominate trade across Etharis

**Castinellan threat**: Castinella views Morencia as a cauldron of dangerous arcane power. They once captured a Morencian captain to guide their armada through the reefs. Charneault fisherfolk spotted the invasion and warned Morencia. The Navigator's Guild and Charneault navy ambushed and routed the armada. Since then, Castinella has shifted to trade agreements — suspiciously brokered by the Augustine Trading Company's leader, **Kolyhs Papinian**.

**The corruption of governance**: Kolyhs controls both the Banker's Guild and the Augustine Trading Company (through proxy). When Doge **Lorenzo Flabenici** convinced the Supreme Council to auction open seats to fund fleet reconstruction (avoiding a Banker's Guild loan), citizens and guild members scrambled for bids — running to the Banker's Guild for loans. Result: the Guild now controls purchased seats, profiting from loan terms while wielding political power. The reformed council must veil its compromised state with competence.

### The Moonless Night Murders

A series of ritualistic killings on moonless nights leaves mangled bodies in alleys and canals. Victims' ghostly forms appear afterward; bodies decompose rapidly despite preservation attempts. The **Maegilate** (arcane academy) and Arsenal investigators have found few clues.

The Supreme Council hired famed monster hunter **Vendemir Kaltstahl** to investigate. He demanded private interviews with each council member — his interrogation methods border on immoral. After conferring privately with Doge Flabenici, he pushed to bring in more hunters. The council debates heatedly. Kaltstahl is close to a solution but has recently missed appointments, making his whereabouts suspect.

### The Morencian Way

Citizens embody ancestral virtues: determination, resilience, sacrifice. Ancestral spirits visit relatives in dreams, forcing them to relive suffering. Those tormented are consoled with a hand on the shoulder and the phrase, "Thank you for suffering."

Determination taken to extremes becomes mania. Hard work is balanced by nocturnal revelry that often slides into excess. Faith is expressed through Mother Morenc — e.g., "Mother Morenc is telling me I've fallen victim to Venin's hand."

### Cultural Events

**Memoria**: At the first new moon, the Maegilate projects illusory spectacles of Morencia's history against the mists. These displays have accidentally summoned hostile spirits of the dead. Mages and priests now stand guard during the event.

**The Black Carnival**: Peak summer. Citizens wear heavy costumes, carry boats, and gorge on raw flesh, rotten fish, and salt water. Nighttime events turn abhorrent — expressions of lust, violence, and slaughter. The first Moonless Night Murder victims were discovered during the last Black Carnival.

**The White Carnival**: Winter onset, when trade slows and resources dwindle. Citizens wear white mourner's masks. The Supreme Council distributes masks to households — if there aren't enough, the family chooses. Those without masks at the toll of the Mourner's Wail bell are placed on skiffs and exiled into the mists. This reenacts the ancestral practice of exiling unproductive citizens in lean times. Very few returned. Accusations grow yearly that masks are being sold to bidders.

## Districts

Five districts surround the Government District, connected by bridges and canals. Arsenal inspectors scrutinize all passage between districts.

**Arbrumel (Harbor)**: Quarantine waters, flotsam scourers, inspector dinghies. The Arsenal shipyard's dark steel walls are watched by roosting crows.

**Vueami (Guest)**: Taverns, inns, fest halls for visitors. City representatives maintain satellite offices. Greeters offer tours, goods legal and illegal, and any service imaginable. Fraudsters are taken to Arsenal jails and never heard from again.

**Zitoean (Citizen)**: Residential blocks with corner shops. Foreigners need escorts or paperwork. Night streets are eerily quiet with robust Arsenal patrols. Constantly under renovation.

**Ke 'Centre (Business)**: Headquarters of the Augustine Trading Company, Banker's Guild, Carpenter's Guild, and Navigator's Guild. Travel is slow — hours between locations. Visitors are watched and frequently questioned by Arsenal sentries.

**Enducour (Government)**: Smallest district. The Supreme Council Hall and Maegilate spires dominate. The doge's alabaster manse is surrounded by thick bog. At night, swamp gases give canals a sickly green glimmer. Lightning materializes above the Maegilate from arcane research.

## Important Individuals

### Doge Lorenzo Flabenici
Third person to hold this lifetime office, elected in mere days — seen by some as hope, by others as omen. Manages Morencia through compounding crises: near-invasion by Castinella, unsolved murders, corrupted council. Accusations that he manipulated White Carnival mask allocations against outspoken families erode confidence. Maintains power through tenacity and negotiation with guildmasters.

### Vendemir Kaltstahl
Legendary monster hunter, slayer of the Three Sister Sorceresses. Hired to solve the Moonless Night Murders. His erratic investigation methods and borderline-immoral interrogations unsettle veteran sentries. After private meetings with council members and the doge, he pushed for more hunters. Recently missing appointments — his dedication and whereabouts are questioned.

### Kolyhs Papinian
Called "The Blazing Brain." Controls both the Banker's Guild and Augustine Trading Company through a proxy scheme. Her merger gave her power over both money and markets. She profited from every crisis — post-invasion loans, council seat auctions. She avoids Kaltstahl's questioning, fearing investigations could expose her machinations. She privately visits vaults containing disturbing client payments: blood, exotic animals, and unknown items whose true value only she knows.

## Important Factions

### The Supreme Council
Morencia's highest authority. Originally ten seats (guildmasters, Maegilate Headmaster, military leaders); expanded to twenty after the seat auction. Led by the doge. Some members serve citizens' interests; many are Kolyhs' proxies or debtors.

### The Navigator's Guild
Guards the secret of piercing the Misty Reefs — fusing ancestral magic with navigation knowledge. The Nautantlicus sextants are enchanted with temporary routes, monitored by the Maegilate. Unpaid fees or severed relations trigger magical contingencies that destroy the devices. Guildmaster **Admiral Endoria Onaglie**, hero of the Castinellan battle (lost an eye), deflects criticism of rising pathway taxes by citing defense funding.

### The Banker's Guild
Morencia's dominant money-lender. Provides loans to citizens, guilds, and government. Vaults offer magically warded asset protection. Merger with Augustine Trading Company extends reach across Etharis. Notable foreign clients include Castinellan entities — a source of citizen suspicion. Whispers of disturbing loan payments — blood, exotic creatures, and worse.

### The Maegilate
Arcane academy descended from the original refugee mages. Trains citizens in ancestral magic while serving the city: guild consulting, Council escorts, Arsenal support. The **Magi Profesorie** sits on the Supreme Council and advocates balanced governance. Secret projects exist that even the Council doesn't know about. The Magi Profesorie is visibly hostile toward Kaltstahl's investigation and fell silent after the last Black Carnival. Public trust in magic is declining even among a population that relies on it.

## Notable Locations

### The Misty Reefs
Supernatural fog wall visible miles out. Ships without proper Nautantlici wreck on reefs, become lost, or face spectral attacks. The mists evolve — ancestral navigation magic must evolve with them. Some scholars fear whatever fuels the mists wants not just to repel intruders but to trap them. Purpose unknown.

### The Arsenal of Morencia
Morencia's navy — considered Etharis's most formidable. Galleons, cutters, and skulker skiffs patrol the reef walls and guide arrivals to quarantine. Arsenal discipline is severe: dereliction is punished with a mile-long swim along the Misty Reef wall.

### The Water Strider Bridge
Connects the Enducour District halves. Smaller bridges radiate outward, giving it the appearance of a swamp spider — sculpted to match. At night, glowing orbs rise from the bog and drift above the bridge. Citizens fear these are remnants of something that lived here before the Morencians. No attacks recorded — yet.

## Quest Hooks

### Can I Go Home?
A family's daughter, exiled during the White Carnival, floats back from the Misty Reefs carrying a Nautantlicus. The family flees with her. The Supreme Council sends agents to reclaim the child and the device. Characters are begged to help — facing loyalists who demand tradition be upheld, unsettled swamp dangers, or reef spirits who want the child to join them. Political angle: convince council members to spare the child.

### The Moon Hits Your Eye
A rotting, persistent crow leads characters to **Isaa Guillaume**, daughter of the late First Mate Brieuc Guillaume, hero of the Castinellan battle. The crow may be her grandfather's pet, Lunelle. Chasing it leads into an antiquities collector's trap-laden sub-basements — a macabre museum with ties to the Banker's Guild. Inside: Brieuc's skeletal remains and Admiral Onaglie's perfectly preserved dissected eye. An eyewitness account claims Onaglie went mad and killed Brieuc as a sacrifice to the mist spirits. Public revelation could destroy a Supreme Council member's reputation and destabilize the city.
