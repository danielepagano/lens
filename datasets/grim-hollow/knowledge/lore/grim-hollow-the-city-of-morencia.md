# The City of Morencia

Ship captains know when they are near Morencia. The gulf’s water becomes choppy. The temperature noticeably drops. Skies abruptly become overcast. Passengers are ushered below decks. And the mist obscures everything from view.

On islands in the Gulf of Lions, between the coastlines of Castinella and Charneault, lies the city-state of Morencia. Travelers successfully navigating its protective mists and harsh, oppressive reefs find islands swept up in profiteering, power-plays, penitence, and purging.

Visitors to Morencia soon realize that behind every daily dalliance, trade deal, or duty fulfilled are nights filled with decadence, deception, and death. Among these contrasts, Morencia’s people cautiously live and mysteriously die. Factions and leaders overtly and covertly act to their own benefit. A prevailing fear looms in the air, much like its guardian mists. But what lies behind it all?

## Geography

An unnatural fog wall rolls across the seascape surrounding Morencia, slowly turning into an enveloping, eerie mist. Upon entering the Misty Reefs, the helmsman commands silence. The captain hawkishly watches the crow’s nest for lookout warnings. A first mate listens for crew calls from the port, starboard and prow to any dangers.

The crew works with careful synergy; along with the life-saving Nautantlicus obtained from Morencia’s Navigators Guild. Captain and crew utter prayers that they have the most current one. If they don’t, they’ll know soon enough: the hull will burst, bringing in dark water. Spectral voices of past perished cry from the mists and water below, welcoming their new dead.

Otherwise, after a gripping passage through the mists and among the reefs, vessels safely emerge to enter Morencia’s harbor. Here, an armada awaits them. While waiting to be escorted to the quarantine bay, crew and passengers take in the boats and the dock-riddled shorelines, canal entryways, bridge connected isles, and arch- and crenellation-carved stonework of its buildings.

Morencia sits upon once-desolate swamp isles. Beyond its harbor, narrow canals wind through building-lined waterways. Even the city’s brightest mornings bring a sunlight-stifling haze. At night, the city’s canal lamps join a dim moonlight’s effort to keep nightlife safe.

Temperatures are fitting for the season. However, there are two short periods during summer and winter, where temperatures grow extremely deadly—but are celebrated by citizens.

## History

Morencia’s settlers—whose culture, beliefs, magical prowess, and ways of life were challenged by the strictures of the Bürachers—fled that land in search of safe haven from subjugation. They pressed beyond the region’s borders, far south, into the Castinellan lands. Here, they thought themselves safe, but the long reach of the Bürach Empire pushed all the way to the southern coast over time.

Fearful of the assimilation and possibly persecution of her people, the refugees’ spiritual leader, Mother Frida Morenc, urged them to construct boats and sail into the Gulf of Lions. Frida, along with a few other refugees, remained behind: a dooming act. Their sacrifice lured the pursuing Bürachers away from their peoples’ sea escape.

Many perished on the water. Dehydration, hyperthermia, and hunger drove people to cruel, cold consensus and horrific, heated acts. Boat hulls, damaged by reef and rock outcroppings, forced them to lighten loads. First supplies were dumped. Then, people willingly leapt from the boats—sacrifices worthy of Mother Morenc herself.

Despite their anguish, they clung to hope. The seemingly supernatural fog that shielded the area was studied by the most magically learned among them. Through trial and error, and after many deaths, the combined magic of several refugees allowed the fog to be pierced.

Soon enough, the refugees happened upon a group of dark, swampy islands. Once ashore, they forgave, healed, and mourned the tragic journey. They scoured the islands for resources with which to build a new life. In honor of the loss of their beloved leader, they named their new home Morencia.

With the mysterious fogs and the almost fiendish reefs and tides to protect them, Morencia flourished. From a swamp settlement, a city grew. Over time, their focus turned outward to navigation and trade. Descendants from its first settlers were not only skilled in magic-craft, but were adept carpenters, sailors, and shipwrights. With these talents, they constructed stalwart buildings and bridges. They built highly sought ships. They meticulously charted their surrounding waters. And they perfected the magic to pierce the supernatural protections of the area.

Within each Morencian citizen there is an innate determination and resiliency that contributes to their city’s prosperity and remembrance of its settlers’ sacrifices. Never will they have to fear invasion from a greater power, for the very island protects them.

### Politics

As a recognized trade center, Morencia invites travelers, merchants, and their representatives. This means the population of Morencia fluctuates drastically, and businesses catering to travelers flourish around the docks. Overpopulation is a fear, however, which has led to several city-wide policies.

Upon arrival, all ships are subject to harbor quarantine, where they undergo thorough scrutiny by harbormaster agents. During this time, crews and passengers are escorted to the Vueami, or the Guest District, where they can obtain most comforts. Agents from Morencia’s guilds or government agencies conduct meetings here, providing discretion commensurate with the nature of the business.

The authorities frequently check with proprietors of businesses that cater to travelers to gain further insights into guests’ business, meetings, activities, and plans. Service guides tend visitors’ needs, providing escorts and passes to other districts. While travelers and visitors are welcome, they are not allowed to become permanent residents of Morencia without proper vetting and approval. The harbormaster shares these limitations upon arrival, and the time visitors spend in the city is monitored.

This policy limits foreign merchants’ presence and influence, while Morencia’s business entities greatly sway and manage holds beyond its shores. Chief among them is the Augustine Trading Company, which dictates the imports and exports throughout Etharis’s ports. The company benefits from a merger with the local Banker’s Guild and strong influence upon the Supreme Council.

Throughout their history, Castinella attempted to conquer or destroy Morencia’s people, militarily and politically. Morencian citizens hear stores of the cruelty and depravities of the Arcanist Inquisition, and they vow to never let its influence reach their shores. In return, the Inquisitors view Morcenia as not just as a cauldron of arcane power waiting to explode, but also as a cesspool of excess and frivolity.

Recently, the Castinellan armada captured a Morencian ship captain in an attempt to intimidate them into safely guiding the Castinellan armada through the Misty Reefs. If not for vigilant fisherfolk of Charneault who saw the potential invasion and got word to Morcenia’s authorities, Morencia might not have been able to foil the plot.

The Navigator’s Guild worked with the Charneault navy to catch the Castinellan armada off guard. With the allied attack, the Castinellan armada ships were forced to slink away out of the mists. Significant losses were dealt to all fleets. Since then, the Castinellan Provinces have not attempted an invasion of Morencia.

The Castinellan influence eventually surfaced-through surprising trade agreements with the Augustine Trading Company. These dealings reek of guileful motives, but the company’s leader, Kolyhs, assured elitists that no treachery exists and that profiting from their historical enemies is the best vengeance they could achieve.

The Banker’s Guild profited immensely from the Castinellan deal. The guild welcomed loans to Castinellan interests while offering a generous loan to the Supreme Council for reconstruction of Morencia’s fleet. The Bankers Guild leader, also Kolyhs Papinian, assured them that this was done with the safety of Morencia people in mind.

Morencia’s elected governor Doge Lorenzo Flabenici suspects something iniquitous in Kolyhs’ motives. He attempts to convince the Supreme Council to forego the Bankers Guild loan and consider another avenue.

Doge Flabenici convinced the council members they should auction open council seats. Generous citizens would gain a voting seat and provide citizen voices while supporting the stabilization of Morencia’s arsenal. The council agreed that this move would spark confidence in the council’s decision-making and quell suspicions of reaching influence of the Bankers Guild and Augustine Trading Company in matters of city governance.

The Supreme Council’s decision incited chaos. Citizens and guild members liquidated assets and called in favors to outbid others. Bids became incredibly high as prospects reached out to the Banker’s Guild for exorbitant loans.

In the end, open seats were filled, the rebuilding of Morencia’s fleet began, and its citizens pleased as new faces took seats on the council and the guild’s influence lessened. Or so they thought.

As the Banker’s Guild profits from loan terms, they benefit from their purchased seats. This weakens the power of doge and other guild seats tremendously. Nevertheless, the reformed Supreme Council must veil their immorally mired state with seeming competence.

### The Moonless Night Murders

Morencia’s majestic nights have become increasingly dangerous as of late. A series of vicious killings have left bodies mangled in district alleyways and floating in canals. Arsenal investigators, with the help of mystics from the Maegilate, have discovered few clues.

The ritualistic killings take place on nights when the moon is shrouded in cloud cover. No witness steps forward, but many claim to have seen ghostly forms of the victims moving through the city in the hours after the murder had happened. All of the bodies also decompose rapidly despite chemicals or magic being used for preservation.

Rumors mill throughout the districts. Some claim the victims were those being punished for forgetting the sacrifices of their ancestors. Other believe Castinellan inquisitors have found their way to Morencia’s shores. Scared citizens demanded answers and the Supreme Council has none. In hopes to solve the killings, the Supreme Council has hired the famous monster hunter Vedemir Kaltstahl to investigate. His arrival pacified Morencians but has caused consternation among armada sentries, who do not want citizens to lose faith in their own soldiery to protect them.


### The Morencian Way

The people attempt to embody the virtues of their ancestors: determined, resilient, hard-working and family oriented. They understand that sacrifice for the community is honorable and the importance of fair trade. Morencians are naturally cautious. They don’t need to be reminded of the ancestor’s hardships.

Some hardships are harder to forget. It is believed that ancestral spirits visit relatives in their dreams, never allowing them to forget their suffering. Those that endure these unconscious reminders are pitied by others and discreetly consoled with a hand on the shoulder and a phrase, “Thank you for suffering.” These tormented ensure that when the day demands it, they bear their strain behind welcoming smiles.

As with all virtues, anything taken to an extreme can become a vice with only a modicum of ill intent. Determination and resilience becomes mania and shortsightedness. A dedication to perfection leads to excessive celebration when no one is looking.

Those that profess faith in high powers often do so by relating their deity’s dogma through Mother Morenc, their ancestral spiritual leader. For example, when a business deal goes sour, someone might say, “Mother Morenc is telling me I’ve fallen victim to Venin’s hand,” or “Morael once reminded Mother Morenc to be patient; prosperity awaits.”

### Cultural Happenings

Morencian hard work is balanced by Morencian celebration. A good say’s work is often followed by a night of revelry. Beyond smaller district events, there are three times when its citizens join together to ostensibly remember their people’s struggles, successes, and tenacity. More often, however, it turns into debauchery and sin—or worse.

### Memoria

During the first new moon, the Maegilate presents a spectacle of conjuration and illusion magic. Against the canvas of Morencia’s protective mists, the city’s history is presented. Mother Morenc’s story, the flight from Bürach persecution, the Battle of the Castinellan Armada, and more. This spectacle captures the reviled, shocking, and tragic events.

On more than one occasion the festivities have summoned forth spirits of Morencian dead, who threaten onlookers. Now mages and priests are on hand during these festivities, protecting the curious throngs who both hope and dread to have a repeat haunting.

### The Black Carnival

At the peak of summer heat and humidity, the Morencians celebrate the lives of those who’ve come before them, ensuring their prosperity and survival. A parade sees participants wear heavy ornate costumes or carry boats upon their back as they march along the parade route in the intense heat. Others participate in gluttonous feasting, engaging in contests of eating raw animal flesh, rotten fish, and drinking salt water.

At night, carnival activities take on a more abhorrent and carnal tone: expressions of lust, violence, and slaughter are common. By Supreme Council decree, these darker events occur away from Morencia’s streets and waterways. The council recently received reports of deathly decadent activities during the most recent Black Carnival, coincidentally as the discovery of the first victims of the Moonless Night Murders occurred.

### The White Carnival

Morencia’s sister celebration occurs at the first few days of winter’s onset. The Misty Reefs become even more difficult to navigate during this time, necessitating activities to distract citizens from a time of decreased trade, less prosperity, and few resources.

The activities of the White Carnival are meant to remind people that their forebears also had to endure such plights, and to hearten the people that spring is just a few weeks away. They demonstrate respect for those sacrifices by wearing the traditional mourner’s mask: a white face mask in an expression of deep sorrow.

The Supreme Council sends a number of these masks to each district citizen’s residence, where they are handed out. Should there not be enough masks for each member of the family, then the family must decide who gets them. Those without masks at the toll of the Mourner’s Wail bell are solemnly escorted to Morencia’s dock in procession. There they are placed on a skiff and exiled into the mists.

Those who resist or flee escort are pursued, subdued and exiled. Accusations of the selling off of Mourner’s Masks to bidders grows every year, forcing public outcry. This reenacts the ritual from the early days of Morencia, where citizens who were not adding to the economy or prosperity of the city during times of want were sent out into the mists. If they returned unharmed, they were considered blessed by the ancestors and allowed to stay. Very few did return, however.

## Districts

Morencia is organized into five main districts, with four surrounding the Government District, each reachable by bridge or canal. Arsenal inspectors at each checkpoint scrutinize everyone passing between districts.

### Arbrumel (Harbor District)

Docks of varying sizes accommodate arriving vessels through the Misty Reefs, Local vessels with proper authorizations are given cursory inspections, and Morencian cutters quickly guide foreign ships to the quarantine bay waters. Boat and dockside vendors welcome arrivals with foodstuffs and ominous warnings of recent happenings. Flotsam scourers skirt about on skiffs, scavenging floating debris from vessels sank by the Misty Reefs for anything of value. Dark plumes of smoke rise from the imposing Arsenal of Morencia’s shipyards. Dark steel walls surround the area, atop which murders of crows roost and watch.

### Vueami (Guest District)

The taverns, inns, and fest halls here offer any range of comforts for Morencia’s visitors. City representatives live in satellite house-offices, where they meet business contacts or review prospective trade offers. Business owners learn what they can about guests to help the city filter good business dealings from bad. Identified fraudsters are taken to Arsenal jails and never heard from again. Other visitors are met by Morencian greeters, who offer guided tours, goods both legal and illegal, recommendations for services both vital and recreational, and any other service one might imagine.

### Zitoean (Citizen District)

The majority of Morencian citizens live here. Residential blocks are identifiable by numbers and by statues carved from building foundations. Corner stores and small vendor tents line most walkways and canals. Businesses that operate in Ke ’Centre have shops here: citizens need not go far to resupply, and at lower prices than elsewhere.

Foreign visitors are only allowed here under escort or with the necessary paperwork. At night, streets are eerily quiet. Very few travel unless they are going to or from work, or to or from some revelry. Dedicated lamplighters move quickly to light canals and walkway lamp posts. Arsenal patrol presence is robust. The district is constantly under renovation.

### Ke ‘Centre (Business District)

The prominent Augustine Trading Company, the Bankers, and the Carpenter’s and Navigator’s Guilds houses are headquartered here. Travel is slow at best or, at worst, impossible—taking hours to get from place to place. Citizens know when company or guildmasters travel: their dark wooden skiffs dart through canals.

Foreigners, ambassadors, businesspeople, and their intermediaries mingle here. Though visitors are watched carefully, they are free to travel as long as they have a good reason to provide to Arsenal sentries when stopped and questioned—which can be frequently and closely. Visitors can acquire paperwork to save themselves some hassle, but it is only granted temporarily and with good cause.

### Enducour (Government District)

From the checkpoints of the Water Strider Bridge, the Supreme Council’s Hall is clearly visible, as is the spires of the Maegilate, the city’s arcanum center. This is Morencia’s smallest district, as only matters of governance and academia occur within its streets. Councilmembers, professors, guests, and students travel from and to other districts after their daily business is conducted.

Morencia’s lifetime elected doge lives here in a three-story, alabaster stone manse surrounded by a thick bog. At night, rising swamp gases give the lantern-lit canals a sickly green glimmer. Occasional displays of lightning and thunder materialize above the Maegilate, believed to be the result of perverse magic research.

## Important Individuals

### Doge Lorenzo Flabenici

There have been only three people to serve as the city’s official representative. The doge serves a lifetime term. Traditionally, the election process is several weeks long. Flabenici’s election occurred in mere days, so quickly that some Morencians saw this as a sign of hope. Others proclaimed this an omen of darker days to come.

Flabenici manages Morencia through turbulent periods. Overconfidence in the Misty Reefs’ power almost led to invasion by the Castinellan armada. Unsolved murders connected to the Black Carnival whittle confidence in Flabenici’s influence. His successful lobby to auction Supreme Council seats to replenish the treasury angered district leaders. Rising accusations that the doge adjusted White Carnival mask allocations of some of Morencia’s outspoken and wealthy families weighs heavily on citizen confidence in him. Yet, Doge Flabenici demonstrates resiliency. By presenting the people’s needs to the Supreme Council and negotiating with guildmasters, he maintains a tenuous but firm grasp on his position.

### Witch Hunter Vendemir Kaltstahl

They hope that he can solve the Moonless Night Murders and restore lost confidence in the Supreme Council’s ability to keep the city safe. Despite disagreement from some council leaders, the investigation began. The monster hunter erratically moved between locations and evidence—perplexing veteran sentry escorts. After a time with seemingly little progress, Kaltstahl demanded to speak with each member of the Supreme Council in private. Most council-people were hesitant, the monster hunters interrogation techniques border on immoral. Kaltstahl then conferred privately with Doge Flabenici, arousing suspicion. Shortly after, the doge presented a motion to the Supreme Council to bring in more monster hunters. The council heatedly debates this request. Kaltstahl is on the brink of solving the murders and bringing forth those responsible but needs help from afar. However, the monster hunter missed some recent appointments, making his whereabouts and dedication to this hunt a matter of speculation.

### Kolyhs Papinian

Most Morencians readily admit that the personality dubbed “The Blazing Brain” is the real power in Morencia. As the Banker’s Guild leader, she allowed the Augustine Trading Company to acquire a majority stake in the guild, while simultaneously acquiring a majority stake in the Augustine Trading Company through proxy. This clever ploy gave Kolyhs the insight and influence to write loans and secure trade deals.

In time, her proxy became leader of the trading company, then revealed her status as proxy. Most profited from Kolyhs efforts, so she was voted in unanimously. Control of both the money and the markets allowed Kolyhs to benefit tremendously through Morencian historical turmoil.

In the aftermath of the Castinellan Provinces invasion, lucrative loans were written. In the auction for Supreme Council seats, prospects ran to the Banker’s Guild for leveraging funds. Of course, Kolyhs bought a seat herself and has proxies in other seats.

Councilmembers and citizens suspect Kolyhs corrupted the council, but they haven’t quite figured the extent of the corruption. The entrepreneur avoids questioning by Vendemir Kaltstahl. While not thought to be complicit in the Moonless Night Murders, she fears that any investigations into the machinations of the powers-that-be in Morencia might lead to unwanted revelations.


## Important Factions

### The Supreme Council

Morencia’s main governing body is the highest legal and political authority in the city-state. It consisted of ten seats, nine of which were seated by Morencia’s guildmasters, the Maegilate Headmaster, and other business and military leaders. The recently opened citizen representatives seats have expanded the council’s number to twenty.

The council is always led by Morencia’s current doge. This structure allows for the considerations and influence from each corner of Morencian society. While intrigue and self-interest roils through the council, some leaders have the best interests of Morencia’s citizens at heart. The council sent the monster hunter Vendemir Kaltstahl to help solve a series of unexplained murders and voted to acquire more monster hunters to aid the effort.

### The Navigator’s Guild

Deeply rooted in this guild’s history is the mystery of the Misty Reefs and the secrets of Morencian magic. The fusion of the two is the key to navigating Morencia’s protective mists and the dangers that they veil. This knowledge is deeply guarded by the guild, and costly to maintain.

Morencian allies that have this knowledge carry an enchanted Nautantlicus, an artifact sextant magically imbued with a route through the mists. This enchantment is temporary and monitored by the Maegilate. If the recurring costs for having safe passage are not paid for or relations with its keeper and Morencia is severed, a magic contingency removes them.

The Navigator’s Guild has been known to increase the “pathway tax” for merchants, leading to harsh criticism by leaders that the sextants and their secrets should be kept under balanced oversight of the Maegilate. Their guildmaster, Admiral Endoria Onaglie, assuages such concerns, asserting that tax increases are used to aid Morencia’s defenders who have contributed to society with their sacrifices. Admiral Onaglie is a hero of the Battle of the Castinellan Armada, having lost an eye in the battle.

### The Banker’s Guild

The guild’s influence is apparent. It is the most prominent money-lending entity in Morencia. As such, it provides loans to citizens, guilds, and government, with privately negotiate terms. Guild vaults offer asset protection bolstered by the Misty Reefs and magically warded by the Maegilate. The merger with the Augustine Trading Company expands their reach throughout Etharis and allows for investment beyond the mists.

Among its prominent clientele abroad are mercantile entities of the Castinellan Provinces. Trade relations with Morencia’s enemy has not escaped citizen notice. The guild endures scrutiny for its exorbitant loan terms, but whispering also speaks to disturbing payments of client’s blood, exotic animals, and other disturbing items.

Kolyhs Papinian denies the existence of such transactions to the public, but in private she frequently visits the vaults that contain these unique acquisitions. Only Kolyhs knows the true value of these payments.

### The Maegilate

The first gatherings of Morencia’s magically talented settlers evolved into this teaching body. Selected citizens master magic of their ancestors. Arcane academia also includes city service: guild consulting, Supreme Council escorts, Arsenal support, and duties within the Maegilate itself. The Magi Profesorie oversees the city’s arcane interests and is the voice of the institute on the Supreme Council. As an entity, the Maegilate strives for a balanced hand in council decision-making. The Supreme Council recognizes consultations with the Augustine Trading Company and the Arsenal. These meetings guide the testing of Morencia’s armament advances. Its progress is kept guarded by these entities. These meetings halted in the aftermath of the last Black Carnival celebration. The Magi Profesorie, who provides updates on the collaboration, remains silent and visibly irritated at the arrival of the monster hunter, Vendemir Kaltstahl, and the Supreme Council’s recent vote to bring in more hunters to aid Kaltstahl in the Moonless Night Murders. Groups with the Maegilate work on special projects, and not all are known to the public—or even to the Supreme Council. While the members of the Maegilate are treated with respect by the population, the distrust of magic continues to grow, even within a population that has relied upon it to ensure trade continues.

## Notable Locations

### The Misty Reefs

Morencia’s ghostly mist wall is spotted miles out by vessel lookouts. This is more than enough time to avoid it, or if entering, using the Nautantlicus given to the vessel by Morencia’s Navigator’s Guild. If the magical sextant isn’t closely heeded, vessels know quickly enough. They can become lost in the misty labyrinth, wreck upon jagged reefs and sandbars, or attacked by spectral spirits of sunken ships.

The specialists of Morencia did not control the mist or the reefs; in fact, they struggle to understand the ever-evolving supernatural forces that feed it. The ancestral magic that originally allowed for navigation of the Misty Reefs has had to evolve along with the phenomenon. It is feared by some scholars that whatever fuels the magic of the Misty Reefs wants to do more than keep people away—it may want to trap people there. But to what purpose? Few dare to speculate.

### The Arsenal of Morencia

All Morencians proudly boast that their navy is the most formidable in all Etharis. Few contest this. The mighty galleons, sleek cutters, and skulker skiffs are made from the finest craftsmanship. The fleet is spread in locations along Morencia’s shorelines. Its largest presence floats in waters outside just outside the Arsenal shipyard walls.

Cutters vigilantly watch the Misty Reef walls for any sign of vessel arrival, to which they guide into three-day quarantine waters. There, the Arsenal auxiliary, consisting of flotsam skulkers and inspector dinghies, inspect cargo and hulls for anything out of the ordinary. Sailors rotate duty from ship to canal and walkway patrols. The Arsenal leaders are stern disciplinarians. Dereliction is not met with brig time, but with a mile long-swim along the Misty Reef’s wall to whatever fate awaits.

### The Water Strider Bridge

A breathtaking spectacle of Carpenter’s Guild effort, this bridge connects the two halves of the islands of the Enducour District. Several smaller bridges connect it from the other districts, giving it the appearance of a swamp spider. Sculptors indulged further by carving these bridges into a majestic, life-like resemblance of the bog arachnid.

During the day, the view from the center of the main bridge allows a view into every district bridge was a favored place to watch the Black Carnival events or Memoria presentation. At night, the bridge it lit by glowing orbs that naturally rise from the bog and fly about and above the bridge. This draws the curious who claim and fear that they are the remnants of something that lived here before the Morencians came. There has been no record of the lights attacking anyone. Yet.

## Quest Hooks

The following quest hooks may take place in Morencia.

### Can I Go Home?

Out on the docks, a Morencian family mourns the loss of their daughter, exiled during the last White Carnival. Imagine their horror when they spot her floating out from the Misty Reefs! They collect her, they find her in possession of a Nautantlicus. Elated but afraid, they flee with her.

The Supreme Council soon finds out what has happened and sends emissaries to collect her and the magical navigation device to figure out what has happened. The fleeing family stumbles into the characters and begs them to help get their child to safety. The characters might face Supreme Council or Morencian loyalists, both of whom demand that tradition of the White Carnival be upheld.

Their flight to take the child to safety may take them through into the unsettled swamp, where natural dangers or into the Misty Reefs, where spirits long for the child to join them. They may reach out to Supreme Council members to argue for the life of the child and sway the council’s decision.

### The Moon Hits Your Eye

While traveling through any of Morencia’s districts, the characters continually spot a rotting crow, perched on a nearby bridge, dock ledge, or rooftop. The crow annoyingly pesters the characters, leading them into the Zitoean District, home of Isaa Guillaume, daughter of the deceased hero of the ship Relentless, First Mate Brieuc Guillame. Upon mention of the crow, Isaa reveals that this might be Lunelle, her grandfather’s pet crow. She shares what she knows about the historic Castinellan Provinces’s invasion and the heroic acts of Admiral Endoria Onaglie, adding that her father died heroically during the battle against Castinella. Isaa offers to compensate the character if they can capture Lunelle and bring him back home.

Chasing the crow takes the characters into the business of an antiquities collector with close ties to the Banker’s Guild. This noble’s trap-laden sub-basements are a macabre museum of historic treasures. Included in them is the skeletal remains of Brieuc Guillame, and the perfectly preserved dissected eye of Admiral Endoria Onaglie. A transcribed eye-witness account from that time insists that the Admiral lost her mind and killed Brieuc as a sacrifice to the spirits of the mists. If these discoveries were made public, they could outrage the city and may besmirch the reputation of one of Morencia’s great heroes and member of the Supreme Council.
