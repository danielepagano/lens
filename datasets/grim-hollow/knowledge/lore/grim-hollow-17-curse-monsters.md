# Chapter 17: Curse Monsters

Invoking the Curse

Employing a rare substance called Shadowsteel to channel their power into spells that are irretrievably corrupt, Etharis’s most cruel spellcasters have made spite into an art form. Shadowsteel curses and the creatures they spawn have been crafted specifically to spread pain and horror throughout the land, binding what might have been a goodly person into a single expression of vice so vile that their very existence causes strife, destruction, and death.

In every case, these horrific monsters are a blight upon the world until they’re destroyed or, in rare circumstances, cured of their afflicting curse. Some of the creatures created by Shadowsteel curses are documented below, though it’s entirely possible that this is a mere sampling of the horrors possible with this power.

## Using Curse Monsters

Curse monsters come about when a victim of a Shadowsteel curse (see the Grim Hollow Player’s Guide) fails to shake its effects. They finally succumb to the final stage of the curse, transforming into a terrible creature that loses all semblance of its former self. It is so rare for someone who’s become a curse monster to be turned back into their original form that even the most studied sages just assume it’s impossible. Of course, something is always impossible until it is done.

Should a character fall victim to this outcome, the newly created monster falls under the control of the Game Master while the player creates a new character. Assuming the monster isn't immediately killed by the other characters, it might escape and become an ongoing element of the campaign.

Characters too can wield the power of Shadowsteel curses, so it is possible that an NPC could fall victim to a curse as well. Again, the Game Master should weave this new monster into the ongoing campaign’s story. Although the creature remembers nothing of its prior life beyond the languages it knew -- and thus shouldn't automatically have a vendetta against its tormentor -- it can and should return to plague the characters in some way later on.

Finally, these curse monsters can be used like a typical creature in a Grim Hollow campaign: as monsters to be hunted and destroyed before more innocent people are injured or killed. In the course of investigating or hunting the creature, the characters may learn more of the backstory of how it came to be. In some cases, the identity of the caster who established the Shadowsteel curse that led to the creature’s existence might be learned.

In cases like this, a quest to seek justice on behalf of the creature, or even seek a way to reverse the effects of the curse, might be attempted. Such a storyline might be beyond the ability of the characters. Vengeance might be of no interest to the characters, who only want to defeat the creature that threatens the local populace. But for characters who fight evil, demand justice, or even just want to see the most wicked of individuals punished, tracing the curse back to the caster might be a satisfying arc.



## Curse Monster Hooks

Some potential story hooks for curse monsters are listed below, springboards for what is very likely a much more complicated situation. In all cases, it’s important to note that each of these creatures represents a powerful element you can use within the context of the story of your specific game. Use these in your game as-is, or as inspiration for your own ideas.

Avatar of Slaughter. At the site of a recent battle, the characters find both sides completely obliterated with no clear winner. In fact, apart from a single set of footprints, there’s no evidence that anyone left the battlefield alive at all.

Bloated Gastromorph. A country fair erupts into chaos when one of the contestants in a pie-eating contest suddenly transforms into a horrific monstrosity of epic proportions.

Body Snatcher. What appears to be a string of serial murders has thrown the city into the grips of terror. All that remains of the victims is their skin.

Dream Whisperer. The local magistrate is stricken with an affliction that robs them of their ability to speak the local dialect.

Herald of Calamity. A glowing skeletal figure is seen on the roof of a local clockwork by the light of the moon. Afterward, countless accidents occur that put the local horologist in danger. They refuse to work until an exorcism has been completed successfully.

Mind Siphon. Several prominent sages have gone missing from the local library. A massive insect has been spotted lurking in the shadows on the library grounds.

Plague Carrion. A deadly plague has run rampant across the countryside, and the characters have been employed to find the source of the sickness.

Sightless Agony. A strange gargoyle was spotted haunting the old, abandoned outpost. The characters are sent to investigate.

Verminous Abomination. Hordes of rats have been plaguing the city. Strangely, it is believed they are also picking pockets and carrying away tiny inedible bits into a possible underground lair.

Weeping Willow. Several local farmhands wandered into the woods and never returned. The sound of weeping has been reported coming from within those woods.


## Avatar of Slaughter

In any war, the most vicious combatants revel in carnage, destroying homes and lives without a second thought. Within the confines of such conflicts, a Curse of Uncontrollable Wrath can be the most powerful instrument of vengeance. Brave knights and bloodthirsty tyrants alike are reduced to a state of primal rage upon becoming an Avatar of Slaughter. Under the right circumstances, even a pacifist can fall prey to this curse if the perpetrator responsible is truly vile. An Avatar of Slaughter appears as a daemonic minotaur with smoke fuming from its nostrils, existing only to kill any one unfortunate enough to cross its path. They often seek out friends and family first, as they unconsciously attempt to demolish their former lives. Kitt Lapeña Avatar of Slaughter

Engaging an Avatar of Slaughter in combat is a task best left to the extremely brave or equally reckless. Born in the fires of conflict, they cannot be completely killed by it, though their affinity for violence means a defeated Avatar of Slaughter’s essence seeks refuge within a weapon.

## Bloated Gastromorph

Often whispered of in folk tales and legends, most frequently as a warning to children regarding the gruesome consequences awaiting those who recklessly indulge, the Bloated Gastromorph is an incarnation of gluttony and selfishness. This horrific creature has been transformed and twisted by the final stages of a Curse of Ravenous Hunger. Those afflicted become an enormous, slug-like monster possessing multiple fist-like claws, a wicked tentacular tongue, and a giant, voracious maw of gnashing teeth. Often dwelling in dank ruins of once-great castles, a Bloated Gastromorph cares about nothing but consuming every morsel of flesh it encounters, living or not. Bereft of memories of their prior lives, these creatures dwell fully in this new existence of unfettered consumption. Kitt Lapeña Gastromorph

## Body Snatcher

Body Snatchers are humanoids who have been transformed by a Curse of Damned Aging. Notoriously difficult to locate, Body Snatchers frequently hide in plain sight, since they essentially resemble their host creature. It’s common that when on the trail of a Body Snatcher, monster hunters and investigators find themselves trailing a step behind the creature. The only evidence that typically remains is the withered and discarded skin sack of their latest victim. Body Snatchers possess an amorphous form, which they use to inhabit the bodies of their victims, living inside them as a parasite that consumes their being from the inside. Jens Kuczwara Body Snatcher

The Body Snatcher takes complete control of its host body, enhancing it with supernatural capabilities. The only sign that an individual has been invaded by a Body Snatcher is their erratic behavior and rapid aging—a host body can appear to age a lifetime in a matter of a few weeks.

While far from helpless, Body Snatchers prefer to avoid detection, engaging enemies only in dire circumstances or when changing hosts. They typically make every effort to complete the latter in seclusion to maintain their gruesome charade.

## Dream Whisperer

Those learned individuals with aspirations of greatness, but who have fallen to fate’s twisted ironies and been afflicted by a Curse of Lost Sentiment, may emerge from such trials as a Dream Whisperer. Now a deformed, hunched creature covered in contorted arms, the Dream Whisperer uses its many hands to grasp and shred the hopes, dreams, thoughts, and memories of its victims.

Though animalistic in behavior, Dream Whisperers often reside within larger settlements, cities, and the like, where they can spread their influence and remain undetected for long periods of time. Preferring to attack at night, Dream Whisperers often sneak into unsuspecting targets’ homes through windows and otherwise inaccessible openings, since their many arms allow them to scale otherwise impossible surfaces. Once inside, their arms serve a second function—to physically tear treasured memories from their victims and replace them with feelings of paranoia and doubt. Kitt Lapeña Dream Whisperer

Fighting a Dream Whisperer is a dangerous task. Brave heroes have been driven mad by the Dream Whisperer’s aberrant attacks. Comrades-in-arms have been compelled to turn on each other, and the weakest minds snap, leaving a drooling mess in the creature’s wake. With each stolen memory, the Dream Whisperer seeks to slake its unquenchable thirst for its lost cognition.

## Herald of Calamity

The tragic individuals who become Heralds of Calamity were afflicted by the Fates themselves, or so the story goes. As recipients of a Curse of Ill-Fated Fortune, Heralds of Calamity bring tragedy upon all who linger in their presence. Legends say these foul beings hunt unseen, lurking near vital locations such as a village well, bridge, or other communal site where they can inflict many casualties before survivors think to abandon the place. When seen, Heralds of Calamity appear as incorporeal skeletal figures, though they are invisible except under the light of the moon. Unlike other curse monsters, Heralds of Calamity are not physically formidable, nor do they wield tremendous arcane power. Many monster hunters, however, have fallen believing that a Herald of Calamity would be an easy challenge. Expert archers have misfired, fatally wounding companions. Seasoned adventurers have slipped on trivial obstacles and received debilitating injuries. Jens Kuczwara Herald of Calamity

Regardless of the circumstance, all who have engaged a Herald of Calamity have the same advice: Rely on the maxim that if something can possibly go wrong, it will.

## Mind Siphon

When an individual places too much value on their own opinions and ability, and simultaneously mistrusts all others, they risk drawing the ire of those capable of afflicting them with a Curse of Fastidious Pride. The curse begins with an ever-growing sense of hubris regarding their own abilities and knowledge. As their narcissism grows, their feelings of being valued by others diminish. Eventually the afflicted individual cannot rest, believing that tireless work and toil is the only way for them to live up to their own self-perceived greatness. The transformation into a hideous, malformed, multi-limbed abomination is coupled with an insatiable need to feed on the intelligence of others. As their victims multiply, the Mind Siphon uses their dismembered corpses to construct a macabre hive-like dwelling. Lucy Lisett Mind Siphon

The Mind Siphon has a twisted insectoid appearance, possessing the torso of the original individual where the thorax would reside. In place of their original head is a gaping maw of fangs and tentacular tongues. From the base of the torso, the remainder of this creature resembles a chitinous abdomen with many appendages ending in hands upon which it walks. Lastly, a mouthlike protrusion at its front is topped by a dozen or more blinking eyes.

## Plague Carrion

A Plague Carrion appears as a vile, decaying creature spewing pestilence across the landscape, destroying vegetation and poisoning living creatures unlucky enough to cross its path. A Plague Carrion is a sad creature that loathes its own existence and eschews combat, though such a creature defends itself when forced or cornered. Despite being the least aggressive of all the curse monsters, individual Plague Carrions are believed to represent the greatest threat to the people of Etharis. Some even say Weeping Pox is a product of a Plague Carrion. Jens Kuczwara Plague Carrion

A Curse of Foul Blight is most frequently used by hags and dark sorcerers to spite beautiful rivals out of jealousy. Those who fall victim suffer a miserable fate. If not cured quickly, the victim becomes a Plague Carrion, a deformed creature covered in gaping cysts that expel clouds of profane corruption. Venomous insects belch forth from these calloused apertures.

A Plague Carrion is compelled by its curse to constantly wander, tormented by its own corporeal decay if it lingers in the same place for too long. This cruel punishment ensures its foul blight spreads far and wide throughout the land.

## Sightless Agony

Transforming into a Sightless Agony is the result of being afflicted with a Curse of Crushing Sensation. Victims find themselves fleeing the company of others in search of peaceful solitude, as even the gentle sounds of nature become jarring and obtrusive to these poor souls. Such unfortunates have often left those behind who care for them, or perhaps even those who have the need for some rare skill the individual possesses. A Sightless Agony seeks a dark, quiet place to hide itself. They often tear out their own eyes and claw away their skin to escape the agony of sensation, and the company of others becomes entirely unbearable. In such a state, they know only a boundless, white-hot rage. Lucy Lisett Sightless Agony

When discovered, a Sightless Agony appears as a mutated gargoyle with skin flayed from its malformed figure. They attack with razor-sharp claws and a mouthful of fangs, and though a Sightless Agony possesses wings, they are loath to fly far, since the feeling of the wind is nearly unbearable. Even standing in one place, the Sightless Agony quivers and twitches as sensations from their immediate environment cause them to flinch at the unseen.

## Verminous Abomination

Few demonstrate avarice like those who fall victim to a Curse of Insatiable Greed. Those afflicted transform into a bloated, rat-like creature known as a Verminous Abomination. The Verminous Abomination is consumed with a desire for possessions—from beautiful riches to the discarded trash of city dwellers: the form or value of the trinket does not matter. All objects are prized by a Verminous Abomination, and for another to possess them is considered an offense. Verminous Abominations dwell beneath large population centers, creating labyrinthine lairs to guard the hoards they amass. Adventurers who seek to clear out a complex—be it within sewers, mines, caverns, or wherever such a creature might dwell—rarely return to the surface, as fighting a Verminous Abomination in its lair is a challenge that few can overcome. ## Weeping Willow

The wise have been known to provide this advice: if you hear crying in a woodland or forest that has become strangely quiet, run as quickly as you can—though it may already be too late. The cry of a Weeping Willow is difficult to ignore and has often led the unwary to an untimely demise. A Weeping Willow is the unfortunate victim of a Curse of Conceited Obsession. Once a vain or narcissistic individual, they become a twisted half-tree, half-humanoid creature warped by self-love and malicious intent. A Weeping Willow is obsessed with its own appearance, calling for brave heroes to aid it in its distress. Upon reaching the Weeping Willow, however, heroes find only a vicious predator, a grove of unusual shimmering trees, and a very likely fate worse than death. Combating a Weeping Willow unprepared often ends in disaster. The creature’s enthralling cries and illusory beauty are difficult to resist, drawing its prey closer. The touch of a Weeping Willow can transform any creature's into a tree, bearing mirrored leaves for the vain creature to gaze into. Survivors of these encounters say that if you can resist the creature’s malignant lure, it’s best to leave them alone with their gardens of reflecting trees and self-admiration.
