# Chapter 16: Dark Fantasy Rules

This chapter contains optional rule systems that reinforce the dark fantasy tone of Grim Hollow. The AI design operator should be aware these systems exist and what they represent narratively, even though mechanical adjudication is outside its scope.

## Alternate Rule Systems

- **Alternative Rest System** — Replaces standard Short and Long Rests with a grittier structure: Quick Rests (1 hour, limited recovery), Short Rests (8 hours of sleep), and Long Rests (32 hours in a place of genuine safety — not a dungeon, alley, or wilderness). Thematically, rest becomes a meaningful resource that forces characters to care about the places and people that can shelter them.

- **Grievous Wounds** — When a character is dropped to 0 HP, they can choose to stay at 1 HP but roll a serious injury (festering wounds, internal bleeding, torn muscles, damaged hearing, etc.). Thematically, Etharis punishes survival — every fight leaves a mark that doesn't simply vanish after a night's sleep.

- **Permanent Wounds** — A subset of Grievous Wounds that cannot be healed without powerful magic (Regenerate). Lost limbs, scarred lungs, psychological fractures. Thematically, these represent the lasting cost of adventuring in a world that does not romanticize violence.

- **New Conditions (Bleeding, Corruption, Dazed)** — Additional status effects used by Grim Hollow rules. Corruption is the most significant: it tracks how much the darkness has changed a character, accumulating until the character dies or transforms into a creature of the GM's choosing.

- **Great Beast Mutations** — When characters encounter the Great Beast or powerful eldritch/mutative forces, they risk random physical and psychological transformation. Thematically, the Great Beast is not a monster to be defeated — it is an idea (corruption, annihilation, the inevitability of all endings) made manifest. Proximity changes people permanently.

- **Diseases and Afflictions** — Three tiers: natural diseases (treatable with medicine), magical contagions (harder to resist, harder to cure), and curse diseases like the Weeping Pox (nearly incurable even by powerful magic). Diseases are meant to function as real threats in Etharis, not minor inconveniences.

- **Legendary Templates** — Rules for applying legendary actions to non-legendary monsters, making solo boss encounters appropriately dangerous without requiring purpose-built stat blocks.

- **Heroism and Corruption (Resolve Dice / Beast Dice)** — A meta-currency system tracking the balance between heroism and darkness across the campaign. The players hold Resolve Dice; the GM holds Beast Dice. Thematically, this represents Fate becoming unstable after the gods died — both heroic sacrifice and callous evil visibly tip the scales of the world.
