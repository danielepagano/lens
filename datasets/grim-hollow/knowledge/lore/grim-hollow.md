### Dark Themes of the Realms

As you bring your own ideas and shades of light and dark to your campaigns and adventures, it’s important to understand what Etharis, as the world of this Grim Hollow campaign setting, offers. Later in the chapter, a summary of each of the nations and city-states is provided.

Here, however, are the concepts and themes that Grim Hollow offers.

#### The World is Broken

Etharis is a world on the brink of destruction. The gods are dead, fear and superstition are rife, and every realm is beset by cataclysms threatening to collapse civilization entirely.

The bleak realities of Etharis make the realm incredibly dangerous. The common folk cower in their fragile settlements while monsters stalk freely across the land. Travel between population centers carries risk. Even within the walls of relative safety, greed and desperation foster a different kind of evil that preys upon the kind and unwary.

In times of such difficulty, the sins of hatred, prejudice, and cruelty often rise above kinder virtues. Etharis is filled with people who dream of a brighter tomorrow but are often willing to commit terrible deeds today to more easily survive the night. Raising and feeding an army requires taxes. A king cannot be a generous sovereign while defending his realm from monsters.

As the Game Master, you must decide how to portray the NPCs that live in this milieu. If empathy and sacrifice often end in ruin or death, does that change how a character acts and reacts? What angles do people take if they are commoners, powerful entities, and everyone else in between? Does the presence of powerful heroes give hope and encourage goodness, or are others more likely to take advantage of their presence for their own selfish means?

#### The Gods are Dead

The disappearance of the gods is the event that many blame for the current state of Etharis. Few people know the truth of their downfall, but all are aware that praying to the gods brings no aid and no relief. The divine magic that once offered healing and protection to those in need has dwindled, and few remain who can kindle its power.

The existence of the characters, especially those who wield the power of divine magic, is an anomaly in Etharis. They might be treated with awe, but they are just as likely to be seen as frauds or dangerous outliers. With no organized church structure behind these characters, there is no implicit trust or respect. If seen as a unique prophet of the Arch Seraphs, reactions could vary from cultlike worship to repulsion, hostility, or disbelief.

Power groups are just as likely to want to kill or discredit someone who wields the power of the dead gods. Threats against secular power are not tolerated by those with so much to lose.

The death of the gods has serious consequences, even for those who don’t wish to partake in a divine class. Healing and curative magic is not readily available at shrines or temples. Similarly, resurrection is a near impossibility. Instead, those who can afford it rely on the medical sciences of apothecaries and surgeons for healing.

#### Magic is Mistrusted

Spellcasters in Etharis find themselves the target of hatred and mistrust. A magic inquisition has sprung up in the Castinellan Provinces, bureaucracies attempt to regulate and control the lives of mages elsewhere, and mobs hunt down those the common folk can blame for their misfortunes. Where once they delved into the mysteries of the universe, now wizards and their kin must seek to ensure their very survival.

What does this mean for a Grim Hollow adventure? Arcane casters who hurl flames or manipulate minds in an obvious manner to save a village are likely to find themselves shunned in the aftermath, or even being attacked by the townsfolk they just saved. When, in the mind of a naïve country farmer, magic is the tool of daemons and witches, being saved by magic is no salvation at all.

As the Game Master, you can decide the extent to which each person or location in your world mistrusts magic, and what form that mistrust takes. As long as the characters understand the theme is present, it becomes their responsibility to manage it to the best of their capabilities.

#### Humanity is Flawed

When faced with difficult circumstances, some rise to the occasion and help those in need. In a dark fantasy setting, many people fall on the other side of the coin. Etharis is populated by the desperate and fearful—those who look out for themselves, often to the detriment of others. Whether it’s a shrewd noble murdering their political rivals over dinner or an impoverished farmer stealing vital medicine from their elderly neighbor to treat their own sickly child, the people of Etharis are rarely given a fair choice when deciding between decency and survival.

In short, Etharis is home to the scheming, the greedy, and the cruel. And even those who would otherwise eschew such evils may be forced into them through circumstances. Thus, Game Masters should remember that help is not something given freely by strangers, whether they have much, little, or nothing to lose. Bribery, intimidation, coercion, and sometimes outright blackmail are languages better understood in a world of mistrust and paranoia, when appeals for mercy or sympathy inevitably fail.

What is a helping hand in a traditional fantasy campaign should be looked at with suspicion in Grim Hollow. The danger of the monster guarding its lair might not be much different than an innkeeper guarding their hostel. After all, a bit of poison in the stew could net great wealth.

#### Hope Within the Darkness

Despite the dire circumstances facing the world, hope lingers. It may be scattered, dimmed, and assaulted on all sides, but it has not been extinguished. A farmer stands against the oncoming soldiers, giving his friends and family time to flee. A merchant, with a kindly smile, hands a loaf of bread to a poor beggar. A paladin, the last of his order, continues his quest to rid the world of a daemon. Across Etharis, small acts of kindness, of bravery, of charity, all stand out amid the dark backdrop. Few and far between, their scarcity only serves to make them more special. As the Game Master, bringing these glimmers of hope into the world is part of what makes playing in a dark fantasy narrative rewarding for yourself and the players. A dark fantasy setting is one in which good rarely vanquishes evil, and even then, it is only a temporary victory. However, those hard-fought victories, won at a steep price, deserve celebration. The secret of a successful dark fantasy campaign is knowing when to present that opportunity for heroism and selflessness, that ray of light in an otherwise dark world. Olha Nykytiuk Dreamer Mercenary

### Factions of Etharis (lore.grim-hollow-factions-of-etharis)

- **Augustine Trading Company**. Amid the splendors of Morencia sits an organization that guards its treasure as jealously as a dragon defends its hoard. The Augustine Trading Company is an ancient and venerable institution that maintains a presence in every country, from the southern reaches of Castinella to the misty city of Castalore. The Trading Company puts profit above people, meaning that missions often end with the members having to decide if they are going to do the proper thing or the expedient, profitable thing. Those who choose the latter are rewarded. Those who chose the former find themselves named enemies of the Augustine hierarchy.
- **Company of Free Swords**. An ancient and elite mercenary company, the Free Swords have tipped the scales of war for many nations and provinces. The company includes warriors, wizards, priests, druids, rogues, and spies among its ranks. It has a branch in nearly every corner of the continent. They have maintained a code of honor since their founding: stay loyal to the company, be honest in your dealings, and take vengeance on those who betray you or your comrades. They live simple lives free of excess, spending most of their time honing their fighting skills.
- **Ebon Syndicate**. The Ebon Syndicate is an organization with a finger in every major criminal trade. Assassination, kidnapping, drug trafficking, theft, and the black market all fall within their purview. The Ebon Syndicate prefers to keep its actions and schemes in the shadows, especially in areas where they may not have the strongest foothold. In these cases, the Syndicate tends to hire mercenaries and freelance charlatans to do the most dangerous work.
- **Thaumaturge**. The Grand Thaumatic Order of archmages is a myth. Its existence has been a rumor throughout history, yet most commonfolk regard it as a fairy tale intended to frighten the conspiratorial. The truly downtrodden look to the Thaumaturge as a symbol of rebellion against authoritarian and intolerant regimes.
- **Watchers of the Faithful**. Composed of priests, paladins, chaplains, and other clergy, the Watchers of the Faithful have dedicated themselves to preserving the old faiths in a new form. They follow the Eternal Dogma: revere the Arch Seraphs, follow their laws as handed down by the High Priest, and destroy all that is unholy and evil in their sight—through any means necessary.
- **Arcanist Inquisition**. The Arcanist Inquisition is a religious order that hunts down arcanists to prevent them from using their powers to threaten the established political and social structures they prefer. They patrol the provinces of Castinella and beyond, spreading their teachings about the dangers of magic, stirring mistrust of arcanists among the local populations.
- **Crimson Court**. The sun-starved nation of Ostoya is ruled by a cabal of vampire nobles called the Crimson Court. This loose system of nobility and feudal servants oversees every aspect of the nation, from the highest noble decrees to the lowest elements of criminality. As the ruling body of a nation, the Crimson Court has a great deal of power, but also a great deal of responsibility.
- **Monster Hunter Guilds**. Monster hunting is an ancient trade. What separated hunters from common warriors was curiosity—a genuine desire to understand one’s enemy. Knowledge rose as the monster hunter’s most lethal weapon. Recent times have seen an increase in organized hunting guilds. Killing monsters has become a corporatized trade as hunters band together and share knowledge, resources, and contracts. Some guilds have arranged themselves into a formalized structure like a mercenary company, with captains accepting quests and assigning them to teams of hunters.
- **Morbus Doctore**. The Morbus Doctore was created to combat the Weeping Pox, The members are easy to spot: boots and gloves dried with blood, strange coats and satchels hung with vials, and frightful masks meant to protect them from the disease. Established in the city of Liesech, they now operate in other major metropolitan areas of Etharis—looking for newly infected people to treat.
- **Order of Dawn**. The Order of Dawn formed when Ostoyans fled the rule of their vampire overlords and settled in the sympathetic province of Raevo. These dissidents occupied a fort outside of Castalore, where they became a militia fully committed to the destruction of the undead and the liberation of the rest of Ostoya. Members are trained and battle-ready, prepared to give their lives for their cause.
- **Prismatic Circle**. The Prismatic Circle is an order of druids who believe that only war and bloodshed can keep the world safe from the reawakening of Gormadraug, the Prismatic Wyrm. Several of the Valikan Clans follow their teachings, turning their home region of Thrull into an area of constant warfare and occasional human sacrifice.
