Name

- Type of location (everything else below is optional, just add if relevant to story)
- Scale and distance to other places
- Sensory feel: looks, sounds, smells
- Social feel: who is usually here, mood
- History/usage: how this place has been used over time
- Why it matters: dangers, opportunities, adventure relevance
- What the place does: at least one feature that acts on a scene — an access rule, a cost, something on a schedule (the tide takes the causeway; the toll-keeper logs every name)
- Tensions or secrets
- Ground: only for a place the party will explore, sneak through, chase across or fight in; never a settlement or travel. A `## Ground` section of 3–7 zones, one line each: what it is; light; terrain and footing; cover; height relative to its neighbours; each neighbour and how you cross to it (a check, if any) or why you can't; bands to the non-adjacent zones that matter. Only the thresholds the ruleset distinguishes (`kb_get rules.encounter` if not in scope; without one, close / near / far, bright / dim / dark, open / difficult / climb), stated plainly; never a sum along a route. Zones are lines, not objects.
